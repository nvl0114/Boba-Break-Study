的 (de): "My", "Your", "Their"

Use 的 to show who something belongs to.

🧠 Grammar Pattern

1. Subject + 的 + Noun

Put 的 (de) between the owner and the thing they own.

Subject
owner
+
的
de
+
Noun
thing

的 works like "'s" or "my / your / his…" in English. The owner always comes first, the thing owned comes last.

我的手機
Wǒ de shǒujī
My phone

2. Singular & Plural Owners

Add 們 (men) to make the subject plural. The pattern stays the same.

我
I
→
我們
we

Singular: 我的 · 你的 · 他的 → my · your · his
Plural: 我們的 · 你們的 · 他們的 → our · your (pl.) · their

我們的錢
Wǒmen de qián
Our money

3. Which "he / she / it"?

In writing, 他 / 她 / 它 / 牠 all sound the same (tā) but are written differently.

他 = he (male / mixed)
她 = she
它 = it (objects, things)
牠 = it (animals)

你 / 妳: 你 is for anyone, 妳 is specifically for a female "you".

她的包
Tā de bāo
Her bag
他們的錢包
Tāmen de qiánbāo
Their wallet
