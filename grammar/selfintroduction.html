Meeting Taiwanese People

自我介紹

Learn how to introduce yourself and answer common questions in Taiwan.

1. What's Your Name?

Learn how to ask someone's surname and name.

A
您好貴姓？
Nín guì xìng?
May I ask your surname? (polite)
B
我姓王。
Wǒ xìng Wáng.
My surname is Wang.
A
您姓什麼？
Nín xìng shénme?
What's your surname?
B
我姓王，叫王小明。
Wǒ xìng Wáng, jiào Wáng Xiǎomíng.
My surname is Wang. My name is Wang Xiaoming.
A
你叫什麼名字？
Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
What's your name?
B
我叫王小明。
Wǒ jiào Wáng Xiǎomíng.
My name is Wang Xiaoming.

2. Where Are You From?

Learn how to talk about your country or hometown.

A
你是哪國人？
Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?
What nationality are you?
B
我是美國人。
Wǒ shì Měiguó rén.
I'm American.
A
你是哪裡人？
Nǐ shì nǎlǐ rén?
Where are you from?
B
我來自美國。
Wǒ láizì Měiguó.
I'm from the United States.
Remember:
哪國人 asks about nationality. 哪裡人 asks where someone is from, often their hometown or place of origin.

3. How Old Are You?

These questions ask about someone's age.

A
你幾歲？
Nǐ jǐ suì?
How old are you?
A
你多大？
Nǐ duō dà?
How old are you?
B
我二十歲。
Wǒ èrshí suì.
I'm 20 years old.
Tip: Asking about age can feel personal when meeting an adult for the first time. Use it when the situation feels comfortable.

4. How Long Have You Been in Taiwan?

A
你來台灣多久了？
Nǐ lái Táiwān duōjiǔ le?
How long have you been in Taiwan?
B
我來台灣三年了。
Wǒ lái Táiwān sān nián le.
I've been in Taiwan for three years.
B
我來台灣三個月了。
Wǒ lái Táiwān sān ge yuè le.
I've been in Taiwan for three months.

5. Why Did You Come to Taiwan?

A
你來台灣做什麼？
Nǐ lái Táiwān zuò shénme?
What did you come to Taiwan for?
A
你為什麼來台灣？
Nǐ wèishénme lái Táiwān?
Why did you come to Taiwan?
B
我來台灣工作。
Wǒ lái Táiwān gōngzuò.
I came to Taiwan to work.
