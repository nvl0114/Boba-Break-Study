Meeting Taiwanese People

自我介紹 · Introducing Yourself

👋 Let's Introduce Yourself!

When you meet Taiwanese people for the first time, they may ask you some simple questions about yourself. Let's learn how to understand and answer them!

In this lesson, you will learn how to:

• Tell someone your surname and full name.
• Say where you come from.
• Talk about your age.
• Explain how long you have been in Taiwan.
• Tell people why you came to Taiwan.

🗣️ 1. Asking About Your Name

Taiwanese people may ask for your surname first. 貴姓 is a polite way to ask someone's surname.

Question A — Polite

Q
您貴姓？
Nín guìxìng?
May I ask your surname?
A
我姓王。
Wǒ xìng Wáng.
My surname is Wang.
A
我姓王，叫王小明。
Wǒ xìng Wáng, jiào Wáng Xiǎomíng.
My surname is Wang. My name is Wang Xiaoming.
Short answer: 我姓 + surname
Long answer: 我姓 + surname，叫 + full name

Question B — Common Question

Q
您姓什麼？
Nín xìng shénme?
What is your surname?
A
我姓王。
Wǒ xìng Wáng.
My surname is Wang.

Question C — Asking Your Full Name

Q
你叫什麼名字？
Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?
A
我叫王小明。
Wǒ jiào Wáng Xiǎomíng.
My name is Wang Xiaoming.
When someone asks 你叫什麼名字？, simply answer:
我叫 + your full name.

🌏 2. Where Are You From?

People may ask about your nationality or where you come from.

Q
你是哪國人？
Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?
What nationality are you?
Q
你是哪裡人？
Nǐ shì nǎlǐ rén?
Where are you from?
A
我來自印尼。
Wǒ láizì Yìnní.
I come from Indonesia.
A
我是印尼人。
Wǒ shì Yìnní rén.
I am Indonesian.
You can use two patterns:
1. 我來自 + country
我來自印尼。= I come from Indonesia.

2. 我是 + country + 人
我是印尼人。= I am Indonesian.

🎂 3. Asking About Age

Q
你幾歲？
Nǐ jǐ suì?
How old are you?
Q
你多大？
Nǐ duō dà?
How old are you?
A
我二十五歲。
Wǒ èrshíwǔ suì.
I am 25 years old.
Answer pattern:
我 + your age + 歲。
Example: 我三十歲。= I am 30 years old.
💡 Remember!

你幾歲？ is common when asking children or people you know. When meeting an adult for the first time, asking their age may feel too personal. You do not need to ask everyone this question.

⏳ 4. How Long Have You Been in Taiwan?

Q
你來台灣多久了？
Nǐ lái Táiwān duōjiǔ le?
How long have you been in Taiwan?
A
三年了。
Sān nián le.
For three years.
A
三個月了。
Sān ge yuè le.
For three months.
Remember these two patterns:
Number + 年 = number of years
三年 = three years

Number + 個月 = number of months
三個月 = three months

You can add 了 at the end to make the answer sound complete.

💼 5. Why Did You Come to Taiwan?

Q
你來台灣做什麼？
Nǐ lái Táiwān zuò shénme?
What did you come to Taiwan for?
Q
你為什麼來台灣？
Nǐ wèishénme lái Táiwān?
Why did you come to Taiwan?
A
我來台灣工作。
Wǒ lái Táiwān gōngzuò.
I came to Taiwan to work.
You can answer with:
我來台灣 + reason

A. 工作 — Work
B. 讀書 / 唸書 — Study
C. 結婚 — Get married
D. 旅行 / 旅遊 — Travel
💡 Examples

我來台灣工作。= I came to Taiwan to work.
我來台灣讀書。= I came to Taiwan to study.
我來台灣結婚。= I came to Taiwan to get married.
我來台灣旅行。= I came to Taiwan to travel.
