FREE

MOE Dictionary

A Chinese dictionary from Taiwan's Ministry of Education. You can look up Chinese characters and explore their pronunciation, meanings, and writing information.

Useful when you want to look up unfamiliar Traditional Chinese characters.

Stroke Order Learning Web

An interactive website for learning the correct stroke order of Chinese characters. Watch how each character is written step by step.

TOCFL Mock Tests

Practice your Chinese proficiency test skills

FREE PRACTICE

TOCFL Online Mock Test

An online practice platform for learners preparing for the Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language (TOCFL). Use it to familiarize yourself with computer-based test questions and the exam format.

Try practicing under timed conditions to get familiar with the test experience.
