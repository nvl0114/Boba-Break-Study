Learn Zhuyin Tone Symbols

Learn the four basic tone symbols in Zhuyin and practice listening to different tones.

🎬 What will you learn?

You already know four Zhuyin symbols: ㄌ, ㄏ, ㄕ, and ㄚ.

In this lesson, you'll learn the four basic tone symbols used with Zhuyin.
