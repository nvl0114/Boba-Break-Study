🎬 Let's Learn Zhuyin Tone Symbols!

In this lesson, you'll learn how Mandarin tones are represented in Zhuyin (Bopomofo).

Mandarin has four main tones, plus a neutral tone. In Zhuyin, tone marks help us show how a syllable should be pronounced.

Watch the video first, then try the listening game below and see if you can identify the correct tone!

📖 Zhuyin Tone Symbols

Tone marks are an important part of reading Mandarin. They help us understand how the pitch of a syllable changes.

Fourth Tone
ㄇㄚˋ

💡 Remember: In Zhuyin, the first tone is usually not marked with a visible tone symbol. The second tone uses ˊ, the third tone uses ˇ, and the fourth tone uses ˋ.

🎯 Why are tones important? Changing the tone can change the meaning of a word. That's why listening carefully to tones is an important part of learning Mandarin.
