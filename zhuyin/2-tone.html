🎬 Let's Learn Zhuyin Tone Symbols!

Now that you have learned ㄌ, ㄏ, ㄕ, and ㄚ, it's time to learn how tone symbols work in Zhuyin.

Mandarin Chinese uses different tones, and changing the tone can change the sound and meaning.

In this lesson, you'll learn the four main tone symbols and practice recognizing them by listening.

📖 The Four Tone Symbols

In Zhuyin, tones are shown using small marks. The tone mark is placed around the Zhuyin syllable.

1st Tone

No Mark
The first tone normally has no visible tone mark.

3rd Tone
ㄚˇ
ˇ
A low, dipping tone.
4th Tone
ㄚˋ
ˋ
A falling tone.
💡 Easy way to remember:
Tone 1 → no mark
Tone 2 → ˊ
Tone 3 → ˇ
Tone 4 → ˋ
