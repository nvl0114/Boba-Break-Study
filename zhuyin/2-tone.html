Learn Zhuyin Tone Symbols

Learn the four basic tone symbols and the neutral tone in Zhuyin and practice listening to different tones.

🎬 What will you learn?

You already know four Zhuyin symbols: ㄌ, ㄏ, ㄕ, and ㄚ.

In this lesson, you'll learn the four basic tone symbols plus the neutral tone used with Zhuyin.

Tone symbols show us how the pitch of a Mandarin syllable changes.
