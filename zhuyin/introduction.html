Introduction of Zhuyin

Understanding Taiwan's Phonetic System

What is Zhuyin?

In short, people in Taiwan generally don’t use Pinyin when typing Chinese. Taiwanese children also learn Zhuyin (注音) before they start learning Hanzi (Chinese characters).

You can think of Zhuyin as another system for representing Chinese pronunciation, similar to Pinyin but using different symbols.

If you’re a foreigner learning Chinese and Zhuyin looks confusing at first, don’t worry! In the next Zhuyin lessons, I’ll introduce it little by little, so it’s easier to understand and remember.
