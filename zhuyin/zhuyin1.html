Learn Zhuyin Step by Step

Zhuyin Fuhao, also known as Bopomofo, has 37 symbols in total. They consist of 21 consonant symbols and 16 vowel/final symbols.

I think learning all 37 symbols at once can be quite difficult, especially if your only experience with writing systems is the ABC alphabet.

Think about when we were kids learning the alphabet. We didn't learn everything in one day. We usually spent months learning the letters and their patterns. Even after learning them, we still reviewed them in elementary school until we became familiar with them.

Learning Zhuyin can be the same way.

So instead of trying to learn everything at once, I'll break Zhuyin down into smaller lessons and introduce the symbols little by little. Hopefully, this makes it easier to understand, practice, and remember without feeling overwhelmed.
