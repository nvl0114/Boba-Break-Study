不好意思
(bù hǎoyìsi)
Excuse me / Sorry
我不會中文
(wǒ bú huì zhōngwén)
I don't speak Chinese
聽不懂
(tīng bù dǒng)
I don't understand
看不懂
(kàn bù dǒng)
I don't understand what I read
會講英文嗎
(huì jiǎng yīngwén ma)
Do you speak English?
可以說慢一點嗎
(kěyǐ shuō màn yīdiǎn ma)
Could you speak slower?
我不知道
(wǒ bù zhīdào)
I don't know
請等一下
(qǐng děng yīxià)

我要
(wǒ yào)
I want...

(zhège)

(zuǒbiān)
