🧋 Taiwan Tourist Survival Phrases

The "Panicked Foreigner" Edition: Navigate Taiwan while silently dreaming of bubble tea.

1. Basic Self-Defense

No Hanzi & Pinyin English Meaning Sound Effect
1 不好意思
Bù hǎoyìsi		 Excuse me / Sorry
2 我不會中文
Wǒ bú huì zhōngwén		 I don't speak Chinese
3 聽不懂
Tīng bù dǒng		 I don't understand (auditory)
4 看不懂
Kàn bù dǒng		 I don't understand (reading)
5 會講英文嗎
Huì jiǎng yīngwén ma		 Do you speak English?
6 可以說慢一點嗎
Kěyǐ shuō màn yīdiǎn ma		 Can you speak slower?
7 我不知道
Wǒ bù zhīdào		 I have no idea
8 請等一下
Qǐng děng yīxià		 Please wait a moment

2. Shopping & Pointing

No Hanzi & Pinyin English Meaning Sound Effect

11 那個
Nàge		 That one
12 這裡
Zhèlǐ		 Here
13 那邊
Nàbiān		 Over there
14 哪裡
Nǎlǐ		 Where?
15 多少錢
Duōshǎo qián		 How much is this?
