Checking Out at Taiwan Convenience Stores

Checking out at a convenience store in Taiwan might feel a little overwhelming at first, especially if you're not familiar with the common phrases cashiers use. The good news is that most conversations follow a similar pattern and use the same key vocabulary.

Below are the complete conversations and expressions you'll hear when paying for your items in Taiwan.

歡迎光臨
huān yíng guāng lín
Welcome
來這邊結帳喔
lái zhè biān jié zhàng ó
Come here to check out. (Used by cashiers when opening another checkout counter and calling customers from the queue.)
你好！我要買這個。
ní hǎo! wǒ yào mǎi zhè ge
Hello! I would like to buy this.
If you don’t feel confident speaking or you’re too lazy to say it, you can just hand the item to the cashier without saying anything.
要加熱嗎？
yào jiā rè ma?
Would you like it heated?
Or
要微波嗎？
yào wēi bō ma?
Would you like it microwaved? (A cashier asks this when you buy food that can be heated, such as a lunch box, sandwich, or rice ball.)
要
yào
Yes / Want / Need.
要袋子嗎？
yào dài zi ma?
Do you need a bag? (Plastic bag)
不用
bú yòng
No need / No, thank you.
要餐具嗎？
yào cān jù ma?
Do you need utensils?
要，謝謝。
yào, xiè xiè
Yes, thank you.
現金還是刷卡？
xiàn jīn hái shì shuā kǎ?
Cash or card?
現金
xiàn jīn
Cash
刷卡
shuā kǎ
Card.
總共150塊
zǒng gòng yì bǎi wǔ shí kuài
The total is 150 ntd.
麻煩刷一下卡。
má fan shuā yí xià kǎ
Please swipe/tap your card.
*Give 200 ntd / swipe card*
找你50塊
zhǎo nǐ wǔ shí kuài
Here's your 50 ntd in change.
有會員載具嗎?
yǒu huì yuán zài jù ma?
Do you have membership carrier / Member e-invoice carrier?
沒有
méi yǒu
No, thank you. / No.
謝謝
xiè xiè
Thank you.

Keywords

That’s the end of this lesson. Hopefully, these phrases will help make your convenience store experience in Taiwan easier and more comfortable.

Don’t worry if you don’t understand everything at first. Just remember the key words, and don’t be afraid to try. Enjoy your trip to Taiwan!
