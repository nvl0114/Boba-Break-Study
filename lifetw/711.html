Checking Out at Taiwan Convenience Stores


Checking out at a convenience store in Taiwan might feel a little overwhelming at first, especially if you're not familiar with the common phrases cashiers use. The good news is that most conversations follow a similar pattern and use the same key vocabulary.

Below are some of the most common conversations and expressions you'll hear when paying for your items at a convenience store in Taiwan.

歡迎光臨
huān yíng guāng lín
Welcome
來這邊結帳喔
lái zhè biān jié zhàng ó
Come here to check out. （Used by cashiers when opening another checkout counter and calling customers from the queue.）
你好！我要買這個。
ní hǎo! wǒ yào mǎi zhè ge
Hello! I would like to buy this.
If you don’t feel confident speaking or you’re too lazy to say it, you can just hand the item to the cashier without saying anything.
要加熱嗎？
yào jiā rè ma?
Would you like it heated?
Or
要微波嗎？
yào wēi bō ma?
Would you like it microwaved? (A cashier asks this when you buy food that can be heated, such as a lunch box, sandwich, or rice ball. But in some stores, you'll need to heat it yourself using the microwave outside the cashier counter.)
要
yào
Yes / Want / Need.
要袋子嗎？
yào dài zi ma?
Do you need a bag? (Plastic bag)
不用
bú yòng
No need / No, thank you.
要餐具嗎？
yào cān jù ma?
Do you need utensils?
要，謝謝。
yào, xiè xiè
Yes, thank you. (In Taiwan, when buying food from a convenience store, you can simply say 要，謝謝 because the cashier will provide the appropriate utensils based on the food you bought.)
現金還是刷卡？
xiàn jīn hái shì shuā kǎ?
Cash or card?
