Remember the basic formula:

Drink + Size + Sweetness Level + Ice Level + Topping

By following this simple pattern, you can easily order your favorite drink at tea shops in Taiwan. Don’t be afraid to practice using Mandarin in real situations. Every time you order a drink, you are taking one more step toward improving your Mandarin skills.
