Ordering Drink in Taiwan: A Practical Guide


Ordering drinks in Taiwan is a bit different from many other countries. When you buy a drink, you usually don’t just choose the drink name, you also need to customize your order.

The staff will often ask about your preferred sugar level, ice level, drink size, and toppings. Knowing these options will make your ordering experience easier and help you communicate like a local.

In the following section, I will provide some common drink shop conversations in Taiwan to help you practice real-life situations.

你好！歡迎光臨！
Nǐ hǎo! Huānyíng guānglín!
Hello! Welcome!
想要點什麼？
Xiǎng yào diǎn shénme?
What would you like to order?
我想要一杯(綠茶)
Wǒ xiǎng yào yì (bēi lǜchá.)
I would like a cup of (green tea)
你要大杯還是中杯？
Nǐ yào dà bēi háishì zhōng bēi?
Would you like a large or medium size?
大杯。
Dà bēi.
Large size.
甜度、冰塊呢？
Tiándù, bīngkuài ne?
What sweetness level and ice level would you like?
半糖，少冰。
Bàn táng, shǎo bīng.
Half sugar, less ice.
要加配料嗎？
Yào jiā (pèiliào) ma?
Would you like to add any (toppings?)
好的，我要加(愛玉)
Hǎo de, wǒ yào jiā (àiyù.)
Okay, I would like to add (aiyu jelly)
