Lesson 1

Basic Mandarin Conversation

你好

Nǐ hǎo - Hello

現金
xiàn jīn
Cash
刷卡
shuā kǎ
Card.
總共150塊
zǒng gòng yì bǎi wǔ shí kuài
The total is 150 ntd.
麻煩刷一下卡。
má fan shuā yí xià kǎ
Please swipe/tap your card.
*Give 200 ntd / swipe card*
找你50塊
zhǎo nǐ wǔ shí kuài
Here's your 50 ntd in change. (If you pay with cash)
有會員嗎？
yǒu huì yuán ma?
Are you a member?
有載具嗎？
yǒu zài jù ma?
Do you have an e-invoice carrier?
有會員載具嗎?
yǒu huì yuán zài jù ma?
Do you have membership carrier / Member e-invoice carrier? (In Taiwan, this refers to a membership account and digital carrier used to store e-invoices and collect rewards points...)
沒有
méi yǒu
No, thank you. / No.
謝謝
xiè xiè
Thank you.
