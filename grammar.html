Grammar Guide

The course content generally follows the concepts and learning structure commonly found in Traditional Chinese textbooks. However, the order of the grammar topics may seem a little random.

That’s because the course focuses more on building vocabulary first, so some grammar points are introduced earlier or later depending on the vocabulary being learned. In other words, the lessons may jump between different grammar topics rather than following a strict grammar progression.

This page is dedicated specifically to grammar. Here, you can find the grammar explanations organized in a clearer and more systematic way, making it easier to review and understand how each structure works.

Basic Sentence Patterns

Learn the essential Mandarin sentence patterns for making positive, negative, and yes/no questions using simple everyday examples.

Location & Ongoing Actions

Learn how to use 在 (zài) to talk about where someone is and what someone is doing right now.

是 & 很: “am / is / are”

Learn how 是 and 很 work in Mandarin, and how to use them with nouns and adjectives to describe people and things.
