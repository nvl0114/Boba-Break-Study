1. Saying Hello

A
您好
Nín hǎo
Hello. (Polite)
B
你好
Nǐ hǎo
Hello.

您好 (Nín hǎo)
A polite way to say hello. Use it when speaking to someone you don't know well or someone older.

你好 (Nǐ hǎo)
A common and friendly way to say hello.

2. Saying Thank You

A
謝謝
Xiè xie
Thank you.
B
不客氣
Bú kè qi
You're welcome.

謝謝 (Xiè xie)
Say this when you want to thank someone.

不客氣 (Bú kè qi)
Use this expression to reply to someone who says thank you. It means "You're welcome."

3. Saying Goodbye

A
再見
Zài jiàn
Goodbye.
B
掰掰
Bāi bāi
Bye-bye.

再見 (Zài jiàn)
A common way to say goodbye. It is suitable in many situations.

掰掰 (Bāi bāi)
A casual way to say goodbye. It is very common in Taiwan.

4. Saying Sorry

A
對不起
Duì bù qǐ
I'm sorry.
A
不好意思
Bù hǎo yì si
Sorry. / Excuse me.
A
抱歉
Bào qiàn
I apologize. / Sorry.
B
沒關係
Méi guān xì
It's okay. / No problem.
B
沒問題
Méi wèn tí
No problem.
B
沒事
Méi shì
It's nothing. / Don't worry about it.

對不起 (Duì bù qǐ)
The most basic way to say "I'm sorry." Use it when you make a mistake or hurt someone.

不好意思 (Bù hǎo yì si)
A very common and light way to say "Sorry." You can also use it to say "Excuse me," for example when you want to get someone's attention or pass through a crowd.

抱歉 (Bào qiàn)
A polite and slightly formal way to say "I apologize." It is often used at work, in messages, or with people you don't know well.

沒關係 (Méi guān xì)
Use this to answer an apology. It means "It's okay."

沒問題 (Méi wèn tí)
It means "No problem." Use it to say that you are not upset, or that you agree to help.

沒事 (Méi shì)
It means "It's nothing." Use it to tell the other person not to worry. It is short and casual.

5. Meeting Someone

A
很高興認識你
Hěn gāo xìng rèn shí nǐ
Nice to meet you.
B
我也很高興認識你
Wǒ yě hěn gāo xìng rèn shí nǐ
Nice to meet you, too.

很高興認識你 (Hěn gāo xìng rèn shí nǐ)
Say this when you meet someone for the first time. It means "Nice to meet you."

我也 (Wǒ yě)
Means "Me too" or "I also..."
