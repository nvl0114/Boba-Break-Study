Sentence Pattern
Subject → Verb → Object
我叫麗麗。
Wǒ → jiào → Lìlì
I → am called → Lili
你叫什麼名字？
Nǐ → jiào → shénme míngzi
You → are called → what name
Mandarin commonly uses a Subject + Verb + Object word order, similar to English.
