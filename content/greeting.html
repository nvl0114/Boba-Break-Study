Lesson 1 — Greetings

麗麗 (Lili):
你好！ (Nǐ hǎo!) Hello!
小明 (Xiaoming):
妳好！妳叫什麼名字？ (Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?) Hello! What’s your name?
麗麗 (Lili):
我叫麗麗。你呢？ (Wǒ jiào Lìlì. Nǐ ne?) My name is Lili. How about you?
小明 (Xiaoming):
我叫小明。很高興認識妳。 (Wǒ jiào Xiǎomíng. Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.) My name is Xiaoming. Nice to meet you.
麗麗 (Lili):
我也很高興認識你。 (Wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.) Nice to meet you too.
小明 (Xiaoming):
謝謝！ (Xièxie!) Thank you!
麗麗 (Lili):
不客氣！ (Bú kèqi!) You're welcome!

Sentence Pattern
Subject → Verb → Object
我叫麗麗。
Wǒ → jiào → Lìlì
I → am called → Lili
你叫什麼名字？
Nǐ → jiào → shénme míngzi
You → are called → what name
Mandarin commonly uses a Subject + Verb + Object word order, similar to English.

Grammar & Notes

1. 叫 (jiào) — To Be Called

Formula: Subject + 叫 + Name

Wǒ jiào Lìlì. (My name is Lili)

2. 什麼 (shénme) — What

Placed where unknown info appears.

Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi? (What's your name?)

3. 呢 (ne) — How About You?

Used at the end of questions to bounce back.

Wǒ jiào Lìlì. Nǐ ne? (My name is Lili. You?)

4. 也 (yě) — Also / Too

Placed before the verb/adjective.

Wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng. (I'm also glad)

5. 很 (hěn) + Adjective

Often used before adjectives in simple sentences.

Hěn gāoxìng (Very glad / Nice to meet you)

6. 認識 (rènshi) — To Know

To know or become acquainted with someone.

Rènshi nǐ (To meet/know you)

⭐ Key Patterns to Remember

📌 我叫 + Name → My name is... 📌 你叫什麼名字？ → What’s your name? 📌 你呢？ → How about you? 📌 我也 + Verb/Adjective → I also... 📌 很高興認識你。 → Nice to meet you.
