Who Is My Friend?

Learn how to ask and answer questions about people, places, time, reasons, methods, and descriptions.

📖 Reading: My Friend Xiaomei

Read about Xiaomei and practice common Mandarin question words. Pinyin is shown only above the Lesson 17 vocabulary.

Reading Text

Pinyin appears only above target vocabulary from Lesson 17.

我有一個朋友，她叫小美。 她很漂亮，也很 qīnqiè 親切 。 她是老師，在一 suǒ 所 學校工作。 她每天早上七點去上班。 她星期一到星期六都工作，星期日 xiūxi 休息 。 她每天 zuò 坐 gōngchē 公車 去學校。 她很喜歡她的工作，因為她喜歡 jiāo 教 學生。 她今年二十五歲。 她的生日是二零二一年一月二十三日。 她有很多學生，每天都很忙。 星期日她不上班，她 chángcháng 常常 在家看電視、聽音樂。
English Meaning:
I have a friend. Her name is Xiaomei. She is very beautiful and also very kind. She is a teacher and works at a school. She goes to work at seven o'clock every morning. She works from Monday to Saturday and rests on Sunday. She takes the bus to school every day. She really likes her job because she likes teaching students. She is twenty-five years old this year. Her birthday is January 23, 2021. She has many students and is very busy every day. On Sunday, she does not work. She often stays at home, watches TV, and listens to music.

🧠 Grammar Patterns

Learn how Mandarin question words work. The explanations and examples are written in English.

1. 誰 (shéi) — Who

誰 + 是 + Subject / Person?

誰 asks about a person. It means "who."

The question-word position is usually the same position where the answer would appear.

誰是我的朋友？
Shéi shì wǒ de péngyǒu?
Who is my friend?

小美是我的朋友。
Xiǎoměi shì wǒ de péngyǒu.
Xiaomei is my friend.

2. 什麼 (shénme) — What

Subject + Verb + 什麼？

什麼 asks about a thing, information, activity, or identity. It means "what."

Place 什麼 where the unknown information would normally appear.

你吃什麼？
Nǐ chī shénme?
What do you eat?

3. 為什麼 (wèishéme) — Why

Subject + 為什麼 + Verb？

為什麼 asks for a reason. It means "why."

A common structure is: Subject + 為什麼 + Verb?

你為什麼學中文？
Nǐ wèishéme xué Zhōngwén?
Why do you study Chinese?

4. 什麼時候 (shénme shíhou) — When

Subject + 什麼時候 + Verb？

什麼時候 asks about a time. It means "when."

It can be used to ask about birthdays, schedules, events, and activities.

你的生日是什麼時候？
Nǐ de shēngrì shì shénme shíhou?
When is your birthday?

5. 哪裡 (nǎlǐ) — Where

Subject + 在 + 哪裡？

哪裡 asks about a place. It means "where."

A common structure is 在 + 哪裡.

你在哪裡工作？
Nǐ zài nǎlǐ gōngzuò?
Where do you work?

6. 怎麼 (zěnme) — How

Subject + 怎麼 + Verb？

怎麼 asks about the way or method of doing something. It means "how."

你怎麼去學校？
Nǐ zěnme qù xuéxiào?
How do you go to school?

7. 怎麼樣 (zěnmeyàng) — How / What is ... like?

Subject + 怎麼樣？

怎麼樣 asks about a condition, quality, experience, or description.

It can be translated as "how is it?" or "what is it like?"

小美長得怎麼樣？
Xiǎoměi zhǎng de zěnmeyàng?
What does Xiaomei look like?

8. 怎麼辦 (zěnmebàn) — What should I do?

Situation + 怎麼辦？

怎麼辦 asks what someone should do in a situation.

It can mean "What should I do?" or "What should we do?" depending on context.

今天下雨，我們怎麼辦？
Jīntiān xiàyǔ, wǒmen zěnmebàn?
It is raining today. What should we do?

9. 幾 (jǐ) + Measure Word — How Many?

幾 + Measure Word + Noun？

幾 asks for a small or specific number. It is usually followed by a measure word.

For example, 幾點 asks "what time?"

你幾點上班？
Nǐ jǐ diǎn shàngbān?
What time do you go to work?

10. 多 + Adjective — How ...?

多 + Adjective？

多 can be combined with an adjective to ask about degree or measurement.

多大 asks "how old?" or "how big?" depending on context.

小美多大？
Xiǎoměi duō dà?
How old is Xiaomei?

11. 哪 (nǎ) — Which

哪 + Measure Word + Noun？

哪 asks someone to choose or identify one item from a group. It means "which."

It is normally followed by a measure word and noun.

你是哪個老師的學生？
Nǐ shì nǎ ge lǎoshī de xuéshēng?
Which teacher's student are you?
