LESSON 17 • QUESTION WORDS

5W + 1H — Let's Find the Information

Learn how to ask and answer basic information questions in Mandarin using 5W + 1H and useful question patterns.

📖 Reading: My Friend

Read the short story and look for information about who, what, where, when, why, and how. Pinyin is shown only for Lesson 17 target vocabulary.

Reading Text

Pinyin appears only above Lesson 17 target vocabulary.

我有一個朋友。 她叫小美。 她是老師。 她住在台北。 她每天早上七點去學校。 她 zuò 坐 gōngchē 公車 去公司。 她喜歡喝咖啡。 因為她喜歡咖啡， 所以她常常去 kāfēidiàn 咖啡店 。

她有 jǐ 幾 個朋友？ 她有一個朋友。 咖啡店裡有很多人， 她常常想知道 nǎ yí ge 哪一個 人是她的朋友。

她 duō 多 常去咖啡店？ 因為她喜歡喝咖啡。 她每個星期去很多次。

有一天， 她不知道 zěnme 怎麼 找朋友， 所以她問： 「我現在 zěnmebàn 怎麼辦 ？」 她的朋友說： 「妳可以問： 『 zěnmeyàng 怎麼樣 找到她？』」
English Meaning:

I have a friend. Her name is Xiaomei. She is a teacher. She lives in Taipei. She goes to school at seven every morning. She takes the bus to work. She likes drinking coffee. Because she likes coffee, she often goes to a coffee shop.

How many friends does she have? She has one friend. There are many people in the coffee shop, and she often wants to know which person is her friend.

Why does she go to the coffee shop so often? Because she likes drinking coffee. She goes there many times every week.

One day, she did not know how to find her friend, so she asked: "What should I do now?" Her friend said: "How about asking how to find her?"

🔎 Let's Find the Information

The reading contains many useful pieces of information. Let's use Mandarin question words to find them.

誰？
shuí / shéi
Who?
誰叫小美？
Shéi jiào Xiǎoměi?
Who is named Xiaomei?
→ 她。
→ She.

Example:
誰是老師？
Shéi shì lǎoshī?
Who is the teacher?
→ 小美是老師。

什麼？
shénme
What?
她喜歡喝什麼？
Tā xǐhuān hē shénme?
What does she like to drink?
→ 咖啡。
→ Coffee.

Example:
你喜歡吃什麼？
Nǐ xǐhuān chī shénme?
What do you like to eat?
→ 我喜歡吃麵包。

哪裡？
nǎlǐ
Where?
她住在哪裡？
Tā zhù zài nǎlǐ?
Where does she live?
→ 台北。
→ Taipei.

Example:
你住在哪裡？
Nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?
Where do you live?
→ 我住在台北。

什麼時候？
shénme shíhòu
When?
她什麼時候去學校？
Tā shénme shíhòu qù xuéxiào?
When does she go to school?
→ 每天早上七點。
→ Seven every morning.

Example:
你什麼時候去學校？
Nǐ shénme shíhòu qù xuéxiào?
When do you go to school?
→ 我早上八點去學校。

為什麼？
wèishénme
Why?
為什麼她常常去咖啡店？
Wèishénme tā chángcháng qù kāfēidiàn?
Why does she often go to the coffee shop?
→ 因為她喜歡咖啡。
→ Because she likes coffee.

Example:
為什麼你喝咖啡？
Wèishénme nǐ hē kāfēi?
Why do you drink coffee?
→ 因為我喜歡咖啡。

怎麼？
zěnme
How?
她怎麼去公司？
Tā zěnme qù gōngsī?
How does she go to work?
→ 她坐公車去公司。
→ She takes the bus to work.

Example:
你怎麼去學校？
Nǐ zěnme qù xuéxiào?
How do you go to school?
→ 我坐公車去學校。

幾？
jǐ
How many?
她有幾個朋友？
Tā yǒu jǐ ge péngyǒu?
How many friends does she have?
→ 一個。
→ One.

Pattern:
幾 + Measure Word + Noun

哪一個？
nǎ yí ge
Which one?
哪一個人是她的朋友？
Nǎ yí ge rén shì tā de péngyǒu?
Which person is her friend?
→ 那一個人。
→ That person.

Pattern:
哪一個 + Noun?

多？
duō
How much / how ...?
你多常去咖啡店？
Nǐ duō cháng qù kāfēidiàn?
How often do you go to the coffee shop?

Note:
多 can be used in several question patterns. The meaning depends on the word that follows it.

怎麼辦？
zěnmebàn
What should I do?
我現在怎麼辦？
Wǒ xiànzài zěnmebàn?
What should I do now?

Use 怎麼辦 when asking for a solution to a problem or situation.

怎麼樣？
zěnmeyàng
How is it? / How about...?
這個怎麼樣？
Zhège zěnmeyàng?
How is this? / How about this?

Use 怎麼樣 to ask for an opinion, condition, or suggestion.

🧠 Grammar Patterns

1. 誰？ — Who?

誰 + Verb + ...？

誰 asks about a person. It means "who?".

The question word can appear in the same position where the answer would normally appear.

誰是老師？
Shéi shì lǎoshī?
Who is the teacher?
小美是老師。
Xiǎoměi shì lǎoshī.
Xiaomei is the teacher.

2. 什麼？ — What?

Verb + 什麼？

什麼 means "what?".

It is often placed directly where the unknown object would normally appear.

你喜歡喝什麼？
Nǐ xǐhuān hē shénme?
What do you like to drink?
我喜歡喝咖啡。
Wǒ xǐhuān hē kāfēi.
I like to drink coffee.

3. 哪裡？ — Where?

Verb + 在 + 哪裡？

哪裡 asks about a location. It means "where?".

你住在哪裡？
Nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?
Where do you live?
我住在台北。
Wǒ zhù zài Táiběi.
I live in Taipei.

4. 什麼時候？ — When?

Subject + 什麼時候 + Verb？

什麼時候 means "when?".

It asks about the time when an action happens.

你什麼時候去學校？
Nǐ shénme shíhòu qù xuéxiào?
When do you go to school?
我早上八點去學校。
Wǒ zǎoshang bā diǎn qù xuéxiào.
I go to school at eight in the morning.

5. 為什麼？ — Why?

為什麼 + Sentence？

為什麼 asks for a reason. It means "why?".

A common answer uses 因為 meaning "because".

為什麼你喝咖啡？
Wèishénme nǐ hē kāfēi?
Why do you drink coffee?
因為我喜歡咖啡。
Yīnwèi wǒ xǐhuān kāfēi.
Because I like coffee.

6. 怎麼？ — How?

Subject + 怎麼 + Verb？

怎麼 asks how something is done or by what method.

It is especially useful for transportation, methods, and ways of doing something.

你怎麼去學校？
Nǐ zěnme qù xuéxiào?
How do you go to school?
我坐公車去學校。
Wǒ zuò gōngchē qù xuéxiào.
I take the bus to school.

7. 幾？ — How Many?

幾 + Measure Word + Noun？

幾 is used to ask about a small, usually countable number.

It is normally followed by a measure word.

你有幾個朋友？
Nǐ yǒu jǐ ge péngyǒu?
How many friends do you have?
我有三個朋友。
Wǒ yǒu sān ge péngyǒu.
I have three friends.

8. 哪一個？ — Which One?

哪一個 + Noun？

哪一個 means "which one?".

Use it when there are several choices and you want to identify one of them.

哪一個人是你的朋友？
Nǎ yí ge rén shì nǐ de péngyǒu?
Which person is your friend?

9. 多 — How Much / How ...?

多 + Adjective / Frequency Expression

多 can be used in several question structures.

Its exact English meaning depends on the expression that follows it.

For example, 多大 asks "how old / how big", while 多久 asks "how long".

你多大？
Nǐ duō dà?
How old are you?

你多久去一次咖啡店？
Nǐ duō jiǔ qù yí cì kāfēidiàn?
How often do you go to the coffee shop?

10. 怎麼辦？ — What Should I Do?

Situation + 怎麼辦？

怎麼辦 is used when you need a solution or do not know what to do.

It can be translated as "What should I do?" or "What can I do?".

我找不到朋友，怎麼辦？
Wǒ zhǎo bú dào péngyǒu, zěnmebàn?
I can't find my friend. What should I do?

11. 怎麼樣？ — How Is It? / How About...?

Noun + 怎麼樣？

怎麼樣 is commonly used to ask for an opinion or suggestion.

It can mean "How is it?", "What do you think?", or "How about...?".

這個怎麼樣？
Zhège zěnmeyàng?
How is this? / What do you think of this?

明天去咖啡店，怎麼樣？
Míngtiān qù kāfēidiàn, zěnmeyàng?
How about going to the coffee shop tomorrow?
