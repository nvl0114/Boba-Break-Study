Lesson 17

5W + 1H & Question Words

Learn how to ask for information using 誰、什麼、哪裡、什麼時候、 為什麼、怎麼 and more.

① Reading — 我的朋友

Read the passage and find important information.

我有一個朋友。
她叫小美。
她是老師。
她住在台北。
她每天早上七點去學校。
她坐公車去公司。 zuò gōngchē
她喜歡喝咖啡。 kāfēi
因為她喜歡咖啡，所以她常常去咖啡店。 yīnwèi kāfēi, suǒyǐ chángcháng qù kāfēidiàn
她有幾個朋友？
她有多少個朋友？
哪一個朋友是老師？

② Let's Find the Information 🔎

Use question words to find information in the reading.

👤 誰？— Who?

誰叫小美？

Who is named Xiaomei?

→ 她。

誰？= Who?

Example:

誰是老師？

→ 小美是老師。
📦 什麼？— What?

她喜歡喝什麼？

What does she like to drink?

→ 咖啡。

什麼？= What?

Example:

你喜歡吃什麼？

→ 我喜歡吃麵包。
📍 哪裡？— Where?

她住在哪裡？

Where does she live?

→ 台北。

哪裡？= Where?

Example:

你住在哪裡？

→ 我住在台北。
🕐 什麼時候？— When?

她什麼時候去學校？

When does she go to school?

→ 每天早上七點。

什麼時候？= When?

Example:

你什麼時候去學校？

→ 我早上八點去學校。
❓ 為什麼？— Why?

為什麼她常常去咖啡店？

Why does she often go to the coffee shop?

→ 因為她喜歡咖啡。

為什麼？= Why?

Example:

為什麼你喝咖啡？

→ 因為我喜歡咖啡。
🚌 怎麼？— How?

她怎麼去公司？

How does she go to the company?

→ 她坐公車去公司。

怎麼？= How? / In what way?

Example:

你怎麼去學校？

→ 我坐公車去學校。

③ 5W + 1H 🧠

These question words help us find information.

English Mandarin Meaning
Who 誰？ Siapa?
What 什麼？ Apa?
Where 哪裡？ Di mana?
When 什麼時候？ Kapan?
Why 為什麼？ Kenapa?
How 怎麼？ Bagaimana?

④ More Question Patterns

Learn four useful question patterns.

🆘 怎麼辦？— What should I do?

怎麼辦？

Use 怎麼辦 when asking what to do about a problem or situation.

下雨了，怎麼辦？
→ It's raining. What should we do?
我沒有錢，怎麼辦？
→ I don't have money. What should I do?

💭 怎麼樣？— How is it? / What do you think?

……怎麼樣？

Use 怎麼樣 to ask about an opinion, condition, or experience.

這家咖啡店怎麼樣？
→ How is this coffee shop?
今天怎麼樣？
→ How is today? / How are things today?

🔢 幾？— How many?

幾 + Measure Word + Noun

Use 幾 when asking about a small or unknown number.

你有幾個朋友？
→ How many friends do you have?
現在幾點？
→ What time is it?

📏 多 + Adjective — How ...?

多 + Adjective

Use 多 before an adjective to ask about degree or extent.

台北多大？
→ How big is Taipei?
你多高？
→ How tall are you?
這家咖啡店多遠？
→ How far is this coffee shop?

👉 哪一個？— Which one?

哪一個 + Noun？

Use 哪一個 when choosing one item from several options.

哪一個是小美？
→ Which one is Xiaomei?
你喜歡哪一個？
→ Which one do you like?

🔢 多少？— How much / How many?

多少 + Noun？

多少 is commonly used when asking about an amount or number.

你有多少個朋友？
→ How many friends do you have?
這杯咖啡多少錢？
→ How much is this coffee?
