Dates, Days & When

Learn how to talk about dates, days of the week, birthdays, and when something happens in Mandarin.

💬 Conversation: Talking About Dates

David and Luna are talking about today's date, the day of the week, Luna's birthday, and her birthday plans.

David & Luna
Online
David
欸，你知道今天幾月幾號嗎？
Éi, nǐ zhīdào jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào ma?
Hey, do you know what today's date is?
Luna
知道啊，今天二零二六年八月二十四號。
Zhīdào a, jīntiān èr líng èr liù nián bā yuè èr shí sì hào.
Yes. Today is August 24, 2026.
David
今天星期幾？
Jīntiān xīngqí jǐ?
What day is it today?
Luna
星期一。
Xīngqí yī.
Monday.
David
對了，你生日是幾月幾號？
Duì le, nǐ shēngrì shì jǐ yuè jǐ hào?
By the way, what month and date is your birthday?
Luna
十二月十五號。
Shí'èr yuè shíwǔ hào.
December 15.
David
喔～所以你的生日是什麼時候？
Ō~ suǒyǐ nǐ de shēngrì shì shénme shíhòu?
Oh~ so when is your birthday?
Luna
十二月十五號啊。😂
Shí'èr yuè shíwǔ hào a.
December 15. 😂
David
哈哈，我知道啦！我是想問你今年生日星期幾。
Hāhā, wǒ zhīdào la! Wǒ shì xiǎng wèn nǐ jīnnián shēngrì xīngqí jǐ.
Haha, I know! I wanted to ask what day of the week your birthday falls on this year.
Luna
星期二。
Xīngqí èr.
Tuesday.
David
那你生日要做什麼？
Nà nǐ shēngrì yào zuò shénme?
So what are you going to do on your birthday?
Luna
還不知道, 可能跟朋友一起吃飯吧。
Hái bù zhīdào, kěnéng gēn péngyǒu yìqǐ chīfàn ba.
I don't know yet. Maybe I'll have a meal with friends.

📅 Mandarin Date Format

Mandarin dates are usually said from the largest unit to the smallest unit.

Year → Month → Day
↓
2026年8月24號
二零二六年八月二十四號
August 24, 2026

年 (nián) means year. Years are normally read digit by digit.

2026年 → 二零二六年

月 (yuè) means month. The pattern is simply Number + 月.

日 (rì) and 號 (hào) both refer to the date/day of the month. In everyday conversation in Taiwan, 號 is very common.

🧠 Grammar Notes & Quick Patterns

1. 年 — Year

Number + 年

Explanation: Mandarin normally reads the digits of a year one by one.

二零二六年
èr líng èr liù nián
2026

2. 月 — Month

Number + 月

Explanation: Months are formed by putting the number before 月.

八月
bā yuè
August

3. 日 / 號 — Day of the Month

Number + 日 / 號

Explanation: 日 and 號 both refer to the date/day of the month. In everyday Taiwan Mandarin, 號 is especially common.

二十四號
èr shí sì hào
the 24th

4. Full Date

Year + Month + Day

Explanation: Mandarin dates go from the largest unit to the smallest: year → month → day.

二零二六年八月二十四號
èr líng èr liù nián bā yuè èr shí sì hào
August 24, 2026

5. 星期 / 禮拜 — Days of the Week & Weekend

星期 / 禮拜 + Number / 週末 / 週日

星期 and 禮拜 can both be used for days of the week.

星期一 = Monday
星期二 = Tuesday
星期五 = Friday
星期六 = Saturday
星期日 / 星期天 = Sunday
週末 (zhōumò) = Weekend
週日 (zhōurì) = Sunday

In everyday conversation in Taiwan, 禮拜 is also very common.

今天星期一。
Jīntiān xīngqí yī.
Today is Monday.

6. 幾 — Which Number?

星期 + 幾
幾月幾號

Explanation: 幾 is used when asking for a specific number or numbered item.

星期幾？ = What day of the week?
幾月幾號？ = What month and date?

今天星期幾？
Jīntiān xīngqí jǐ?
What day is it today?

7. 什麼時候 — When?

S + 什麼時候 + V？

Explanation: 什麼時候 means "when?" It asks about the time or occasion when something happens.

你什麼時候回家？
When are you going home?

你的生日是什麼時候？
When is your birthday?

你什麼時候考試？
Nǐ shénme shíhòu kǎoshì?
When is your exam?

8. 幾 vs. 什麼時候

幾 = Which number?
什麼時候 = When?

幾 asks for a specific number.

今天星期幾？
What day is it?

你的生日是幾月幾號？
What month and date is your birthday?

什麼時候 asks when something happens.

你什麼時候回台灣？
When are you going back to Taiwan?
