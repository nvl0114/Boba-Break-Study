LESSON 16 • TIME & TRAVEL

Past Adventures

Learn how to talk about past time using 了 and 已經……了.

📖 Reading: My Summer Trips

This lesson uses a travel story to practice talking about past events. Pinyin is shown only for this lesson vocabulary.

Reading Text

Pinyin appears only above target vocabulary from Lesson 16.

qùnián 去年 shǔjià 暑假 ， 我和朋友一起 qù 去 Zhōngguó 中國 玩。 我們 qù 去 了 Běijīng 北京 和 Shànghǎi 上海 ， 也吃了很多 hǎochī 好吃 的東西。 Běijīng 北京 有很多 piàoliang 漂亮 的 dìfāng 地方 ， 我們 měitiān 每天 都出去玩， 所以每天晚上都很累，但是很 kāixīn 開心 。 Huí 回 台灣 yǐhòu 以後 ， 我 yǐjīng 已經 開始上課了。

jīnnián 今年 shǔjià 暑假 ， 我 yòu 又 和朋友一起 qù 去 Hánguó 韓國 玩。 我們去了很多地方，也買了很多東西。 我 zuì 最 喜歡韓國的 cāntīng 餐廳 ， nàlǐ 那裡 的 shíwù 食物 很好吃。 現在我 yǐjīng 已經 回到台灣了， 正在 zhǔnbèi 準備 kèchéng 課程 。 Suīrán 雖然 這個星期 yào 要 練習很多，但是我還是很 xiǎngniàn 想念 中國和韓國。

míngnián 明年 shǔjià 暑假 ， 我想和朋友一起 qù 去 Tàiguó 泰國 玩。 我 tīngshuō 聽說 泰國有很多漂亮的地方， 也有很多好吃的東西， 所以我很 qīdài 期待 這 cì 次 lǚxíng 旅行 。
English Meaning:
Last summer, my friends and I went to China together. We went to Beijing and Shanghai and ate a lot of delicious food. Beijing has many beautiful places, so we went out every day. We were very tired every night, but we were very happy. After returning to Taiwan, I had already started attending classes.

This summer, my friends and I went to Korea again. We visited many places and bought many things. I liked Korean restaurants the most. The food there was very delicious. Now I have already returned to Taiwan and am preparing for next week's course. Although there is a lot to practice this week, I still miss China and Korea very much.

Next summer, I want to travel to Thailand with my friends. I heard that Thailand has many beautiful places and lots of delicious food, so I am really looking forward to this trip.

🧠 Grammar Patterns

1. 去年 — 今年 — 明年

去年 = last year
今年 = this year
明年 = next year

These three words tell us when something happens in relation to the current year.

去年 means "last year."
今年 means "this year."
明年 means "next year."

Think of the calendar: last year is behind us, this year is where we are now, and next year is ahead of us.

去年我去中國。
Last year I went to China.

今年我去韓國。
This year I went to Korea.

明年我去泰國。
Next year I will go to Thailand.

2. 上個星期 — 下個星期

上個星期 = last week
下個星期 = next week

上 means "up / above."
下 means " down/following."
When used with time, it can refer to a time period before the current one.

上個星期 = last week.
下個星期 = next week.

A simple way to remember this is to imagine a calendar. The previous week is above the current week, while the following week is below it.

3. 了 — Completed Action

Subject + Verb + 了

了 is commonly used after a verb to show that an action has been completed or that a situation has changed.

It is not simply an English "past tense" marker. The important idea is that the action or situation is viewed as completed.

我去了中國。
I went to China.

我們吃了很多東西。
We ate a lot of things.

4. 已經……了 — Already

已經 + Verb + 了

已經 means "already." It tells the listener that something has happened before now or has reached a certain state.

A very common structure is: 已經 + Verb + 了.

The combination emphasizes that the action or change has already happened.

我已經回台灣了。
I have already returned to Taiwan.

我已經開始上課了。
I have already started attending classes.

5. Time Expressions + 了

Time + Subject + Verb

When a sentence already contains a clear time expression such as 去年, 今年, or 明年, Mandarin does not need a separate past or future tense marker.

The time expression already tells us when the action happens.

For example: 去年我去中國。 means "Last year I went to China."

However, 了 can still be used when the speaker wants to emphasize that an action has been completed or that the situation has changed.

去年我去中國。
Last year I went to China.

去年我去了中國。
Last year I went to China. (with completion emphasized)

6. Time + Verb: No English-Style Tense Needed

Time Expression + Subject + Verb

Mandarin does not change the verb according to past, present, or future tense.

Instead, words such as 去年, 今年, 明年, 上個星期, and 下個星期 help establish the time.

去年我去北京。
Last year I went to Beijing.

今年我去韓國。
This year I went to Korea.

明年我去泰國。
Next year I will go to Thailand.
