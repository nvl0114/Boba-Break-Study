LESSON 21 • FEELINGS & INTENSITY

Expressing Feelings & Strong Emotions

Learn how to describe strong feelings and different levels of intensity in Mandarin.

📖 Reading Text

Read the story below. Pinyin is shown only for the target vocabulary introduced in this lesson.

阿豪喜歡小美

Ā Háo 阿豪 很喜歡一個女生。她叫 Xiǎoměi 小美 ，是阿豪的朋友。

小美很漂亮，也 fēicháng 非常 可愛。阿豪 juéde 覺得 小美 xiào 笑 的 shíhou 時候 真好看。

今天，阿豪跟小美 zài yīqǐ 在一起 去看電影。他們也一起喝咖啡。阿豪非常開心。

可是，阿豪看到小美的時候，常常 tài 太 jǐnzhāng 緊張 了。他想 gàosù 告訴 小美：「我喜歡你。」

今天跟小美在一起， shíjiān 時間 guò 過 得太快了！阿豪覺得今天真開心。

English Meaning:

Ahao really likes a girl. Her name is Xiaomei, and she is Ahao's friend. Xiaomei is very beautiful and also extremely cute. Ahao thinks that Xiaomei looks really beautiful when she smiles. Today, Ahao and Xiaomei went to watch a movie together. They also drank coffee together. Ahao was extremely happy. However, whenever Ahao sees Xiaomei, he is often too nervous. He wants to tell Xiaomei, "I like you." Today, the time passed too quickly while he was with Xiaomei! Ahao thinks today was really wonderful.

🧠 Grammar Patterns: Expressing Intensity

1. 真 (zhēn) — really / truly

真 + Adjective

真 is often used to express a strong and natural personal feeling. It can sound emotional, spontaneous, and conversational.

It is commonly used when the speaker wants to say, "really!" or "truly!"

小美真漂亮。
Xiǎoměi zhēn piàoliang.
Xiaomei is really beautiful.

2. 非常 (fēicháng) — very / extremely

非常 + Adjective / Verb

非常 expresses a high degree of intensity. It is generally stronger and more formal than 很.

Use it when you want to emphasize that something is very or extremely strong.

阿豪非常開心。
Ā Háo fēicháng kāixīn.
Ahao is extremely happy.

3. 太……了 (tài...le) — too / so ...!

太 + Adjective / Verb + 了

太……了 expresses an excessive or very strong degree. Depending on the situation, it can mean "too..." or "so...!"

The 了 at the end helps give the sentence a strong, expressive feeling.

時間過得太快了！
Shíjiān guò de tài kuài le!
Time passed too quickly!

4. What Is the Difference?

真 — expresses a natural and personal feeling.

Example: 你真可愛！
"You are really cute!"

非常 — emphasizes a very high degree and sounds more neutral or formal.

Example: 我非常喜歡這部電影。
"I like this movie very much."

太……了 — expresses something that feels excessive, surprising, or strongly emotional.

Example: 今天太熱了！
"Today is too hot!"

今天真開心！
Today is really wonderful!
我非常開心。
I am extremely happy.
今天太開心了！
Today is so much fun!
