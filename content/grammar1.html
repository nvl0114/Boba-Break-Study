Basic Sentence Patterns

Mandarin commonly uses Subject + Verb and Subject + Verb + Object. You can also make negative sentences and yes/no questions using 不, 嗎, and A-not-A patterns.

🧋 The Basic Idea

Think of Mandarin sentence patterns as building blocks. Start with the subject, add the verb, and add an object when needed.

1

S + V

The simplest sentence pattern: Subject + Verb.

Positive
S + V
Example
我吃
Wǒ chī
I eat.
Example
我喝
Wǒ hē
I drink.
Example
哥哥吃
Gēge chī
Older brother eats.
Example
妹妹喝
Mèimei hē
Younger sister drinks.
Negative · 不 (bù)
S + 不 + V

Use 不 (bù) before the verb to make a sentence negative. It is similar to “don't” or “do not” in English.

Negative
我不吃
Wǒ bù chī
I don't eat.
Negative
我不喝
Wǒ bù hē
I don't drink.
Negative
哥哥不吃
Gēge bù chī
Older brother doesn't eat.
Negative
妹妹不喝
Mèimei bù hē
Younger sister doesn't drink.
💡 Remember: 不 goes directly before the verb.

哥哥 + 不 + 吃 → 哥哥不吃
Yes / No Question · 嗎 (ma)
S + V + 嗎？

Add 嗎 (ma) at the end of a sentence to turn it into a simple yes/no question.

Question
你吃嗎？
Nǐ chī ma?
Do you eat?
Question
你喝嗎？
Nǐ hē ma?
Do you drink?
Question
哥哥吃嗎？
Gēge chī ma?
Does older brother eat?
Question
妹妹喝嗎？
Mèimei hē ma?
Does younger sister drink?

A-not-A Question

Another common way to ask a yes/no question is the A-not-A pattern. Repeat the verb and put 不 between the two verbs.

S + V + 不 + V？
A-not-A
你吃不吃？
Nǐ chī bù chī?
Do you eat or not?
A-not-A
你喝不喝？
Nǐ hē bù hē?
Do you drink or not?
A-not-A
哥哥吃不吃？
Gēge chī bù chī?
Does older brother eat or not?
A-not-A
妹妹喝不喝？
Mèimei hē bù hē?
Does younger sister drink or not?
2

S + V + O

Add an Object (O) after the verb to say what someone eats, drinks, does, and so on.

Positive
S + V + O
Example
我吃蘋果
Wǒ chī píngguǒ
I eat apples.
Example
我喝牛奶
Wǒ hē niúnǎi
I drink milk.
Example
哥哥吃香蕉
Gēge chī xiāngjiāo
Older brother eats bananas.
Example
妹妹喝果汁
Mèimei hē guǒzhī
Younger sister drinks fruit juice.
Negative · 不 (bù)
S + 不 + V + O

Put 不 before the verb. The object stays after the verb.

Negative
我不吃蘋果
Wǒ bù chī píngguǒ
I don't eat apples.
Negative
我不喝牛奶
Wǒ bù hē niúnǎi
I don't drink milk.
Negative
哥哥不吃香蕉
Gēge bù chī xiāngjiāo
Older brother doesn't eat bananas.
Negative
妹妹不喝果汁
Mèimei bù hē guǒzhī
Younger sister doesn't drink fruit juice.
Yes / No Question · 嗎 (ma)
S + V + O + 嗎？

Add 嗎 at the end of the sentence to make a yes/no question.

Question
你吃蘋果嗎？
Nǐ chī píngguǒ ma?
Do you eat apples?
Question
你喝牛奶嗎？
Nǐ hē niúnǎi ma?
Do you drink milk?
Question
哥哥吃香蕉嗎？
Gēge chī xiāngjiāo ma?
Does older brother eat bananas?
Question
妹妹喝果汁嗎？
Mèimei hē guǒzhī ma?
Does younger sister drink fruit juice?

A-not-A with an Object

The same A-not-A pattern can be used when the sentence has an object. Repeat the verb and place 不 between the two verbs.

S + V + 不 + V + O？
A-not-A
你吃不吃蘋果？
Nǐ chī bù chī píngguǒ?
Do you eat apples?
A-not-A
你喝不喝牛奶？
Nǐ hē bù hē niúnǎi?
Do you drink milk?
A-not-A
哥哥吃不吃香蕉？
Gēge chī bù chī xiāngjiāo?
Does older brother eat bananas?
A-not-A
妹妹喝不喝果汁？
Mèimei hē bù hē guǒzhī?
Does younger sister drink fruit juice?
