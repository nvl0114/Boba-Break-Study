Basic Sentence Patterns

Mandarin commonly uses Subject + Verb and Subject + Verb + Object. You can also make negative sentences and yes/no questions using 不, 嗎, and A-not-A patterns.

🧋 The Basic Idea

Think of Mandarin sentence patterns like building blocks. Start with the subject, add the verb, and then add an object when needed.

1

S + V

The simplest sentence pattern: Subject + Verb.

Positive
S + V
Example
哥哥吃
Gēge chī
Older brother eats.
Negative · 不 (bù)
S + 不 + V

Use 不 (bù) before the verb to make a sentence negative. It is similar to “don't” or “do not” in English.

Negative
哥哥不吃
Gēge bù chī
Older brother doesn't eat.
💡 Remember: 不 goes directly before the verb.

哥哥 + 不 + 吃 → 哥哥不吃
Yes / No Question · 嗎 (ma)
S + V + 嗎？

Add 嗎 (ma) at the end of a sentence to turn it into a simple yes/no question.

Question
哥哥吃嗎？
Gēge chī ma?
Does older brother eat?

A-not-A Question

Another common way to ask a yes/no question is the A-not-A pattern. Repeat the verb and put 不 between the two verbs.

S + V + 不 + V？
A-not-A
哥哥吃不吃？
Gēge chī bù chī?
Does older brother eat or not?
2

S + V + O

Add an Object (O) after the verb to say what someone eats, drinks, does, and so on.

Positive
S + V + O
Example
哥哥吃香蕉
Gēge chī xiāngjiāo
Older brother eats bananas.
Negative · 不 (bù)
S + 不 + V + O

Put 不 before the verb. The object stays after the verb.

Negative
哥哥不吃香蕉
Gēge bù chī xiāngjiāo
Older brother doesn't eat bananas.
Yes / No Question · 嗎 (ma)
S + V + O + 嗎？

Add 嗎 at the end of the sentence to make a yes/no question.

Question
哥哥吃香蕉嗎？
Gēge chī xiāngjiāo ma?
Does older brother eat bananas?

A-not-A with an Object

The same A-not-A pattern can be used when the sentence has an object. Repeat the verb and place 不 between the two verbs.

S + V + 不 + V + O？
A-not-A
哥哥吃不吃香蕉？
Gēge chī bù chī xiāngjiāo?
Does older brother eat bananas or not?
✓

S + V

🧋 Easy way to remember:

不 = make it negative
嗎 = turn it into a yes/no question
A-not-A = ask “A or not A?”
