QUESTION WORDS • BEGINNER

誰 (shéi) = Who

Learn how to ask "who" in Chinese, and why it works differently from English. 🧋

👋 Before We Start

誰 (shéi) means "who". You will use it to ask about people: who is he, who is your friend, who is going to Taiwan.

The big surprise: in Chinese, the question word does not move to the front of the sentence like in English. It stays exactly where the answer will go.

What you will learn:

  • What 誰 means and how to say it
  • Why 誰 sits in the same place as the answer
  • Using 誰 as the subject of a sentence
  • Using 誰 with verbs (sleep, travel, study)

🧠 Let's Learn: 誰

1. What does 誰 mean?

誰 means "who". It is a question word used to ask about a person (or people).

QUESTION
他是誰？
tā shì shéi?
Who is he?
ANSWER
他是我的男朋友。
tā shì wǒ de nánpéngyǒu.
He is my boyfriend.
  • 誰 (shéi) = who
  • 他 (tā) = he
  • 是 (shì) = to be (is / am / are)
  • 男朋友 (nánpéngyǒu) = boyfriend
💡 You may also hear 誰 pronounced shuí. Both are correct. In Taiwan, shéi is very common.

2. 誰 stays where the answer goes

In English, the question word jumps to the front: "Who is he?". In Chinese, the question word does not move. It sits in the exact spot where the answer will appear.

ENGLISH
Who is he?
"Who" moves to the front
CHINESE
他是誰？
誰 stays in the answer's place
QUESTION
他是誰？
tā shì shéi?
Who is he?
ANSWER
他是我的男朋友。
tā shì wǒ de nánpéngyǒu.
He is my boyfriend.
他是tā shì
+
誰shéi (question)
→
他是tā shì
+
Answer我的男朋友
💡 The trick: To answer a Chinese question with 誰, just replace 誰 with the answer. Everything else in the sentence stays the same.

3. 誰 at the beginning of a sentence

What if the person you want to know about is the subject of the sentence? Then 誰 goes at the beginning, because the answer would also go at the beginning. This is not "moving to the front like English". It is simply the place where the answer goes.

QUESTION
誰是你的女朋友？
shéi shì nǐ de nǚpéngyǒu?
Who is your girlfriend?
ANSWER
她是我的女朋友。
tā shì wǒ de nǚpéngyǒu.
She is my girlfriend.
誰shéi
+
是你的女朋友shì nǐ de nǚpéngyǒu

  • 你的 (nǐ de) = your
  • 我的 (wǒ de) = my
  • 女朋友 (nǚpéngyǒu) = girlfriend
  • 她 (tā) = she
💡 Notice how the answer 她 sits in exactly the same spot as 誰. Same position, different word.

4. 誰 with verbs

When you want to know who does an action, put 誰 in front of the verb. The answer replaces 誰 in the same spot.

QUESTION
誰學習中文？
shéi xuéxí Zhōngwén?
Who studies Chinese?
ANSWER
我學習中文。
wǒ xuéxí Zhōngwén.
I study Chinese.
QUESTION
誰去台灣旅遊？
shéi qù Táiwān lǚyóu?
Who is going to Taiwan to travel?
ANSWER
我們去台灣旅遊。
wǒmen qù Táiwān lǚyóu.
We are going to Taiwan to travel.
誰shéi
+
Verbe.g. 學習, 睡覺, 去
  • 學習 (xuéxí) = to study / to learn
  • 中文 (Zhōngwén) = Chinese language
  • 去 (qù) = to go
  • 旅遊 / 旅行 (lǚyóu / lǚxíng) = to travel
  • 睡覺 (shuìjiào) = to sleep
💡 旅遊 and 旅行 both mean "to travel". 旅遊 is more about a trip for fun and sightseeing, while 旅行 is the general word for traveling. In daily life, many people use them almost the same way.

5. Quick summary

Remember this one rule and you can handle every 誰 question.

  • 他是誰？ → 他是我的朋友。
    誰 is at the end, because the answer is at the end.
  • 誰是女生？ → 她是女生。
    誰 is at the start, because the answer is at the start.
  • 誰睡覺？ → 我睡覺。
    誰 is before the verb, because the answer is before the verb.
💡 Golden rule: Chinese does not move the question word. Find where the answer would go, and put 誰 there. To answer, swap 誰 for the real answer.
