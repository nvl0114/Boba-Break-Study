🧠 Grammar & Sentence Explanations

1. 會 (huì) — Learned Skills

Pattern: Subject + 會 + Verb

會 is used to talk about a skill that someone has learned. It means "know how to" or "can."

💡 我會說英文。 (I can speak English.)
💡 我不會游泳。 (I can't swim.)

2. 能 (néng) — Ability Based on Circumstances

Pattern: Subject + 能 + Verb

能 means "to be able to." It is used when the ability to do something depends on a person's situation, physical condition, time, or other circumstances — not on a learned skill.

💡 我下午不能來。 (I can't come in the afternoon.)
💡 我晚上能來。 (I can come in the evening.)

3. 可以 (kěyǐ) — Permission

Pattern: Subject + 可以 + Verb

可以 is commonly used to ask for or give permission. It can mean "may," "be allowed to," or sometimes "can."

💡 我可以出去嗎？ (May I go out?)
💡 你可以來我家。 (You are allowed to come to my house.)
Quick memory trick:

會 → a learned skill. 能 → depends on circumstances. 可以 → permission.

4. 的話 (de huà) — "If..."

Pattern: (如果) + Clause + 的話 + Result

的話 is placed at the end of a conditional clause to mean "if" or "in that case." It can be used together with 如果 (rúguǒ, "if") at the beginning of the sentence, or on its own — 的話 alone is very common in everyday speech.

💡 你可以來的話，再跟我說。 (If you can come, let me know.)

5. 再 (zài) — "Then" / "Again"

Pattern: Subject + 再 + Verb

再 placed before a verb means the action happens afterward, or as a next step — often "then" in English, especially after a condition. It can also mean "again," referring to a repeated action.

💡 你可以來的話，再跟我說。 (If you can come, tell me then.)
💡 先問媽媽，再來我家。 (Ask mom first, then come to my house.)
