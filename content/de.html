Possession with 的

Learn how to say “my”, “your”, and “their” in Mandarin. 🌷

🧠 Let's Learn: 的 (de)

1. What does 的 mean?

的 (de) is used to show that something belongs to someone. In English, it often means “'s” or “my / your / his / her / our”.

我的手機
wǒ de shǒujī · my phone
Think of it like this:

我 = I / me
的 = shows belonging
手機 = phone

So, 我的手機 means my phone.

2. The Basic Pattern

Put the person first, then 的, and finally the thing that belongs to them.

+
的belonging

Nounthe thing
Formula: Subject + 的 + Noun

Owner + 的 + thing

Example: 你 + 的 + 錢 = 你的錢 (your money)

3. Make It Personal

Change the first word to talk about a different person. The pattern stays the same!

他的包
tā de bāo · his bag
她的手機
tā de shǒujī · her phone

4. A Small Tip: 你、妳、您

你 = you (commonly used)
妳 = you (female, often used in writing)
您 = you (polite / respectful)

All three can use the same pattern: 你的 / 妳的 / 您的.

您的錢包
nín de qiánbāo · your wallet (polite)

5. One More Useful Word: 我們

我們 (wǒmen) means “we / us”. Add 的 to show something belongs to the group.

我們的朋友
wǒmen de péngyǒu · our friend

Remember: Owner + 的 + Noun. Keep the order simple and steady. 🌱
