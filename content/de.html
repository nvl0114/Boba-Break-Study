Possession with 的

Learn how to say “my”, “your”, and “their” in Mandarin. 🌷

🧠 Let's Learn: 的 (de)

1. The Pattern

的 (de) shows that something belongs to someone. In English, it works like “'s” (Tom's phone). It also covers words like my, your, our, their, his, her.

Put the owner first, then 的, then the thing.

💡 Good to Know

  • 你 / 妳 / 您 all mean “you”. 妳 is for a woman. 您 is more polite.
  • 他 / 她 sound the same (tā). 他 = he, 她 = she.
  • 我們 / 你們 / 他們 mean “we”, “you (more than one)”, and “they”. Add 的 and they become “our”, “your”, and “their”.
  • 們 makes a word plural (more than one person).

The pattern never changes: Owner + 的 + Thing 🌱
