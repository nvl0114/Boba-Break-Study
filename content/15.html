LESSON 15 • TELLING TIME

Telling Time in Mandarin Chinese 🕐

Learn how to tell the time, talk about minutes and seconds, and describe different parts of the day in Mandarin.

🕐 Telling Time in Mandarin

Telling time in Mandarin is quite logical. You usually say the hour first, followed by the minute.

Hour + 點 + Minute + 分
三點十分 → 3:10
八點二十五分 → 8:25
三點
3:00
三點五分
3:05
六點十分
6:10
九點二十五分
9:25

🧠 Grammar Patterns & Examples

1. 點 — Hour / O'clock

Number + 點

點 (diǎn) means "o'clock" or indicates the hour on a clock.

一點 = 1:00
兩點 = 2:00
三點 = 3:00
八點 = 8:00
十二點 = 12:00

現在三點。
Xiànzài sān diǎn.
It is 3:00 now.

2. 分 — Minutes

Hour + 點 + Minute + 分

分 (fēn) means minute(s). Put it after the number of minutes.

五分 = 5 minutes
十分 = 10 minutes
二十分 = 20 minutes
三十五分 = 35 minutes

For clock time, say 三點五分, not the more literal 三點五分鐘.

三點五分。
Sān diǎn wǔ fēn.
3:05.

3. 半 — Half Past

Hour + 半

半 (bàn) means half. In time expressions, it means 30 minutes past the hour.

三點半 = 3:30
七點半 = 7:30
十點半 = 10:30

You normally do not need to say 三十分.

現在六點半。
Xiànzài liù diǎn bàn.
It is 6:30 now.

4. 刻 — Quarter

一刻 = :15
半 = :30
三刻 = :45

刻 (kè) is used for 15-minute units.

一刻 = 15 minutes
兩刻 = 30 minutes
三刻 = 45 minutes

三點一刻 = 3:15
三點兩刻 = 3:30
三點三刻 = 3:45

三點半 is much more common than 三點兩刻 for 3:30.

三點一刻。
Sān diǎn yí kè.
3:15.

5. 秒 — Seconds

Hour + 點 + Minute + 分 + Second + 秒

秒 (miǎo) means second(s).

十秒 = 10 seconds
三十秒 = 30 seconds
五十五秒 = 55 seconds

For a very precise time: 三點十分二十秒 = 3:10:20.

秒 is usually unnecessary in everyday conversation.

三點十分二十秒。
Sān diǎn shí fēn èr shí miǎo.
3:10:20.

6. 現在 — Now

現在 + Time

現在 (xiànzài) means "now".

現在幾點？
What time is it now?

現在三點。
It's 3:00 now.

現在三點半。
It's 3:30 now.

現在幾點？ is a very natural and simple way to ask the current time.

現在幾點？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
What time is it now?

7. Parts of the Day

Part of Day + Clock Time

Mandarin has several expressions for different parts of the day. Their boundaries are approximate.

清晨 5:00–7:00 • early dawn
早晨 5:00–8:00 • early morning
早上 5:00–10:00 • morning
上午 8:00–12:00 • morning / AM
中午 11:00–13:00 • noon
下午 13:00–17:00 • afternoon / PM
傍晚 17:00–19:00 • early evening
晚上 18:00–24:00 • evening / night
夜裡 22:00–02:00 • during the night
凌晨 00:00–05:00 • after midnight

8. Putting Everything Together

Part of Day + Clock Time

You can combine a part-of-day expression with a clock time.

早上七點 → 7 AM
早上八點半 → 8:30 AM
上午十點十五分 → 10:15 AM
中午十二點 → 12 PM
下午兩點 → 2 PM
下午三點半 → 3:30 PM
傍晚六點 → 6 PM
晚上八點 → 8 PM
晚上十點四十五分 → 10:45 PM
夜裡十一點 → 11 PM
凌晨一點 → 1 AM
凌晨三點半 → 3:30 AM

晚上十點四十五分。
Wǎnshàng shí diǎn sìshíwǔ fēn.
10:45 PM.

9. Quick Formula 📝

現在 + Time

Exact time: 現在三點。

With minutes: 三點五分。

Half past: 三點半。

Quarter past: 三點一刻。

Quarter to: 三點三刻。

With a part of the day: 早上八點。

現在幾點？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
What time is it now?
