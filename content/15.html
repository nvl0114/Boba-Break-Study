LESSON 15 • TELLING TIME

Telling Time in Mandarin Chinese

Learn how to tell time, hours, minutes, fractions like half and quarters, and parts of the day in Mandarin.

💬 Conversation: Telling Time

David and Luna are coordinating their schedule and discussing the time.

David & Luna
Online
David
現在幾點？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
What time is it now?
Luna
現在下午三點半。
Xiànzài xiàwǔ sān diǎn bàn.
It's 3:30 PM now.
David
你早上幾點起床？
Nǐ zǎoshang jǐ diǎn qǐchuáng?
What time do you get up in the morning?
Luna
我早上六點起床。
Wǒ zǎoshang liù diǎn qǐchuáng.
I get up at 6 in the morning.

🧠 Grammar Notes & Quick Patterns

1. 點 — Hour

Number + 點

Explanation: 點 (diǎn) means o'clock / hour.

現在三點。
Xiànzài sān diǎn.
It is 3:00 now.

2. 分 — Minutes

Hour + 點 + Minute + 分

Explanation: 分 (fēn) means minute(s). Put it after the number of minutes.

六點十分
Liù diǎn shí fēn
6:10

3. 半 — Half past

Hour + 點 + 半

Explanation: 半 (bàn) means half (30 minutes). You don't need to say 三十分.

現在六點半。
Xiànzài liù diǎn bàn.
It is 6:30 now.

4. 刻 — Quarter

Hour + 點 + 刻 / 三刻

Explanation: 刻 (kè) is used for 15 minutes (一刻 = 15 min, 三刻 = 45 min).

三點一刻
Sān diǎn yí kè
3:15

5. 秒 — Seconds

Hour + 點 + Minute + 分 + Second + 秒

Explanation: 秒 (miǎo) means second(s). Used for precise time.

三點十分二十秒
Sān diǎn shí fēn èrshí miǎo
3:10:20

6. 現在 — Now

現在 + [Time]

Explanation: 現在 (xiànzài) means now. Use it when asking or answering current time.

現在幾點？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
What time is it now?

7. Parts of the Day

Part of Day + Hour + 點

Explanation: Combine time-of-day words like 早上 (morning), 下午 (afternoon), 晚上 (evening), 凌晨 (early morning) before the hour.

下午三點半
Xiàwǔ sān diǎn bàn
3:30 PM
