The Measure Word 個 (Gè) & Numbers

Learn how measure words work in Mandarin, master the versatile counter 個, and count with numbers.

🧠 Grammar Patterns: Measure Words & 個

1. Introduction to Measure Words

Number + Measure Word + Noun

Explanation: In Mandarin Chinese, you cannot attach a number directly to a noun. Whenever you want to count objects, people, or concepts, you must use a specific measure word (classifier) in between the number and the noun.

Structure Pattern: Number + Measure Word + Noun (e.g., 三個人)

三個人
Sān gè rén.
Three people.

2. Introduction to 個 (Gè)

The Most Common Measure Word

Explanation: 個 (gè) is the most common and versatile general measure word in Mandarin. It is often used for people, abstract ideas, places, and many everyday nouns that do not have a specialized or unique measure word.

Tip: When in doubt with a generic noun, 個 is frequently your safest fallback option!

這問題
Zhè gè wèntí.
This problem / question.

3. Nouns Used with 個 (Gè)

人, 地方, 問題, 月, 公司

Explanation: In our vocabulary list, several common nouns pair up with 個 directly when counting or specifying "this/that":

• 一個人 (One person)
• 一個地方 (One place)
• 一個問題 (One problem)
• 一個月 (One month)
• 一個公司 (One company)

那公司
Nà gè gōngsī.
That company.

4. Demonstratives with 個 (這 / 那)

這 / 那 + 個 + Noun

Explanation: To say "this" (這 zhè) or "that" (那 nà) before a noun, you also need to insert 個 in between:

• 這 + 個 + 人 = 這個人 (This person)
• 那 + 個 + 地方 = 那個地方 (That place)

這個人
Zhè gè rén.
This person.
