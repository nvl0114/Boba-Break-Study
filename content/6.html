🧠 Grammar & Sentence Explanations

1. 叫什麼名字 (What name) & 叫... / 我姓...

• 叫 (jiào) means "to be called" or "to be named". Structure: `Name + 叫 + Name`
• 什麼名字 (shénme míngzi) translates to "what name". E.g., `您叫什麼名字？` (What is your name?)
• 姓 (xìng) means family surname. E.g., `我姓林` (My surname is Lin).

2. 幾歲 (How old) & Number + 歲

• 幾 (jǐ) means "how many" (typically for numbers under 10). 歲 (suì) means "years old".
• To state your age, combine a number with 歲: `Number + 歲` (E.g., `二十歲` for 20 years old).

3. Number + 十 + Number (Tens Formation)

• In Mandarin, tens are formed by putting the multiplier digit before 十 (shí), and the remainder unit after.
• E.g., `二十` (èrshí) = 2 × 10 = 20. `二十二` (èrshí'èr) = 20 + 2 = 22.

4. S + 呢 (Topic Marker / Turn-Tossing)

• 呢 (ne) is a structural particle placed at the end of a noun or pronoun to toss a question back to the other person (equivalent to "How about...?" or "And you?").
• E.g., `我叫美美。您呢？` (My name is Meimei. How about you?).

5. 什麼 (shénme - What) & Interrogative Sentences

• In Mandarin, question words like 什麼 stay in the exact same position where the answer would go in a regular statement (no moving to the front like in English).
• E.g., Statement: `我的愛好是電影。` → Question: `您的愛好是什麼？` (What is your hobby?).

6. 也 (yě - Also / Too)

• 也 (yě) means "also" or "too". Word order rule: **也** always comes **before the verb or adjective**, right after the subject.
• E.g., `我也很高興認識您！` (I *also* am very glad to meet you!).
