CONVERSATION LESSON • SELF INTRODUCTION

Introduce Self 自我介紹

Learn how to introduce yourself, ask someone's name, surname, nationality, age, and hobbies in Mandarin.

In this lesson, you will learn:

• How to say your name and surname
• How to ask someone's name and age
• How to use 什麼 for questions
• How to use 呢 to ask “How about you?”
• How to use 也 for “also / too”
• How to introduce your hobbies

📚 Grammar

Let's understand the sentence patterns from the conversation.

1

Asking & Saying Names

叫什麼名字？
叫 means “to be called / to be named.”

To ask someone's name:
你 / 您 + 叫什麼名字？

To say your name:
我叫 + Name。
您叫什麼名字？
Nín jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?

我叫美美。
Wǒ jiào Měiměi.
My name is Meimei.
💡 叫 + Name = “be called + name”
2

Saying Your Surname

我姓 + Surname
姓 means “surname / family name.”

Use:
我姓 + 姓氏。

To ask someone's surname:
您貴姓？
我姓林。
Wǒ xìng Lín.
My surname is Lin.

您貴姓？
Nín guìxìng?
What is your surname?
3

Asking Age: 幾歲

Number + 歲
幾歲 means “how old?”

Ask:
你 / 您 + 幾歲？

Answer:
我 + Number + 歲。
您幾歲？
Nín jǐ suì?
How old are you?

我二十二歲。
Wǒ èrshí'èr suì.
I'm 22 years old.
4

Numbers: 十

Number + 十 + Number
For numbers from 20–99, Mandarin uses 十 as the tens marker.

For example:
二十 = 20
二十二 = 22
三十五 = 35
二十歲
èrshí suì
20 years old

二十二歲
èrshí'èr suì
22 years old
5

呢 — “How about...?”

Statement + 呢？
呢 is commonly used to return a question to another person.

English:
“How about you?”

Instead of repeating the whole sentence, simply use 呢？
我叫美美。您呢？
Wǒ jiào Měiměi. Nín ne?
My name is Meimei. How about you?

我二十歲。您呢？
Wǒ èrshí suì. Nín ne?
I'm 20 years old. How about you?
6

什麼 — “What”

Question Word + Sentence
什麼 means “what.”

Mandarin usually does not move question words to the beginning like English.

Keep 什麼 in the position where the answer would normally appear.
您叫什麼名字？
Nín jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?

您姓什麼？
Nín xìng shénme?
What is your surname?

您的愛好是什麼？
Nín de àihào shì shénme?
What are your hobbies?
7

也 — “Also / Too”

Subject + 也 + Verb / Adjective
也 means “also / too.”

It usually comes before the verb or adjective.
我也很高興認識您。
Wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng rènshi nín.
Nice to meet you too.
💡 也 = also / too
我也... = I also... / Me too...
