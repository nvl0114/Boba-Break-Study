Using 是 (to be)

Learn how to say “I am a teacher” and ask “Are you a student?” in Mandarin. 🧋

🧠 Let's Learn: 是 (shì)

1. What is 是?

是 (shì) works like “is / am / are” in English. It connects a person to who or what they are.

Important: the word after 是 must be a description (a noun) like “teacher” or “student”. It is not a verb (an action word).

  • ✓ 是 + noun → 我是老師 (I am a teacher)
  • ✗ 是 + verb → we don't say “I am eat”. For actions, we use the verb directly.
我是老師
wǒ shì lǎoshī
I am a teacher.

2. Positive Sentence (+)

Say who you are talking about, then 是, then the identity. The first word can be a person (S) or any other word.

Swho
+
是is / am / are
+
Identitywhat
我是老師
wǒ shì lǎoshī
I am a teacher.

3. Negative Sentence (−)

Put 不 (bù) in front of 是 to say “am not / is not / are not”.

Swho
+
不是is not
+
Identitywhat
我不是老師
wǒ bú shì lǎoshī
I am not a teacher.

💡 Sound tip: 不 is normally bù, but before another 4th-tone word like 是 it sounds like bú.

4. Questions (?) — 2 Ways

There are two easy ways to ask a yes/no question. Both mean the same thing.

Way 1: use 是不是 (is or is not?)

Swho
+
是不是is or is not
+
Identitywhat
你是不是老師？
nǐ shì bú shì lǎoshī?
Are you a teacher?

Way 2: add 嗎 (ma) at the end of the sentence

Swho
+
是is / am / are
+
Identitywhat
+
嗎question
你是老師嗎？
nǐ shì lǎoshī ma?
Are you a teacher?

5. How to Answer

Answering is simple. Say 是 / 是的 for “Yes” and 不是 for “No”. Then repeat the sentence.

  • Yes = 是 / 是的
  • No = 不是
Q
你是不是老師？
nǐ shì bú shì lǎoshī?
Are you a teacher?
A (Yes)
是！我是老師。
shì! wǒ shì lǎoshī.
Yes! I am a teacher.
Q
你是老師嗎？
nǐ shì lǎoshī ma?
Are you a teacher?
A (No)
不是！我不是老師。
bú shì! wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.
No! I am not a teacher.

6. More Examples with 的

You can make the identity longer with 的. “中文的老師” means “Chinese teacher” (teacher of Chinese). All 4 patterns still work the same way.

(+)
我是中文的老師
wǒ shì zhōngwén de lǎoshī
I am a Chinese teacher.
(−)
我不是中文的老師
wǒ bú shì zhōngwén de lǎoshī
I am not a Chinese teacher.
(?) 是不是
你是不是中文的老師？
nǐ shì bú shì zhōngwén de lǎoshī?
Are you a Chinese teacher?
(?) 嗎
你是中文的老師嗎？
nǐ shì zhōngwén de lǎoshī ma?
Are you a Chinese teacher?

💡 Good to Know

  • 是 is used with identity words (teacher, student, employee). It is not used before action words.
  • 不是 = “is not”. 不 always goes before 是.
  • 是不是 and 嗎 ask the same question. Pick the one you like!
  • To answer “yes”, say 是 or 是的. To answer “no”, say 不是.
  • 的 links two nouns, like 工廠的員工 (factory's employee = factory employee).

The patterns never change: S + 是 + Identity, S + 不是 + Identity, S + 是不是 + Identity, S + 是 + Identity + 嗎 🌱
