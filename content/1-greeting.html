Lesson 1 — Greetings & Introductions

David:
您好！ (nín hǎo) Hello
Amy:
您好！ (nín hǎo) Hello
David:
請問，妳叫什麼名字？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?) Excuse me, what is your name?
Amy:
我叫 Amy。你呢？ (Wǒ jiào Amy. Nǐ ne?) My name is Amy. How about you?
David:
我叫 David。請問，妳貴姓？ (Wǒ jiào David. Qǐngwèn, nǐ guìxìng?) My name is David. Excuse me, what is your family name?
Amy:
我姓林。你姓什麼？ (Wǒ xìng Lín. Nǐ xìng shénme?) My family name is Lin. What is your family name?
David:
我姓王。妳是哪國人？ (Wǒ xìng Wáng. Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?) My family name is Wang. What nationality are you?
Amy:
我是美國人。你呢？ (Wǒ shì Měiguó rén. Nǐ ne?) I am American. How about you?
David:
我是台灣人。 (Wǒ shì Táiwān rén.) I am Taiwanese.
Amy:
你是不是學生？ (Nǐ shì bú shì xuéshēng?) Are you a student?
David:
是，我是學生。妳呢？ (Shì, wǒ shì xuéshēng. Nǐ ne?) Yes, I am a student. How about you?
Amy:
我也是學生。很高興認識你！ (Wǒ yě shì xuéshēng. Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ!) I am also a student. Nice to meet you!
David:
我也很高興認識妳！ (Wǒ yě hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ!) Nice to meet you too!

Grammar & Notes

1. 姓 (xìng) — Family Name

Used to state someone's family name or surname.

我姓王。
Wǒ xìng Wáng.
My family name is Wang.
我姓林。
Wǒ xìng Lín.
My family name is Lin.

2. 叫 (jiào) — To Be Called

Used to state someone's full name or given name.

我叫 David。
Wǒ jiào David.
My name is David.
我叫 Amy。
Wǒ jiào Amy.
My name is Amy.

3. 是 (shì) — To Be

Used to connect a person with an identity, nationality, or occupation.

我是學生。
Wǒ shì xuéshēng.
I am a student.
我是美國人。
Wǒ shì Měiguó rén.
I am American.
我是台灣人。
Wǒ shì Táiwān rén.
I am Taiwanese.

4. 什麼 (shénme) — What

Used as an interrogative word to ask about unknown details.

你叫什麼名字？
Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?
你姓什麼？
Nǐ xìng shénme?
What is your family name?

5. 是不是 — A-not-A Question

Used to form a yes/no question using the verb "to be".

你是不是學生？
Nǐ shì bú shì xuéshēng?
Are you a student?
是，我是學生。
Shì, wǒ shì xuéshēng. (Yes)

6. 嗎 (ma) — Question Particle

Placed at the end of a statement to turn it into a yes/no question.

你是台灣人嗎？
Nǐ shì Táiwān rén ma?
Are you Taiwanese?
不是，我是美國人。
Bú shì, wǒ shì Měiguó rén. (No)
