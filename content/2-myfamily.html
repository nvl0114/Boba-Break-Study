My Family (我的家人）

老師 :
小美 :
小明 :
老師：
小美，你家有幾個人？ Lǎoshī: Xiǎoměi, nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ gè rén? Teacher: Xiaomei, how many people are there in your family?
小美：
我家有六個人，有爸爸、媽媽、哥哥、妹妹和我。 Xiǎoměi: Wǒ jiā yǒu liù gè rén, yǒu bàba, māma, gēge, mèimei hàn wǒ. Xiaomei: There are six people in my family: my dad, mom, older brother, younger sister, and me.
小明：
你有沒有姐姐？ Xiǎomíng: Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu jiějie? Xiaoming: Do you have an older sister?
小美：
沒有，我沒有姐姐。 Xiǎoměi: Méiyǒu, wǒ méiyǒu jiějie. Xiaomei: No, I don't have an older sister.
小明：
那你有沒有哥哥？ Xiǎomíng: Nà nǐ yǒu méiyǒu gēge? Xiaoming: Then, do you have an older brother?
小美：
有，我有一個哥哥。你呢？ Xiǎoměi: Yǒu, wǒ yǒu yí gè gēge. Nǐ ne? Xiaomei: Yes, I have one older brother. What about you?
小明：
我家有五個人，有爸爸、媽媽、兩個弟弟和我。 Xiǎomíng: Wǒ jiā yǒu wǔ gè rén, yǒu bàba, māma, liǎng gè dìdi hàn wǒ. Xiaoming: There are five people in my family: my dad, mom, two younger brothers, and me.
小美：
你有沒有姐姐和妹妹？ Xiǎoměi: Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu jiějie hàn mèimei? Xiaomei: Do you have an older sister or a younger sister?
小明：
沒有，我沒有姐姐，也沒有妹妹。我只有兩個弟弟。 Xiǎomíng: Méiyǒu, wǒ méiyǒu jiějie, yě méiyǒu mèimei. Wǒ zhǐ yǒu liǎng gè dìdi. Xiaoming: No, I don't have an older sister or a younger sister. I only have two younger brothers.
老師：
你們都有兄弟姐妹嗎？ Lǎoshī: Nǐmen dōu yǒu xiōngdì jiěmèi ma? Teacher: Do you both have siblings?
小美：
有啊，我有哥哥和妹妹。 Xiǎoměi: Yǒu a, wǒ yǒu gēge hé mèimei. Xiaomei: Yes, I have an older brother and a younger sister.
小明：
有啊，我有兩個弟弟。 Xiǎomíng: Yǒu a, wǒ yǒu liǎng gè dìdi. Xiaoming: Yes, I have two younger brothers.
老師：
很好！你們的家人都很有趣。 Lǎoshī: Hěn hǎo! Nǐmen de jiārén dōu hěn yǒuqù. Teacher: Great! Your families are very interesting.
小明：
老師，你有沒有兄弟姐妹？ Xiǎomíng: Lǎoshī, nǐ yǒu méiyǒu xiōngdì jiěmèi? Xiaoming: Teacher, do you have any siblings?
老師：
有，我有一個姐姐。 Lǎoshī: Yǒu, wǒ yǒu yí gè jiějie. Teacher: Yes, I have an older sister.

Grammar & Notes

1. 有 — yǒu — “to have / there is / there are”

Used to say that someone has something or that something exists.

我有一個哥哥。
Wǒ yǒu yí gè gēge.
I have an older brother.

沒有 — méiyǒu — “do not have / there isn’t / there aren’t”

The negative form of 有.

我沒有姐姐。
Wǒ méiyǒu jiějie.
I don’t have an older sister.

2. 有沒有 — yǒu méiyǒu — “do you have...?”

An A-not-A question pattern used to ask whether someone has something.

你有沒有姐姐？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu jiějie?
Do you have an older sister?

Answers:

有。 — Yes, I do.
沒有。 — No, I don’t.

3. 幾 — jǐ — “how many”

Used to ask about an unknown number.

你家有幾個人？
Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ gè rén?
How many people are there in your family?

4. 個 — gè — general measure word

個 is a common measure word used before people and many other nouns.

一個哥哥
yí gè gēge
one older brother
兩個弟弟
liǎng gè dìdi
two younger brothers

The basic pattern is:

Number + 個 + noun

5. 兩 — liǎng — “two”

兩 is commonly used before a measure word, while 二 is commonly used for numbers, counting, dates, phone numbers, etc.

兩個弟弟
liǎng gè dìdi
two younger brothers

6. 和 — hàn — “and”

Used to connect nouns or people.

爸爸、媽媽和我
bàba, māma hàn wǒ
Dad, Mom, and me.
哥哥和妹妹
gēge hàn mèimei
Older brother and younger sister.

Pattern:

A + 和 + B = A and B.
