LESSON 13 • TIME & SENTENCE STRUCTURE

Advanced Time Expressions & Word Order

Mastering time word placement, subjects, verbs, objects, and locations in Mandarin sentences.

📖 Lesson Overview & Slide Content

In Mandarin Chinese, expressing when and where actions happen follows specific structural rules. Unlike English where time phrases often appear at the very end of a sentence, Mandarin places time words either right before or right after the subject.

🧠 Grammar Patterns: Sentence Structure with Time

Slide 2: Time + S + V + O

Time + Subject + Verb + Object

Explanation: The time expression comes at the very beginning of the sentence, right before the subject.

今天我打電話。
Jīntiān wǒ dǎ diànhuà.
Today I am making a phone call.

Slide 3: S + Time + V + O

Subject + Time + Verb + Object

Explanation: The time expression can also be placed right after the subject and before the verb.

我明天考試。
Wǒ míngtiān kǎoshì.
I have an exam tomorrow.

Slide 4: S + Time + Place + V + O

Subject + Time + Place + Verb + Object

Explanation: When both time and location are present, the time comes first, followed by the place, then the action.

昨天我在圖書館練習中文
Zuótiān wǒ zài túshūguǎn liànxí zhōngwén.
Yesterday I practiced Chinese at the library.
