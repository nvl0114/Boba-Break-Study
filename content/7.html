LESSON 7 • LOCATIONS & PRESENCE

Locations, Presence & Activities (在)

Master expressions for locations, checking if someone is present, and pairing places with activities.

🧠 Grammar Patterns: 習慣與位置 (zai)

1. Basic Location Presence

Subject + 在 + Place

Explanation: Use 在 (zài) to indicate that a person or object is located at a specific place.

Examples: 醫生在醫院。(The doctor is at the hospital.) | 蘋果在家。(The apple is at home.)

醫生在醫院。
Yīshēng zài yīyuàn.
The doctor is at the hospital.

2. Negative Location Presence

Subject + 不在 + Place

Explanation: Place 不 (bù) before 在 to indicate someone or something is NOT at a location.

Examples: 他不在公園。(He is not at the park.) | 香蕉不在學校。(The banana is not at school.)

他不在公園。
Tā bú zài gōngyuán.
He is not at the park.

3. Asking Presence with 嗎 or 在不在

Subject + 在 + Place + 嗎？ / Subject + 在不在 + Place

Explanation: Form questions using question particle 嗎 at the end or the A-not-A form 在不在.

Examples: 老師在教室嗎？ (Is the teacher in the classroom?) | 他在不在日本？ (Is he in Japan or not?)

老師在教室嗎？
Lǎoshī zài jiàoshì ma?
Is the teacher in the classroom?

4. Asking "Where?" (在哪裡)

Subject + 在 + 哪裡 (nǎlǐ)?

Explanation: Use 哪裡 to ask for the physical location of someone or something.

Example: 媽媽在哪裡？ (Where is mom?)

媽媽在哪裡？
Māma zài nǎlǐ?
Where is mom?

5. Places with Activities

Subject + 在 + Place + Activity

Explanation: Combine location phrases with continuous actions such as studying, working, or exercising.

Example: 哥哥在日本讀書。(Older brother studies in Japan.)

哥哥在日本讀書。
Gēge zài rìběn dúshū.
Older brother studies in Japan.
