🧠 Grammar Patterns: 在 (Zài)

1. Location Pattern: Subject + 在 (Zài) + Location

Subject + 在 + Location

Explanation: The word 在 (zài) acts as a preposition meaning "at" or "in". It is placed before a location noun to tell where someone or something is located.

Negative Form: Subject + 不在 (bù zài) + Location (e.g., 我不在家 - I am not at home)
Interrogative & Answer: Subject + 在 + 哪裡 (nǎlǐ)? Answer: Subject + 在 + Location.

我在家。
Wǒ zài jiā.
I am at home.

2. Action at Location: Subject + 在 + Location + Verb

Subject + 在 + Location + Verb

Explanation: Unlike English (where you say "I read books *at* home"), Chinese structure places the location *before* the action verb: Subject + [At Location] + [Verb].

Negative Form: Subject + 不 + 在 + Location + Verb (e.g., 我不在學校讀書 - I don't study at school)
Interrogative Form: Subject + 在 + 哪裡 + Verb? (Where do you [verb]?)

我在學校讀書。
Wǒ zài xuéxiào dúshū.
I study at school.

3. Ongoing Action: 正在 (Zhèngzài) + Verb

Subject + 正在 + Verb (Doing...)

Explanation: To express that an action is currently in progress (equivalent to "-ing" in English), use 正在 (zhèngzài) or just 在 right before the verb.

Negative Form: Subject + 沒有 (méiyǒu) + 正在 + Verb (e.g., 我沒有正在上課 - I am not currently having class)
Interrogative & Answer: Subject + 正在 + 嗎? or 你在做什麼? Answer: 正在 + Verb.

我正在上課。
Wǒ zhèngzài shàngkè.
I am having class (right now).

4. Questions & Answers with 哪裡 (Where)

Question: 你在哪裡？ Answer: 我在...

Explanation: To ask "Where are you?", use 哪裡 (nǎlǐ). The response mirrors the pattern using 在 + Location.

• Q: 你在哪裡？ (Where are you?)
• A: 我在公園。 (I am at the park.)
• Q: 你在哪裡上班？ (Where do you work?)
• A: 我在公司上班。 (I work at a company.)

你在哪裡上班？
Nǐ zài nǎlǐ shàngbān?
Where do you work?
