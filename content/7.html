GRAMMAR LESSON • LOCATIONS

Grammar: 在 (Zài) & Places

Learn how to state simple locations, check presence, and combine places with daily activities in Mandarin.

🧠 Grammar Card 1: Simple Locations & Presence

1. Simple Positive Location

Subject / Noun + 在 + Place

Explanation: Use 在 (zài) to state where a person or noun is located.

醫生在醫院 (Yīshēng zài yīyuàn - The doctor is at the hospital)
蘋果在家 (Píngguǒ zài jiā - The apple is at home)

2. Simple Negative Location

Subject / Noun + 不 + 在 + Place

Explanation: Place 不 (bù) before 在 to state where someone or something is not located.

他不在公園 (Tā bú zài gōngyuán - He is not at the park)
香蕉不在學校 (Xiāngjiāo bú zài xuéxiào - The banana is not at school)

3. Location Question with 嗎

Subject / Noun + 在 + Place + 嗎？

Explanation: Add 嗎 (ma) to ask if someone is at a specific place. Answer using 對 (duì) or 不 (bù).

老師在教室嗎？ (Lǎoshī zài jiàoshì ma? - Is the teacher in the classroom?)
Answer (Positive): 對，老師在教室 (Duì, lǎoshī zài jiàoshì - Correct, the teacher is in the classroom)
Answer (Negative): 不，老師不在教室 (Bù, lǎoshī bú zài jiàoshì - No, the teacher is not in the classroom)
爸爸在辦公室嗎？ (Bàba zài bàngōngshì ma? - Is dad in the office?)
Answer (Positive): 對，爸爸在辦公室 (Duì, bàba zài bàngōngshì - Correct, dad is in the office)
Answer (Negative): 不，爸爸不在辦公室 (Bù, bàba bú zài bàngōngshì - No, dad is not in the office)

4. A-not-A Presence Question

Subject / Noun + 在 + 不 + 在 + Place

Explanation: Use 在不在 to ask whether someone is at a location. Give short answers like 在 or 不在.

他在不在日本？ (Tā zài bu zài Rìběn? - Is he in Japan or not?)
護士在不在醫院？ (Hùshi zài bu zài yīyuàn? - Is the nurse in the hospital or not?)
Short Answer (Positive): 在 (Zài - Here / Is there)
Short Answer (Negative): 不在 (Bú zài - Not here / Not there)

5. Asking If Someone Is There (Without Place)

在不在 / 在嗎 (Asking presence)

Explanation: Use 在嗎 or 在不在 without mentioning a place to simply ask if someone is available/there.

她在嗎？ (Tā zài ma? - Is she there?)
Short Answer (Positive): 在 (Zài - Here / Is there)
Short Answer (Negative): 不在 (Bú zài - Not here / Not there)
哥哥在不在？ (Gēge zài bu zài? - Is older brother there or not?)
Short Answer (Positive): 在 (Zài - Here / Is there)
Short Answer (Negative): 不在 (Bú zài - Not here / Not there)

6. Asking Where with 哪裡

Subject / Noun + 在 + 哪裡

Explanation: Use 哪裡 (nǎlǐ) to inquire about someone's current location.

媽媽在哪裡？ (Māma zài nǎlǐ? - Where is mom?)
Answer: 媽媽在家 (Māma zài jiā - Mom is at home)

🧠 Grammar Card 2: Locations with Activities

1. Positive Activity at Place

Subject + 在 + Place + Activity

Explanation: Combine location and activity to describe what someone is doing somewhere.

哥哥在日本讀書 (Gēge zài Rìběn dúshū - Older brother studies in Japan)
弟弟在家玩遊戲 (Dìdi zài jiā wán yóuxì - Younger brother plays games at home)

2. Negative Activity at Place

Subject + 不 + 在 + Place + Activity

Explanation: Place 不 (bù) before 在 to negate the action happening at that location.

老師不在醫院工作 (Lǎoshī bú zài yīyuàn gōngzuò - Teacher does not work at the hospital)
醫生不在教室教學 (Yīshēng bú zài jiàoshì jiàoxué - Doctor does not teach in the classroom)

3. Activity Question with 嗎

Subject + 在 + Place + Activity + 嗎？

Explanation: Add 嗎 (ma) to ask if someone is performing an activity at a place.

妹妹在學校上課嗎？ (Mèimei zài xuéxiào shàngkè ma? - Is younger sister having class at school?)
Answer (Positive): 對，妹妹在學校上課 (Duì, mèimei zài xuéxiào shàngkè - Correct, younger sister is having class at school)
Answer (Negative): 不，妹妹不在學校上課 (Bù, mèimei bú zài xuéxiào shàngkè - No, younger sister is not having class at school)
他在公園運動嗎？ (Tā zài gōngyuán yùndòng ma? - Is he exercising in the park?)
Answer (Positive): 對，他在公園運動 (Duì, tā zài gōngyuán yùndòng - Correct, he is exercising in the park)
Answer (Negative): 不，他不在公園運動 (Bù, tā bú zài gōngyuán yùndòng - No, he is not exercising in the park)

4. A-not-A Activity & Conjunction 和

Subject + 在 + 不 + 在 + Place + activity

Explanation: Use 在不在 for verification. Use 和 (hé) to link nouns meaning "and" (e.g., apples and bananas).

你們在不在家吃蘋果和香蕉？ (Nǐmen zài bu zài jiā chī píngguǒ hé xiāngjiāo? - Are you at home eating apples and bananas or not?)
Short Answer (Positive): 在 (Zài - Here / Is there)
Short Answer (Negative): 不在 (Bú zài - Not here / Not there)

5. Asking Where with Activity

Subject + 在 + 哪裡 + activity

Explanation: Use 哪裡 (nǎlǐ) combined with an activity to ask where someone is performing it.

爸爸在哪裡上班？ (Bàba zài nǎlǐ shàngbān? - Where does dad work?)
Answer: 爸爸在辦公室上班 (Bàba zài bàngōngshì shàngbān - Dad works in the office)
