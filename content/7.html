Grammar: 在 (Zài) & Places

Learn how to say where someone or something is, ask about locations, and combine 在 with activities.

Grammar Patterns

1

Basic Location

Use 在 to say that a person or thing is located somewhere.

Pattern Subject + 在 + Place
Examples
我在家。 Wǒ zài jiā. I am at home.
爸爸在辦公室。 Bàba zài bàngōngshì. Dad is in the office.
老師在學校。 Lǎoshī zài xuéxiào. The teacher is at school.
2

Negative Location

Use 不在 when someone or something is not at a particular place.

Pattern Subject + 不在 + Place
Examples
我不在家。 Wǒ bú zài jiā. I am not at home.
他不在學校。 Tā bú zài xuéxiào. He is not at school.
媽媽不在醫院。 Māma bú zài yīyuàn. Mom is not at the hospital.
3

Yes / No Questions with 嗎

Add 嗎 at the end of a location sentence to make a yes/no question.

Pattern S + 在 + Place + 嗎？
Examples
你在家嗎？ Nǐ zài jiā ma? Are you at home?
老師在學校嗎？ Lǎoshī zài xuéxiào ma? Is the teacher at school?
爸爸在辦公室嗎？ Bàba zài bàngōngshì ma? Is Dad in the office?
4

A-not-A Location Questions

Use 在不在 to ask whether someone is at a place or not.

Pattern S + 在不在 + Place？
Examples
你在不在家？ Nǐ zài bú zài jiā? Are you at home?
媽媽在不在醫院？ Māma zài bú zài yīyuàn? Is Mom at the hospital?
老師在不在學校？ Lǎoshī zài bú zài xuéxiào? Is the teacher at school?
Tip: 在不在 is an A-not-A pattern. It literally asks “is there / isn't there?”
5

Asking “Where?”

Use 哪裡 to ask where someone or something is.

Pattern S + 在 + 哪裡？
Examples
你在哪裡？ Nǐ zài nǎlǐ? Where are you?
媽媽在哪裡？ Māma zài nǎlǐ? Where is Mom?
老師在哪裡？ Lǎoshī zài nǎlǐ? Where is the teacher?
6

Location + Activity

You can place an activity after the location to say what someone is doing at that place.

Pattern S + 在 + Place + Activity
Examples
我在學校讀書。 Wǒ zài xuéxiào dúshū. I study at school.
爸爸在辦公室上班。 Bàba zài bàngōngshì shàngbān. Dad works at the office.
哥哥在公園運動。 Gēge zài gōngyuán yùndòng. My older brother exercises in the park.
