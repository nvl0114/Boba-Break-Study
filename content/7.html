🧠 Grammar Patterns: 在 (Zài) & Locations

1. Expressing Existence at a Place

Subject + 在 (Zài) + Location

Explanation: The word 在 (zài) acts as a preposition meaning "at" or "in". To state that someone or something is located at a specific place, put 在 and the location right after the subject.

Structure Pattern: Subject + 在 + Location (e.g., 醫生在醫院)

醫生在醫院
Yīshēng zài yīyuàn. — The doctor is at the hospital.

2. Negative Form of 在 (Zài)

Subject + 不 (Bù) + 在 + Location

Explanation: To say someone is not at a location, place the negative adverb 不 (bù) right before 在 (zài).

Structure Pattern: Subject + 不在 + Location (e.g., 他不在公園)

他不在公園
Tā bú zài gōngyuán. — He is not at the park.

3. Question Form with 嗎 (Ma) or 哪裡 (Nǎlǐ)

Subject + 在 + 哪裡 / Location + 嗎?

Explanation: You can ask yes/no location questions by adding 嗎 at the end, or ask "where" using 哪裡 (nǎlǐ) in place of the location.

• 媽媽在哪裡？ (Where is mom?)
• 老師在教室嗎？ (Is the teacher in the classroom?)

媽媽在哪裡
Māma zài nǎlǐ? — Where is mom?

4. Actions Happening at a Location

Subject + 在 + Location + Action Verb

Explanation: Unlike English (where "at" comes after the verb), in Mandarin, the location phrase introduced by 在 comes before the action verb.

• 哥哥在日本讀書 (Older brother studies in Japan)
• 爸爸在辦公室上班 (Dad works in the office)

哥哥在日本讀書
Gēge zài Rìběn dúshū. — Older brother studies in Japan.

5. People & Family Members

Family / People Nouns

Explanation: Talking about people and occupations who act as subjects in location sentences, such as doctors, teachers, family members, etc.

• 醫生 (Doctor) • 老師 (Teacher)
• 爸爸 (Dad) • 媽媽 (Mom) • 哥哥 (Older brother)

老師在教室嗎
Lǎoshī zài jiàoshì ma? — Is the teacher in the classroom?

6. Places & Workplaces

Location Nouns & Workplaces

Explanation: Common locations used with 在 to specify buildings, institutions, open spaces, or foreign countries.

• 醫院 (Hospital) • 公園 (Park)
• 學校 (School) • 辦公室 (Office) • 日本 (Japan)

爸爸在辦公室上班
Bàba zài bàngōngshì shàngbān. — Dad works in the office.
