Mastering 在 (Zài), Locations, & Ongoing Actions

Learn how to describe locations, ongoing actions, negative/interrogative forms, and core activities in Mandarin.

🧠 Grammar Patterns: 在 (Zài) & Ongoing Actions

Carousel 1: Location & Basic Questions

1. Affirmative Location

S + 在 + Place

Explanation: Used to state where someone or something is located.

我在家。
Wǒ zài jiā.
I am at home.

2. Negative Location

S + 不在 + Place

Explanation: Used to state where someone or something is not located.

我不在家。
Wǒ bú zài jiā.
I am not at home.

3. Yes/No Question with 嗎

S + 在 + Place + 嗎

Explanation: Add 嗎 at the end of a location sentence to ask if someone is at a place.

你在家嗎？
Nǐ zài jiā ma?
Are you at home?

4. Alternative Question (在不在)

S + 在不在 + Place

Explanation: Using the positive-negative form (在不在) to ask if someone is at a place.

你在不在家？
Nǐ zài bú zài jiā?
Are you home or not?

5. Where Question (哪裡)

S + 在 + 哪裡

Explanation: Use 哪裡 (nǎlǐ) to ask for the location of someone or something.

你在哪裡？
Nǐ zài nǎlǐ?
Where are you?

6. How to Answer

Answering Location Questions

Explanation:
• For 嗎 / 在不在 questions: Answer with 在 (Yes, am/is) or 不在 (No, am/not).
• For 哪裡 questions: Answer by stating the place.

我在學校。
Wǒ zài xuéxiào.
I am at school.

Carousel 2: Actions at a Location

1. Action at Place Pattern

S + 在 / 正在 + Place + V

Explanation:
• 在 + Place + V: Indicates doing an action at a specific location.
• 正在: Emphasizes that the action is actively happening right now.

Note on 在 vs 正在: 在 simply places the action at a location, whereas 正在 strongly highlights the continuous, ongoing progress of the action right at this moment.

我在學校讀書。
Wǒ zài xuéxiào dúshū.
I study at school.

2. Negative Action at Place

S + 沒（有）+ 在 + Place + V

Explanation: Used to state that an action is not/was not taking place at that location.

我沒有在學校讀書。
Wǒ méiyǒu zài xuéxiào dúshū.
I am not studying at school.

3. Action at Place Question with 嗎

S + 在 / 正在 + Place + V + 嗎

Explanation: Ask if someone is doing an action at a location using 嗎 at the end.

你在學校讀書嗎？
Nǐ zài xuéxiào dúshū ma?
Are you studying at school?

4. Alternative Question (在不在)

S + 在不在 + Place + V

Explanation: Asking whether someone is performing an action at a location or not.

你在不在學校讀書？
Nǐ zài bú zài xuéxiào dúshū?
Are you studying at school or not?

Carousel 3: Ongoing Actions

1. Ongoing Action Pattern

S + 在 + V

Explanation: Similar to "to be + V-ing" in English, showing an action currently in progress.

我在上課。
Wǒ zài shàngkè.
I am having class.

2. Negative Ongoing Action

S + 沒 + V

Explanation: Used to state that an ongoing action is not happening.

我沒上課。
Wǒ méi shàngkè.
I am not having class.

3. Ongoing Action Question

S + 在 + V + 嗎

Explanation: Ask if someone is currently doing a specific action.

你在上課嗎？
Nǐ zài shàngkè ma?
Are you having class?

4. Asking What Someone is Doing

你在做什麼？

Explanation: A common question used to ask "What are you doing?"

你在做什麼？
Nǐ zài zuò shénme?
What are you doing?

5. How to Answer

Answering Ongoing Actions

Explanation:
• Answer directly with the action in progress (e.g., 我正在讀書 / 我在玩遊戲).
• Or answer negatively using 沒 (e.g., 我沒玩遊戲).

我正在玩遊戲。
Wǒ zhèngzài wán yóuxì.
I am playing a game.
