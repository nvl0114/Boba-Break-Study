TIME & DISTANCE

How Far Is It?

Learn how to talk about distance, travel time, and how close or far places are.

💬 Conversation

Listen to the conversation and notice how the speakers use 離 to describe distance.

The pinyin below focuses only on the vocabulary words from this lesson.

小美
阿豪，你今天怎麼來淡水的？
Ahao, how did you come to Tamsui today?
阿豪
我坐了兩次公車，還坐了兩次火車。
I took the bus twice, and I also took the train twice.
小美
兩次公車，兩次火車？你家離淡水很遠嗎？
離 lí　遠 yuǎn
Two buses and two trains? Is your home far from Tamsui?
阿豪
很遠，大概三十公里。坐車要兩個小時。
遠 yuǎn　大概 dàgài　公里 gōnglǐ　小時 xiǎoshí
Very far. About thirty kilometers. It takes two hours by vehicle.
小美
其實我家離淡水也很遠。
其實 qíshí　離 lí　遠 yuǎn
Actually, my home is also far from Tamsui.
阿豪
我知道。那你怎麼來的？
I know. Then how did you come here?
小美
我昨天住在我阿姨家。
I stayed at my aunt's home yesterday.
阿豪
你阿姨家離這家餐廳很近嗎？
離 lí　近 jìn
Is your aunt's home close to this restaurant?
小美
對，很近，走路五分鐘就到了。
近 jìn　走路 zǒulù　分鐘 fēnzhōng
Yes, very close. It only takes five minutes on foot.
阿豪
原來是這樣。
原來 yuánlái　這樣 zhèyàng
Oh, I see. So that's how it is.
小美
你為了我的生日，坐了這麼久的車……
為了 wèile　久 jiǔ
You traveled for such a long time for my birthday...
阿豪
沒關係，生日快樂最重要。 🎂
重要 zhòngyào
It's okay. Saying happy birthday is the most important thing. 🎂

🧠 Grammar Patterns: 離 — Expressing Distance

1. Basic Distance Pattern

A + 離 + B + 很遠 / 很近

離 is used to express the distance between two places or things.

A is the place or person you are talking about. B is the reference point.

很遠 means "very far" and 很近 means "very close."

我家離淡水很遠。
離 lí　遠 yuǎn
My home is far from Tamsui.

2. Asking About Distance

A + 離 + B + 遠嗎？

Use this structure to ask whether one place is far from another.

You can also use 很近嗎？ when asking whether something is close.

你家離淡水遠嗎？
離 lí　遠 yuǎn
Is your home far from Tamsui?

3. Giving an Exact Distance

A + 離 + B + 有 + Number + 公里

Use this structure when you want to give a specific distance.

You can add 大概 when the number is approximate.

我家離淡水大概三十公里。
離 lí　大概 dàgài　公里 gōnglǐ
My home is about 30 kilometers from Tamsui.

4. Distance vs. Travel Time

A + 離 + B + 很遠，V + 要 + Time

離 describes physical distance.

要 + time describes how long the trip takes.

These two structures can be combined in one sentence.

我家離淡水很遠，坐車要兩個小時。
離 lí　遠 yuǎn　小時 xiǎoshí
My home is far from Tamsui, and it takes two hours by car.

5. Talking About a Nearby Place

A + 離 + B + 很近

Use 很近 when two places are close to each other.

You can add a walking time to make the sentence more specific.

我阿姨家離這家餐廳很近。
離 lí　近 jìn
My aunt's home is close to this restaurant.

6. Walking Time

走路 + Number + 分鐘

When describing walking time, use 走路 followed by the number of minutes.

走路五分鐘就到了。
走路 zǒulù　分鐘 fēnzhōng
It only takes five minutes on foot.
