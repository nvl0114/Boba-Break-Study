Basic Sentence Patterns

In Mandarin, a simple sentence often follows a basic pattern. Think of it like building a sentence with blocks.

🧱 What is S + V + O?

S = Subject — who does the action
V = Verb — the action
O = Object — what receives the action

我　吃　飯。
Wǒ　chī　fàn.
I eat rice / I have a meal.

So you can think: WHO + DOES WHAT + WHAT

Grammar Patterns

PATTERN 1
S + V + O
Make a simple positive sentence.
我吃飯。
Wǒ chī fàn.
I eat / I have a meal.
PATTERN 2
S + 不 + V + O
Put 不 before the verb to make a simple negative sentence.
我不吃飯。
Wǒ bù chī fàn.
I don't eat / I'm not eating.
PATTERN 3
S + V + O + 嗎？
Add 嗎 at the end to turn the sentence into a yes/no question.
你吃飯嗎？
Nǐ chī fàn ma?
Do you eat? / Have you eaten?
PATTERN 4
S + V + 不 + V？
Repeat the verb with 不 between them to ask an A-not-A question.
你吃不吃飯？
Nǐ chī bù chī fàn?
Do you eat / Are you going to eat?
