Grammar Pattern

S + V + O

This is one of the most basic sentence patterns in Mandarin.

S = Subject — who does the action
V = Verb — what action they do
O = Object — what receives the action

S
我
+
V
吃
+
O
飯
我 吃 飯 🔊
I eat a meal.
