Ahao's Birthday Mission

Today is Xiaomei's birthday. She invited the whole class to celebrate at "Haifeng Restaurant", a seaside restaurant in Tamsui. When Ahao went out to buy a gift, his phone suddenly ran out of battery — with no map, he has to rely on asking strangers and reading road signs to make his way to Tamsui step by step.

Use the directional buttons in the bottom-left corner to move Ahao (the buttons always correspond to the true map directions and will not change when the camera rotates).

When walking close to an important location, the INTERACT button will appear in the bottom-right corner. Tap it to continue the story.

Want to look around? Drag your finger across the screen to look 360° around the environment.
