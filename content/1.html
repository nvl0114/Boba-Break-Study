Mandarin Core Sentence Structures

Build simple Mandarin sentences step by step.

🧠 Grammar Patterns

1. Basic Sentence: S + V + O

Mandarin basic sentences follow the Subject + Verb + Object structure.

S
Subject
+
V
Verb
+
O
Object

S = Subject
Who is performing the action?

V = Verb
What action is being done?

O = Object
What receives the action?

我 吃 飯
Wǒ chī fàn.
I eat a meal.

2. Negative Sentence: S + 不 + V + O

To make a sentence negative, insert 不 (bù) right before the verb.

S
Subject
+
不
not
+
V
Verb
+
O
Object

不 (bù) is placed directly before the verb to turn the action into a negative statement (meaning "not" or "do not").

我 不 吃 飯
Wǒ bù chī fàn.
I don't eat a meal.

3. Question Particle: S + V + O + 嗎

Turn any statement into a "Yes/No" question by adding the question particle 嗎 (ma) at the very end.

Statement
S + V + O
+
嗎
ma?

The word order of the statement does not change. Simply add 嗎 (ma) to the end of the sentence to form a question.

你 吃 飯 嗎
Nǐ chī fàn ma?
Do you eat a meal?

4. Alternative Question: S + V + 不 + V

Ask a direct choice question using the Verb + 不 + Verb (A-not-A) pattern.

S
Subject
+
V
Verb
+
不
not
+
V
Verb

This asks whether someone does or does not do an action (e.g., "eat or not eat"). Note that the object usually comes after this structure if needed.

你 吃 不 吃
Nǐ chī bu chī?
Do you eat or not?

5. How to Answer Questions

Learn how to respond affirmatively or negatively to 嗎 (ma) and A-not-A questions.

Affirmative Answer (Yes): Repeat the Verb
To say "Yes", simply use the verb by itself (or repeat the core positive structure).

Negative Answer (No): Use 不 + Verb
To say "No", use 不 (bù) followed by the verb.

吃 / 不吃
Chī / Bù chī.
Eat (Yes) / Don't eat (No).
