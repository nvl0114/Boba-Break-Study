💡 Did you know? Mandarin has a magical tone-changing rule called Tone Sandhi!

The word 你好 (nǐ hǎo) is written with two third tones, but in natural everyday speech, it transforms into (ní hǎo) so your tongue won't trip! 🤭

Check out these other fun examples:
✨ 不去 (bù qù) → pronounced as (bú qù) = Not going
✨ 一個 (yī gè) → pronounced as (yí gè) = One (item)

Let's dive deeper into how this works in this lesson! 🚀
Tone sandhi rules might feel a bit tricky at first like swirling tapioca pearls, but don't worry—you don't need to memorize everything overnight! 🥤💖

Enjoy the journey, keep listening, and your ears will naturally get used to it. 慢慢來 (Màn màn lái) — take it nice and easy, just like sipping your favorite boba! 🌱✨
