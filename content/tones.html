Mandarin Tone Practice

Here is an explanation of the 4 tones in Mandarin Chinese. Please listen carefully.

Did you know that Mandarin Chinese has a special tone-changing rule called tone sandhi?

The word 你好 (nǐ hǎo) is written with two third tones, but in natural speech, it is pronounced as (ní hǎo) because the first third tone changes into a second tone.

Other examples:
不去 (bù qù) → (bú qù) = not going
一個 (yī gè) → (yí gè) = one (thing)

In this lesson, we will learn more about Mandarin tone sandhi and how to use it correctly.

Tone sandhi rules may seem confusing at first, but don’t worry, you don’t need to memorize everything right away. With time, as you keep learning and listening more, you’ll gradually develop an understanding of how tone sandhi works naturally. 慢慢來 — take it step by step.
