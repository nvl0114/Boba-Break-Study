🎥 Video Explanation: Taiwanese Family Traditions

Watch this video to understand how family relationship structures and traditional titles work in Chinese and Taiwanese culture.

Chinese family terms can be quite extensive, and if you’re not familiar with Chinese family traditions, they may feel a little confusing to memorize. One of the easiest ways to remember them is through real-life connections. If you have a Taiwanese or Chinese partner or friends, learning how they address their family members can make these terms much easier to understand and remember. The video is the explanation of how family members are addressed in Taiwanese tradition.
