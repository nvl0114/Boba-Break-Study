LESSON 11 • READING & SENTENCE STRUCTURE

Beyond Pinyin: Advanced Reading & Connections

Transitioning to direct Hanzi recognition and mastering essential linking structures.

📖 Lesson Article & Reading Guide

In many beginner Mandarin textbooks, pinyin is displayed throughout the entire book. Because of this, many learners don't realize that when they first start learning, their eyes and brain tend to focus more on the pinyin than on the Hanzi.

The only way to become familiar with reading Hanzi is to stop relying on pinyin and train your memory to recognize the characters themselves. Therefore, starting from now, I will no longer display pinyin for Hanzi that have appeared before. Pinyin will only be shown when a Hanzi has not appeared previously, so you can learn its pronunciation without becoming dependent on pinyin.

Reading Text

Pinyin indicators appear only on target new vocabulary items:

我叫小美。我有個哥哥和妹妹。我 gēn跟 哥哥 zhù zài住在 yīqǐ一起， 妹妹也住在 jiālǐ家裡。 我們都喜歡吃飯和看電影。哥哥還喜歡聽 chànggē唱歌， dànshì但是 妹妹不喜歡。妹妹喜歡 zìpāi自拍， kěshì可是 哥哥不喜歡。

jīntiān今天 是 xīngqíliù星期六， suǒyǐ所以 我們 dōu都 有 duō多 時間。 yīnwèi因為 tiānqì天氣 很好，我們想 chūqù出去 玩。 ránhòu然後 我們一起去公園。 rúguǒ如果 下雨，我們就回家。晚上我們可以吃 miàn麵 huòzhě或者 吃飯。你要吃麵 háishì還是 吃飯?

English Meaning: My name is Xiaomei. I have an older brother and a younger sister. I live together with my older brother, and my younger sister also lives at home. We both like eating meals and watching movies. My older brother also likes listening to singing, but my younger sister doesn't like it. My younger sister likes taking selfies, but my older brother doesn't like it. Today is Saturday, so we all have plenty of time. Because the weather is very good, we want to go out and play. Then we go to the park together. If it rains, we will go home. In the evening, we can eat noodles or rice. Do you want to eat noodles or rice?

🧠 Grammar Patterns: Detailed Breakdown

1. 和 (hé) — "and"

A + 和 + B

Explanation: 和 connects two nouns, people, or things.

我有哥哥和妹妹。
Wǒ yǒu gēge hé mèimei.
I have an older brother and a younger sister.

2. 跟 (gēn) — "with / and"

A + 跟 + B + Verb

Explanation: 跟 is commonly used when doing something along with another person.

我跟朋友吃飯。
Wǒ gēn péngyǒu chīfàn.
I eat with my friend.

3. 也 (yě) — "also / too"

Subject + 也 + Verb

Explanation: 也 usually comes right before the verb or adjective.

我也喜歡中文。
Wǒ yě xǐhuān zhōngwén.
I also like Chinese.

4. 都 (dōu) — "all / both"

Subject + 都 + Verb

Explanation: 都 means everyone/everything in the group does something.

我們都喜歡吃飯。
Wǒmen dōu xǐhuān chīfàn.
We all like eating.

5. 還 (hái) — "still / also"

S + 還 + Verb / Adj

Explanation: Indicates continuation or an additional action/trait.

我還在家。
Wǒ hái zài jiā.
I am still at home.

6. 還是 (háishì) — "or" in questions

A + 還是 + B？

Explanation: Used specifically for choosing between alternatives in questions.

你要喝茶還是咖啡？
Nǐ yào hē chá háishì kāfēi?
Do you want tea or coffee?

7. 或者 (huòzhě) — "or" in statements

A + 或者 + B

Explanation: Used for statements giving options or alternatives.

我晚上吃麵或者吃飯。
Wǒ wǎnshàng chī miàn huòzhě chīfàn.
I will eat noodles or rice tonight.

8. 但是 / 可是 (dànshì / kěshì) — "but"

Sentence A + 但是/可是 + Sentence B

Explanation: Conjunctions used to introduce a contrast or objection.

我喜歡吃麵，但是我不喜歡吃飯。
Wǒ xǐhuān chī miàn, dànshì wǒ bù xǐhuān chīfàn.
I like eating noodles, but I don't like eating rice.

9. 因為 (yīnwèi) & 所以 (suǒyǐ) — "because... so..."

因為 + Reason，所以 + Result

Explanation: Correlative conjunctions for cause and effect.

因為今天下雨，所以我很忙。
Yīnwèi jīntiān xiàyǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ hěn máng.
Because it's raining today, I am busy.

10. 然後 (ránhòu) — "then / afterwards"

A，然後 B。

Explanation: Connects sequential actions chronologically.

我先吃飯，然後看電視。
Wǒ xiān chīfàn, ránhòu kàn diànshì.
I eat first, then watch TV.

11. 如果 (rúguǒ) — "if"

如果 + Condition，(就) + Result

Explanation: Introduces a hypothetical condition, frequently paired with 就.

如果下雨，我就回家。
Rúguǒ xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù huí jiā.
If it rains, I will go home.
