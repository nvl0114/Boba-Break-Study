LESSON 11 • READING & SENTENCE STRUCTURE

Beyond Pinyin: Advanced Reading & Connections

Transitioning to direct Hanzi recognition and mastering essential linking and condition structures.

📖 Lesson Article & Reading Guide

In many beginner Mandarin textbooks, pinyin is displayed throughout the entire book. Because of this, many learners don't realize that when they first start learning, their eyes and brain tend to focus more on the pinyin than on the Hanzi.

The only way to become familiar with reading Hanzi is to stop relying on pinyin and train your memory to recognize the characters themselves. Therefore, starting from now, I will no longer display pinyin for Hanzi that have appeared before. Pinyin will only be shown when a Hanzi has not appeared previously, so you can learn its pronunciation without becoming dependent on pinyin.

Reading Text

Pinyin is shown below characters where appropriate:

wǒ我 jiào叫 xiǎoměi小美。 wǒ我 yǒu有 yī一 gè個 gēge哥哥 hé和 yī一 gè個 mèimei妹妹。 wǒ我 gēn跟 gēge哥哥 zhù住 zài在 yīqǐ一起， mèimei妹妹 yě也 zhù住 zài在 jiālǐ家裡。 wǒmen我們 dōu都 xǐhuān喜歡 chīfàn吃飯 hé和 kàn看 diànyǐng電影。 gēge哥哥 hái還 xǐhuān喜歡 tīng聽 chànggē唱歌， dànshì但是 mèimei妹妹 bù不 xǐhuān喜歡。 mèimei妹妹 xǐhuān喜歡 kàn看 zìpāi自拍， kěshì可是 gēge哥哥 bù不 xǐhuān喜歡。

jīntiān今天 shì是 xīngqíliù星期六， suǒyǐ所以 wǒmen我們 dōu都 yǒu有 duō多 shíjiān時間。 yīnwèi因為 tiānqì天氣 hěn很 hǎo好， wǒmen我們 xiǎng想 chūqù出去 wán玩。 ránhòu然後 wǒmen我們 yīqǐ一起 qù去 gōngyuán公園。 rúguǒ如果 xià yǔ下雨， wǒmen我們 jiù就 huí jiā回家。 wǎnshàng晚上 wǒmen我們 kěyǐ可以 chī吃 miàn麵， huòzhě或者 chīfàn吃飯。 nǐ你 yào要 chī吃 miàn麵 háishì還是 chīfàn吃飯?

English Meaning: My name is Xiaomei. I have an older brother and a younger sister. I live together with my older brother, and my younger sister also lives at home. We both like eating meals and watching movies. My older brother also likes listening to singing, but my younger sister doesn't like it. My younger sister likes looking at selfies, but my older brother doesn't like it. Today is Saturday, so we all have plenty of time. Because the weather is very good, we want to go out and play. Then we go to the park together. If it rains, we will go home. In the evening, we can eat noodles or rice. Do you want to eat noodles or rice?

🧠 Grammar Patterns: Detailed Breakdown

1. 和 (hé) — "and"

A + 和 + B

Explanation:的和 connects two nouns, people, or things.

我有哥哥和妹妹。
Wǒ yǒu gēge hé mèimei.
I have an older brother and a younger sister.

2. 跟 (gēn) — "with / and"

A + 跟 + B + Verb

Explanation: 跟 is commonly used when someone does something with another person.

我跟朋友吃飯。
Wǒ gēn péngyǒu chīfàn.
I eat with my friend.

3. 也 (yě) — "also / too"

Subject + 也 + Verb

Explanation: 也 usually comes before the verb or adjective.

我也喜歡中文。
Wǒ yě xǐhuān zhōngwén.
I also like Chinese.

4. 都 (dōu) — "all / both"

Subject + 都 + Verb

Explanation: 都 means everyone/everything in the group does something. For two people, it can mean both.

我們都喜歡吃飯。
Wǒmen dōu xǐhuān chīfàn.
We all like eating.

5. 還 (hái) — "still / also"

S + 還 + Verb / Adj

Explanation: Has two common meanings: "still" or "also / additionally".

我還在家。
Wǒ hái zài jiā.
I am still at home.

6. 還是 (háishì) — "or" in questions

A + 還是 + B？

Explanation: Use 還是 when asking someone to choose between options in questions.

你要喝茶還是咖啡？
Nǐ yào hē chá háishì kāfēi?
Do you want tea or coffee?

7. 或者 (huòzhě) — "or" in statements

A + 或者 + B

Explanation: 或者 is commonly used for "or" in statements.

我晚上吃麵或者吃飯。
Wǒ wǎnshàng chī miàn huòzhě chīfàn.
I will eat noodles or rice tonight.

8. 但是 / 可是 (dànshì / kěshì) — "but"

Sentence A + 但是/可是 + Sentence B

Explanation: Both mean "but / however" to show contrast.

我喜歡吃麵，但是我不喜歡吃飯。
Wǒ xǐhuān chī miàn, dànshì wǒ bù xǐhuān chīfàn.
I like eating noodles, but I don't like eating rice.

9. 因為 (yīnwèi) & 所以 (suǒyǐ) — "because... so..."

因為 + Reason，所以 + Result

Explanation: 因為 introduces a reason, and 所以 introduces the result.

因為今天下雨，所以我很忙。
Yīnwèi jīntiān xiàyǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ hěn máng.
Because it's raining today, so I am busy.

10. 然後 (ránhòu) — "then / and then"

A，然後 B。

Explanation: 然後 shows the next action or sequence.

我先吃飯，然後看電視。
Wǒ xiān chīfàn, ránhòu kàn diànshì.
I eat first, then watch TV.

11. 如果 (rúguǒ) — "if"

如果 + Condition，(就) + Result

Explanation: 如果 introduces a condition, often paired with 就.

如果下雨，我就回家。
Rúguǒ xiàyǔ, wǒ jiù huí jiā.
If it rains, I will go home.
