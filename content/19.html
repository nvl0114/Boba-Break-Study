My Daily Routine

Learn how to talk about your day using 得, 一點, 從……到, 應該, 先, 完, 覺得, 比, 最, and 太……了.

📖 Reading: My Day

This short journal entry follows one student's morning, school day, and evening. Pinyin is shown only for this lesson's target vocabulary.

Reading Text

我每天早上七點 qǐchuáng 起床 。 我 qǐ 起 de 得 有 yìdiǎn 一點 wǎn 晚 ， 因為昨天晚上 shuì 睡 de 得 很 wǎn 晚 。

我 cóng 從 家裡到學校要 zǒu 走 十 fēnzhōng 分鐘 。 學校離我家不遠，我很喜歡 zǒulù 走路 去學校，因為可以看看 fēngjǐng 風景 。

到了學校以後，我 yīnggāi 應該 xiān 先 去圖書館，可是我還沒做 wán 完 gōngkè 功課 ， 所以我 xiān 先 做 gōngkè 功課 。

我 juéde 覺得 shùxué 數學 bǐ 比 英文難 yìdiǎn 一點 ， 可是我 zuì 最 喜歡的 kēmù 科目 還是 shùxué 數學 ， 因為老師教 de 得 很 qīngchǔ 清楚 。

晚上我要跟朋友一起吃飯。我們吃 de 得 tài 太 多了， dùzi 肚子 很不 shūfú 舒服 ！
English Meaning:
Every morning I wake up at 7. I wake up a bit late, because last night I slept very late.

It takes 10 minutes to walk from my house to school. School isn't far from my house — I like walking to school because I can see the scenery.

After arriving at school, I should go to the library first, but I haven't finished my homework yet, so I do homework first.

I think math is a bit harder than English, but my favorite subject is still math, because the teacher explains very clearly.

In the evening I'm going with friends to eat together. We ate too much — my stomach feels really uncomfortable!

🧠 Grammar Patterns

1. 得 (de) — Explaining How an Action is Performed

Verb + 得 + Adjective

The word 得 (de) is placed after a verb to describe how an activity is carried out (e.g., slowly, clearly, etc.).

我睡得很晚。
wǒ shuì de hěn wǎn
I slept very late.

老師教得很清楚。
lǎoshī jiāo de hěn qīngchǔ
The teacher teaches very clearly.

2. 一點 (yìdiǎn) — A Little / Slightly

Adjective + 一點

一點 (yìdiǎn) means "a little" or "slightly". It is placed after an adjective to soften or qualify its degree (e.g., a bit late, a bit hard).

我起得有一點晚。
wǒ qǐ de yǒu yìdiǎn wǎn
I woke up a bit late.

3. 從……到 (cóng...dào) — From... To...

從 + Place/Time A + 到 + Place/Time B

Used to indicate distance from one place to another, or a time range from one point to another.

從家裡到學校要走十分鐘。
cóng jiālǐ dào xuéxiào yào zǒu shí fēnzhōng
It takes 10 minutes to walk from home to school.

4. 分鐘 / 小時 / 秒 — Time Units

Number + 分鐘 / 小時 / 秒

Expresses duration: 分鐘 (fēnzhōng) = minutes, 小時 (xiǎoshí) = hours, and 秒 (miǎo) = seconds.

要走十分鐘。
yào zǒu shí fēnzhōng
It takes ten minutes to walk.

從這裡到圖書館要一小時。
cóng zhèli dào túshūguǎn yào yì xiǎoshí
It takes one hour from here to the library.

5. 應該 (yīnggāi) — Should

Subject + 應該 + Verb

Used to express obligation or what ought to be done because it is the right course of action.

我應該先去圖書館。
wǒ yīnggāi xiān qù túshūguǎn
I should go to the library first.

6. 先 (xiān) — First / Ahead of Time

Subject + 先 + Verb

Indicates an activity that is done first before anything else.

我先做功課。
wǒ xiān zuò gōngkè
I do homework first.

7. 完 (wán) — Finished / Completed

Verb + 完

Placed after a verb to indicate that an action or task has been fully completed.

我還沒做完功課。
wǒ hái méi zuò wán gōngkè
I haven't finished my homework yet.

8. 覺得 (juéde) — To Feel / To Think

Subject + 覺得 + Opinion

Used to express personal opinions, thoughts, or feelings about something.

我覺得數學比英文難一點。
wǒ juéde shùxué bǐ yīngwén nán yìdiǎn
I think math is a bit harder than English.

9. 比 (bǐ) — Direct Comparison (More... than...)

A + 比 + B + Adjective (+ 一點)

比 (bǐ) is used exclusively to compare two separate entities directly, meaning Object A is more [adjective] than Object B. You can optionally attach 一點 at the end to mean "a bit more [adjective]".

數學比英文難一點。
shùxué bǐ yīngwén nán yìdiǎn
Math is a bit harder than English.

10. 最 (zuì) — Most / Superlative

最 + Adjective / Verb

Superlative marker indicating the highest degree ("most...").

我最喜歡的科目是數學。
wǒ zuì xǐhuan de kēmù shì shùxué
My favorite (most-liked) subject is math.

11. 太……了 (tài...le) — Too...

太 + Adjective + 了

Indicates an excessive or overly high degree ("too much / too...").

我們吃得太多了。
wǒmen chī de tài duō le
We ate too much.
