的

Possession & Describing Nouns

的 (de) is a little grammar word that helps connect a person, thing, or description to a noun. Think of it as a little bridge 🌉 between two words.

🧋 Meet the Little Connector: 的!

Don't overthink it! When you see 的, the word or phrase before it usually tells you whose something is or what kind of thing it is.

A + 的 + B
👤 → 🔗 → 📦
1

的 = “my / your / his / her”

The easiest use of 的 is showing possession. It tells us who something belongs to.

Basic Pattern
Person + 的 + Noun
My
我的朋友
Wǒ de péngyǒu
My friend
Your
你的家
Nǐ de jiā
Your home
His
他的爸爸
Tā de bàba
His dad
Her
她的媽媽
Tā de māma
Her mom
💡 Easy trick:

我的 = my
你的 = your
他的 = his
她的 = her

Just put 的 between the person and the thing!
2

的 = “'s”

的 can also work like 's in English. It connects a person with something that belongs to or is related to that person.

Possession
Person + 的 + Thing
Example
媽媽的電腦
Māma de diànnǎo
Mom's computer
Example
我朋友的家
Wǒ péngyǒu de jiā
My friend's home
Example
老師的電腦
Lǎoshī de diànnǎo
The teacher's computer
Example
王老師的學生
Wáng lǎoshī de xuéshēng
Teacher Wang's student
🧋 English: Mom's computer

🧋 Mandarin: 媽媽的電腦

Think: Mom → 的 → computer 💻
3

的 = “description → noun”

的 is not only for possession! It can also connect a description to a noun.

Description Pattern
Adjective + 的 + Noun
Cute
可愛的孩子
Kě'ài de háizi
A cute child
Pretty
漂亮的女生
Piàoliang de nǚshēng
A pretty girl
Interesting
有趣的老師
Yǒuqù de lǎoshī
An interesting teacher
Good
好的朋友
Hǎo de péngyǒu
A good friend
✨ Think of 的 as a bridge:

可愛 → 🌉 的 → 孩子

可愛的孩子 = cute child
4

Noun + 的 + Noun

The word before 的 does not have to be a person. A place, company, school, or country can also connect to a noun.

Relationship
Noun + 的 + Noun
School
學校的老師
Xuéxiào de lǎoshī
The school's teacher / a teacher at the school
Company
公司的電腦
Gōngsī de diànnǎo
The company's computer
Taiwan
台灣的夏天
Táiwān de xiàtiān
Taiwan's summer
Bank
銀行的工作
Yínháng de gōngzuò
Bank work / work at a bank
5

Sometimes 的 Can Disappear 👻

With close family relationships, Mandarin often drops 的 in everyday speech. Don't worry about this too much at first!

✅ With 的

我的媽媽

Wǒ de māma

My mom

✅ Without 的

我媽媽

Wǒ māma

My mom

Natural
我爸爸
Wǒ bàba
My dad
Natural
我媽媽
Wǒ māma
My mom
Natural
我哥哥
Wǒ gēge
My older brother
Natural
我姐姐
Wǒ jiějie
My older sister
💡 Beginner rule:

If you're unsure, just use 的.

我的媽媽 ✅
我的朋友 ✅

You can learn the shorter everyday forms later. 😊
6

的 vs. 很

These two look very different in a sentence. 很 describes the subject. 的 helps describe a noun.

✨ 很 + Adjective

我很忙。

Wǒ hěn máng.

I am busy.

🌉 Adjective + 的 + Noun

忙的學生

Máng de xuéshēng

A busy student

🧋 Super simple:

我很忙。 → I am busy.

忙的學生 → a busy student

很 = describes someone/something
的 = connects a description → noun
7

的 vs. 是

Here's the easiest way to tell them apart: 是 connects a subject to an identity. 的 connects a modifier to a noun.

👤 是 → Identity

我是學生。

Wǒ shì xuéshēng.

I am a student.

🌉 的 → Connector

我的學生

Wǒ de xuéshēng

My student

💡 Remember:

是 → “Who / What?” 👤
我是學生。
I am a student.

的 → “Whose / Which kind?” 🌉
我的學生。
My student.
8

的 Can Also Mean “Mine / Yours”

Sometimes the noun after 的 is obvious, so Mandarin doesn't need to say it again. 的 can stay at the end.

Possessive Pronoun
Pronoun + 的
Mine
這是我的。
Zhè shì wǒ de.
This is mine.
Yours
哪一個是你的？
Nǎ yí ge shì nǐ de?
Which one is yours?
💡 The noun is already understood!

這是我的（書）。
This is mine (book).

You don't need to repeat the noun.
9

Common Mistakes 🚨

These are easy mistakes beginners often make. Let's keep them simple!

❌ Don't do this

我的很朋友

Don't put 很 between 我 and 的.

✅ Do this

我的好朋友

My good friend.

❌ Wrong for “I am a friend”

我的是朋友

✅ Correct

我是朋友。

I am a friend.

❌ Missing 的

可愛孩子

✅ Beginner-friendly

可愛的孩子

A cute child.
