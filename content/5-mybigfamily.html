Explanation

👴👵 Grandparents

In Taiwan, grandparents are commonly called:

阿公 (āgōng) = grandpa
阿嬤 (āmā) = grandma

👨 Dad's side — 爸爸這邊

Dad's brothers:

Older brother → 伯伯 (bóbo)
Younger brother → 叔叔 (shūshu)

Dad's sister:

Older or younger → 姑姑 (gūgu)

Their children: Children of 伯伯 / 叔叔 → 堂 (táng)

Older male cousin → 堂哥
Younger male cousin → 堂弟
Older female cousin → 堂姐
Younger female cousin → 堂妹

Children of 姑姑 → 表 (biǎo)

Older male cousin → 表哥
Younger male cousin → 表弟
Older female cousin → 表姐
Younger female cousin → 表妹

👩 Mom's side — 媽媽這邊

Mom's siblings:

Older or younger brother → 舅舅 (jiùjiu)
Older or younger sister → 阿姨 (āyí)

Unlike Dad's side, you don't need a different word for older/younger.

Their children → 表 (biǎo)

Older male cousin → 表哥
Younger male cousin → 表弟
Older female cousin → 表姐
Younger female cousin → 表妹

🧠 Super Easy Rule

Dad's brother child → 堂
Dad's sister child → 表
Mom's brother/sister child → 表

And for 伯伯 / 叔叔 / 姑姑 / 舅舅 / 阿姨:

Dad's family: older/younger matters for brothers
Mom's family: older/younger doesn't change the basic term.
