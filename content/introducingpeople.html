Grammar & Sentence Patterns

1. Surname Usage (Subject + 姓 + Name)

Use 姓 (xìng) to ask or state a family name.

我姓王。 (Wǒ xìng Wáng.) — My family name is Wang.
2. Possession (A + 的 + B)

The particle 的 (de) indicates ownership.

她是我的朋友。 (Tā shì wǒ de péngyǒu.) — She is my friend.
3. Yes/No Questions (Statement + 嗎)

Add 嗎 (ma) at the end of a sentence to ask a yes/no question.

他是你的朋友嗎？ (Tā shì nǐ de péngyǒu ma?) — Is he your friend?
4. The Verb 是 (Shì - To Be)

Connects a subject to a noun. Structure: Subject + 是 + Object.

我們是朋友。 (Wǒmen shì péngyǒu.) — We are friends.
