Lesson 2 — Introducing Other People

林先生 (Lin Xiansheng):
請問，你姓什麼？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐ xìng shénme?) Excuse me, what is your family name?
王先生 (Wang Xiansheng):
我姓王。你呢？ (Wǒ xìng Wáng. Nǐ ne?) My family name is Wang. How about you?
林先生 (Lin Xiansheng):
我姓林。 (Wǒ xìng Lín.) My family name is Lin.
王先生 (Wang Xiansheng):
她是你的朋友嗎？ (Tā shì nǐ de péngyǒu ma?) Is she your friend?
林先生 (Lin Xiansheng):
對, 她是我的朋友。 (Duì, tā shì wǒ de péngyǒu.) Yes, she is my friend.
王先生 (Wang Xiansheng):
他也是你的朋友嗎？ (Tā yě shì nǐ de péngyǒu ma?) Is he also your friend?
林先生 (Lin Xiansheng):
對, 他也是我的朋友。 (Duì, tā yě shì wǒ de péngyǒu.) Yes, he is also my friend.
王先生 (Wang Xiansheng):
不好意思, 我走了。 (Bù hǎoyìsi, wǒ zǒu le.) Excuse me, I'm leaving.
林先生 (Lin Xiansheng):
沒問題, 再見！ (Méi wèntí, zàijiàn!) No problem. Goodbye!
王先生 (Wang Xiansheng):
再見！ (Zàijiàn!) Goodbye!

Sentence Pattern
Key Structures
你姓什麼？
Nǐ xìng shénme?
What is your family name?
她是你的朋友嗎？
Tā shì nǐ de péngyǒu ma?
Is she your friend?
Mandarin uses structural particles like 的 for possession and 嗎 for questions.

Grammar & Notes

1. 姓 + Family Name

Used to state or ask someone's family name.

Wǒ xìng Wáng. (My surname is Wang)

2. A 的 B (Possession)

Shows possession (A's B).

Wǒ de péngyǒu (My friend)

3. Questions with 嗎 (ma)

Place at the end of a statement for Yes/No questions.

Tā shì nǐ de péngyǒu ma? (Is she your friend?)

4. Polite Expressions

Common phrases for courtesy.

Qǐngwèn / Bù hǎoyìsi / Zàijiàn

5. The Verb 是 (shì)

Functions like "to be" (am / is / are).

Tā shì wǒ de péngyǒu. (She is my friend)

6. Subject + Verb + Object

Basic sentence structure.

Wǒmen shì péngyǒu. (We are friends)

⭐ Key Patterns to Remember

📌 你姓什麼？ → What is your family name? 📌 A + 的 + B → A's B (e.g., 我的朋友) 📌 Statement + 嗎？ → Yes/No Question 📌 Subject + 是 + Noun → To be (am/is/are)
