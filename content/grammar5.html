有 / 沒有

Have, Exist, and Actions That Happened

有 (yǒu) is one of the most useful verbs in Mandarin. It can mean have, there is / there are, and can also appear before a verb to show that an action or event happened.

🧋 Meet 有 & 沒有!

Think of 有 as the little word that says something exists, is possessed, or happened. Its negative form is 沒有.

有 = have / exist / happened
沒有 = don't have / doesn't exist / didn't happen
1

有 / 沒有 = Have / Has

The first and easiest use of 有 is talking about possession: someone has something.

Positive
Subject + 有 + Noun
Have
我有哥哥。
Wǒ yǒu gēge.
I have an older brother.
Have
我有一個孩子。
Wǒ yǒu yí ge háizi.
I have a child.
Has
她有一台電腦。
Tā yǒu yì tái diànnǎo.
She has a computer.
Negative
Subject + 沒有 + Noun
Don't have
我沒有哥哥。
Wǒ méiyǒu gēge.
I don't have an older brother.
Don't have
我沒有車。
Wǒ méiyǒu chē.
I don't have a car.
📌 Important: 不有 is incorrect.

有 is special. We normally don't make it negative with 不.

❌ 我不有哥哥。
Wǒ bù yǒu gēge.

✅ 我沒有哥哥。
Wǒ méiyǒu gēge.

⭐ Remember:

有 → 沒有
have → don't have

Not:
❌ 有 → 不有
2

Questions: 有嗎？ & 有沒有？

There are two very common ways to ask whether someone has something: 有嗎？ and 有沒有？. Both are natural.

With 嗎
Subject + 有 + Noun + 嗎？
Question
你有哥哥嗎？
Nǐ yǒu gēge ma?
Do you have an older brother?
Question
你有車嗎？
Nǐ yǒu chē ma?
Do you have a car?
A-not-A
Subject + 有沒有 + Noun？
Question
你有沒有哥哥？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu gēge?
Do you have an older brother?
Question
你有沒有車？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu chē?
Do you have a car?
🧋 Easy!

有嗎？ → Have...?

有沒有？ → Have or not have?

Both ask basically the same thing.
3

How to Answer

The nice thing about 有 questions is that the answers are super simple.

✅ YES

有。

Yǒu.

Yes, I do.


有，我有一個哥哥。

Yes, I have an older brother.

❌ NO

沒有。

Méiyǒu.

No, I don't.


沒有，我沒有哥哥。

No, I don't have an older brother.

⭐ Quick Rule:

有嗎？ → 有 / 沒有

有沒有？ → 有 / 沒有

You don't need a complicated answer!
4

有 / 沒有 = There Is / There Are

有 can also mean that something exists or is present in a place. In this use, the place usually comes first.

Positive
Place + 有 + Noun
There are
學校有很多學生。
Xuéxiào yǒu hěn duō xuéshēng.
There are many students at the school.
There is
房間裡有一張桌子。
Fángjiān lǐ yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi.
There is a table in the room.
There is
冰箱裡有牛奶。
Bīngxiāng lǐ yǒu niúnǎi.
There is milk in the refrigerator.
Negative
Place + 沒有 + Noun
There aren't
學校沒有學生。
Xuéxiào méiyǒu xuéshēng.
There are no students at the school.
There isn't
房間裡沒有桌子。
Fángjiān lǐ méiyǒu zhuōzi.
There isn't a table in the room.
5

Asking About Existence

Just like possession, you can use both 有嗎？ and 有沒有？ when asking whether something exists or is present.

With 嗎
Place + 有 + Noun + 嗎？
Question
學校有學生嗎？
Xuéxiào yǒu xuéshēng ma?
Are there students at the school?
Question
房間裡有桌子嗎？
Fángjiān lǐ yǒu zhuōzi ma?
Is there a table in the room?
A-not-A
Place + 有沒有 + Noun？
Question
學校有沒有學生？
Xuéxiào yǒu méiyǒu xuéshēng?
Are there students at the school?
Question
房間裡有沒有桌子？
Fángjiān lǐ yǒu méiyǒu zhuōzi?
Is there a table in the room?

✅ YES

有。

Yǒu.

Yes, there is / there are.


有，學校有很多學生。

Yes, there are many students at the school.

❌ NO

沒有。

Méiyǒu.

No, there isn't / there aren't.


沒有，學校沒有學生。

No, there aren't any students at the school.

6

有 + Verb = An Action Happened

Here's the special one! 😎 Sometimes 有 appears before a verb. In this pattern, 有 does not literally mean “have”. It emphasizes that an action or event did happen.

Action / Event
Subject + 有 + Verb (+ Object)
Happened
我有吃早餐。
Wǒ yǒu chī zǎocān.
I did eat breakfast.
Happened
他有去台北。
Tā yǒu qù Táiběi.
He did go to Taipei.
Happened
我有看這部電影。
Wǒ yǒu kàn zhè bù diànyǐng.
I did watch this movie.
Recently
你最近有認識新的朋友。
Nǐ zuìjìn yǒu rènshi xīn de péngyǒu.
You have met new friends recently.
📌 Important:

Don't think of 有 + Verb as a direct translation of the English present perfect.

The important idea is:

有 + Verb → emphasizes that the action/event happened.

Depending on the context, English might use did, have, or another natural translation.
7

沒有 + Verb = The Action Didn't Happen

Just like 有 + Verb shows that an action happened, 沒有 + Verb shows that it did not happen or has not happened.

Negative Action
Subject + 沒有 + Verb (+ Object)
Didn't
我沒有吃早餐。
Wǒ méiyǒu chī zǎocān.
I didn't eat breakfast.
Didn't
他沒有去台北。
Tā méiyǒu qù Táiběi.
He didn't go to Taipei.
Haven't
我沒有看這部電影。
Wǒ méiyǒu kàn zhè bù diànyǐng.
I haven't watched this movie.
Haven't
我最近沒有認識新的朋友。
Wǒ zuìjìn méiyǒu rènshi xīn de péngyǒu.
I haven't met any new friends recently.
⭐ Think of it like this:

有 + Verb → The action happened.

沒有 + Verb → The action did not happen.
8

Asking About an Action

You can also use 有嗎？ or 有沒有？ to ask whether an action happened.

With 嗎
Subject + 有 + Verb + Object + 嗎？
Question
你有吃早餐嗎？
Nǐ yǒu chī zǎocān ma?
Have you eaten breakfast?
Question
你有看這部電影嗎？
Nǐ yǒu kàn zhè bù diànyǐng ma?
Have you watched this movie?
Question
你最近有認識新的朋友嗎？
Nǐ zuìjìn yǒu rènshi xīn de péngyǒu ma?
Have you met new friends recently?
A-not-A
Subject + 有沒有 + Verb + Object？
Question
你有沒有吃早餐？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu chī zǎocān?
Have you eaten breakfast?
Question
你有沒有看這部電影？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu kàn zhè bù diànyǐng?
Have you watched this movie?
Question
你最近有沒有認識新的朋友？
Nǐ zuìjìn yǒu méiyǒu rènshi xīn de péngyǒu?
Have you met new friends recently?
9

How to Answer Action Questions

Again, the answer can be wonderfully short: 有 or 沒有.

✅ YES

有。

Yǒu.

Yes.


有，我有吃早餐。

Yes, I have eaten breakfast.

❌ NO

沒有。

Méiyǒu.

No.


沒有，我沒有吃早餐。

No, I haven't eaten breakfast.

🧋 Quick Rule:

有 + Verb + 嗎？ → 有 / 沒有

有沒有 + Verb？ → 有 / 沒有
10

有 + Number + Measure Word

有 can also tell us how many things someone has. Put the number and measure word after 有.

Quantity
Subject + 有 + Number + Measure Word + Noun
Three
我有三個朋友。
Wǒ yǒu sān ge péngyǒu.
I have three friends.
Two
她有兩個孩子。
Tā yǒu liǎng ge háizi.
She has two children.
One
我有一台電腦。
Wǒ yǒu yì tái diànnǎo.
I have one computer.
Asking How Many
Subject + 有 + 幾 + Measure Word + Noun？
How many?
你有幾個朋友？
Nǐ yǒu jǐ ge péngyǒu?
How many friends do you have?
How many?
你有幾個兄弟姊妹？
Nǐ yǒu jǐ ge xiōngdì jiěmèi?
How many siblings do you have?
⭐ Answer with the same pattern:

你有幾個朋友？
→ 我有三個朋友。

你有幾個兄弟姊妹？
→ 我有兩個兄弟姊妹。
✓

有 Has 3 Big Jobs 🧋

Don't memorize a hundred rules. Just remember these three big ideas.

👤 1. 有 + Noun → HAVE

我有哥哥。
I have an older brother.
📍 2. Place + 有 + Noun → THERE IS / THERE ARE

學校有學生。
There are students at the school.
🏃 3. 有 + Verb → ACTION HAPPENED

我有吃早餐。
I did eat breakfast.
🧋 And the negative?

It's always:

有 → 沒有
have → don't have
exist → doesn't exist
happened → didn't happen

❌ Not 不有.
