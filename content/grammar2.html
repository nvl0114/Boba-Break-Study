在

Location & Ongoing Actions

在 (zài) is mainly used to talk about location and actions that are currently happening.

🧋 One Word, Two Important Uses

Think of 在 as a useful building block. It can tell you where someone is, or show that someone is doing something right now.

在 · zài
1

Location — “be at / in”

Use 在 before a place to say where someone or something is located.

Positive
S + 在 + Place
Example
我在家。
Wǒ zài jiā.
I am at home.
Example
他在學校。
Tā zài xuéxiào.
He is at school.
Negative · 不在
S + 不在 + Place

To say that someone is not at a place, use 不在.

Negative
我不在家。
Wǒ bú zài jiā.
I am not at home.
Negative
他不在學校。
Tā bú zài xuéxiào.
He is not at school.
Yes / No Question · 嗎
S + 在 + Place + 嗎？

Add 嗎 (ma) at the end to ask whether someone is at a particular place.

Question
你在家嗎？
Nǐ zài jiā ma?
Are you at home?
Question
他在學校嗎？
Tā zài xuéxiào ma?
Is he at school?
Short Answers
在 / 不在

When answering a 在 + Place + 嗎？ question, you can use 在 or 不在.

Yes
在。
Zài.
Yes, I am / Yes, he is.
No
不在。
Bú zài.
No, I am not / No, he is not.

A-not-A Question

You can also ask a yes/no question by repeating 在 with 不.

S + 在 + 不在 + Place？
Example
你在不在家？
Nǐ zài bú zài jiā?
Are you at home or not?
2

Ongoing Action — 在 + Verb

在 can also be placed before a verb to show that an action is currently happening.

Ongoing Action
S + 在 + Verb

In this pattern, 在 does not mean “at/in.” Instead, it indicates that the action is in progress.

Example
我在吃飯。
Wǒ zài chīfàn.
I am eating.
Example
她在讀書。
Tā zài dúshū.
She is studying.
Example
他在工作。
Tā zài gōngzuò.
He is working.
Example
我在學中文。
Wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén.
I am studying Chinese.
Negative
S + 沒有在 + Verb

To say that someone is not currently doing something, 沒有在 can be used.

Negative
我沒有在吃飯。
Wǒ méiyǒu zài chīfàn.
I am not eating.
Negative
他沒有在工作。
Tā méiyǒu zài gōngzuò.
He is not working.
Yes / No Question
S + 有沒有在 + Verb？

有沒有在 + Verb can be used to ask whether someone is currently doing an action.

Question
你有沒有在吃飯？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu zài chīfàn?
Are you eating?
Question
他有沒有在工作？
Tā yǒu méiyǒu zài gōngzuò?
Is he working?
💡 Natural everyday speech: In conversation, Chinese speakers often use 沒在 + Verb as a shorter form.

我沒在吃飯。
I am not eating.

他沒在工作。
He is not working.
3

正在 — “currently doing”

正在 (zhèngzài) is used before a verb to emphasize that an action is currently in progress.

Ongoing Action
S + 正在 + Verb

正在 is similar to “am/is/are currently doing” in English. It puts stronger focus on the fact that the action is happening right now.

Example
我正在吃飯。
Wǒ zhèngzài chīfàn.
I am currently eating.
Example
他正在讀書。
Tā zhèngzài dúshū.
He is currently studying.
Example
她正在工作。
Tā zhèngzài gōngzuò.
She is currently working.
Example
我正在學中文。
Wǒ zhèngzài xué Zhōngwén.
I am currently studying Chinese.
在 vs 正在

Both 在 + Verb and 正在 + Verb can describe an action that is currently happening.

我在吃飯。
Wǒ zài chīfàn.
I am eating.

我正在吃飯。
Wǒ zhèngzài chīfàn.
I am currently eating / I am eating right now.

The basic meaning is very similar. 正在 gives stronger emphasis to the action being in progress right now.
Yes / No Question · 嗎
S + 正在 + Verb + 嗎？

Add 嗎 (ma) at the end to ask whether someone is currently doing something.

Question
你正在吃飯嗎？
Nǐ zhèngzài chīfàn ma?
Are you eating right now?
Question
他正在工作嗎？
Tā zhèngzài gōngzuò ma?
Is he working right now?
💡 Important: 正在 is used for an ongoing action, not for location.

❌ 我正在家。
✅ 我在家。
I am at home.

❌ 他正在學校。
✅ 他在學校。
He is at school.
4

在 vs 正在

These two forms are closely related, but 在 has more than one use. 正在 is specifically used to emphasize an ongoing action.

在 + Place

我在家。

I am at home.

Location

在 + Verb

我在吃飯。

I am eating.

Ongoing action

正在 + Verb

我正在吃飯。

I am eating right now.

Action in progress

正在 ≠ Location

❌ 我正在家。

✅ 我在家。

Use 在 for location.

Easy Rule:

在 + Place → where someone/something is

在 + Verb → an action is happening

正在 + Verb → emphasize that the action is happening right now
5

Quick Guide

Keep these patterns in mind when using 在 and 正在.

① Location

S + 在 + Place

我在台灣。
I am in Taiwan.
② Negative Location

S + 不在 + Place

我不在家。
I am not at home.
③ Location Question

S + 在 + Place + 嗎？

你在學校嗎？
Are you at school?
④ Ongoing Action

S + 在 + Verb

我在吃飯。
I am eating.
⑤ Negative Ongoing Action

S + 沒有在 + Verb

我沒有在吃飯。
I am not eating.
⑥ Ongoing Action Question

S + 有沒有在 + Verb？

你有沒有在吃飯？
Are you eating?
⑦ Emphasized Ongoing Action

S + 正在 + Verb

我正在吃飯。
I am eating right now.
⑧ 正在 Question

S + 正在 + Verb + 嗎？

你正在工作嗎？
Are you working right now?
💡 Remember:

在 + Place = location
在 + Verb = ongoing action
正在 + Verb = ongoing action with stronger emphasis on “right now”
