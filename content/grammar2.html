在

Location & Ongoing Actions

在 (zài) is mainly used to talk about location and actions that are currently happening.

🧋 One Word, Two Important Uses

Think of 在 as a useful building block. It can tell you where someone is, or show that someone is doing something right now.

在 · zài
1

Location — “be at / in”

Use 在 before a place to say where someone or something is located.

Positive
S + 在 + Place
Example
我在家。
Wǒ zài jiā.
I am at home.
Example
他在學校。
Tā zài xuéxiào.
He is at school.
Negative · 不在
S + 不在 + Place

To say that someone is not at a place, use 不在.

Negative
我不在家。
Wǒ bú zài jiā.
I am not at home.
Negative
他不在學校。
Tā bú zài xuéxiào.
He is not at school.
Yes / No Question · 嗎
S + 在 + Place + 嗎？

Add 嗎 (ma) at the end to ask whether someone is at a particular place.

Question
你在家嗎？
Nǐ zài jiā ma?
Are you at home?
Question
他在學校嗎？
Tā zài xuéxiào ma?
Is he at school?
Short Answers
在 = Yes   |   不在 = No
Yes
在，我在家。
Zài, wǒ zài jiā.
Yes, I am.
No
不在，我不在家。
Bú zài, wǒ bú zài jiā.
No, I'm not.

🧩 A-not-A Question — 在不在

在不在 is an A-not-A pattern commonly used to ask about someone's location. Put 不 between the two forms of 在.

S + 在 + 不在 + Place？
A-not-A
你在不在家？
Nǐ zài bù zài jiā?
Are you at home?
A-not-A
他在不在學校？
Tā zài bù zài xuéxiào?
Is he at school?
💡 Easy answer:

在。 = Yes / I am there.
不在。 = No / I am not there.
2

Ongoing Action — “be doing”

在 can also be placed before a verb to show that an action is currently happening. In English, this is similar to “be + V-ing.”

Positive
S + 在 + V + O
Example
我在吃飯。
Wǒ zài chīfàn.
I am eating.
Example
她在讀書。
Tā zài dúshū.
She is studying.
Example
哥哥在喝牛奶。
Gēge zài hē niúnǎi.
Older brother is drinking milk.
Example
妹妹在吃香蕉。
Mèimei zài chī xiāngjiāo.
Younger sister is eating a banana.
Negative · 沒有
S + 沒有 + 在 + V + O

To say that someone is not currently doing something, use 沒有 before 在.

Negative
我沒有在吃飯。
Wǒ méiyǒu zài chīfàn.
I am not eating.
Negative
她沒有在讀書。
Tā méiyǒu zài dúshū.
She is not studying.
Yes / No Question · 嗎
S + 在 + V + O + 嗎？

Add 嗎 at the end to ask whether someone is currently doing something.

Question
你在吃飯嗎？
Nǐ zài chīfàn ma?
Are you eating?
Question
她在讀書嗎？
Tā zài dúshū ma?
Is she studying?
Answers
有 = Yes   |   沒有 = No
Yes
有，我在吃飯。
Yǒu, wǒ zài chīfàn.
Yes, I am.
No
沒有，我沒有在吃飯。
Méiyǒu, wǒ méiyǒu zài chīfàn.
No, I'm not.
📝 Grammar Note:

You can use 在不在 + Verb for ongoing actions, but it is less common and less natural for beginners.

有沒有在 + Verb is usually preferred when asking whether someone is currently doing something.

🧩 A-not-A for Ongoing Actions

For ongoing actions, the more natural beginner-friendly pattern is 有沒有在 + Verb.

S + 有沒有在 + V + O？
A-not-A
你有沒有在吃飯？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu zài chīfàn?
Are you eating?
A-not-A
她有沒有在讀書？
Tā yǒu méiyǒu zài dúshū?
Is she studying?
A-not-A
哥哥有沒有在喝牛奶？
Gēge yǒu méiyǒu zài hē niúnǎi?
Is older brother drinking milk?
A-not-A
妹妹有沒有在吃香蕉？
Mèimei yǒu méiyǒu zài chī xiāngjiāo?
Is younger sister eating a banana?
3

Location vs. Ongoing Action

The position of 在 helps you understand what it is doing in the sentence.

📍 Location

S + 在 + Place

我在家。

I am at home.

🏃 Ongoing Action

S + 在 + V + O

我在吃飯。

I am eating.

🧋 Easy way to remember:

在 + Place → tells you where someone is.
在 + Verb → tells you what someone is doing right now.
