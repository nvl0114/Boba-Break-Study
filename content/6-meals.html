Meals (食物）

雅婷:
早安！來吃早飯吧！ (Zǎo ān! Lái chī zǎofàn ba!) Good morning! Come have breakfast!
昱廷:
好啊！早飯吃什麼？ (Hǎo a! Zǎofàn chī shénme?) Sure! What are we having for breakfast?
雅婷:
有牛奶、麵包和水果。 (Yǒu niúnǎi, miànbāo hé shuǐguǒ.) There is milk, bread, and fruit.
昱廷:
我想喝牛奶，還要吃一個麵包。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē niúnǎi, hái yào chī yí ge miànbāo.) I want to drink milk, and I also want to eat a piece of bread.
雅婷:
還有香蕉和蘋果，你要吃嗎？ (Hái yǒu xiāngjiāo hé píngguǒ, nǐ yào chī ma?) There are also bananas and apples. Do you want some?
昱廷:
要，我要一根香蕉，還要一個蘋果。 (Yào, wǒ yào yì gēn xiāngjiāo, hái yào yí ge píngguǒ.) Yes, I want a banana and also an apple.
雅婷:
你要喝果汁嗎？ (Nǐ yào hē guǒzhī ma?) Do you want to drink juice?
昱廷:
不用了，謝謝。我喝牛奶就好。 (Bú yòng le, xièxie. Wǒ hē niúnǎi jiù hǎo.) No, thank you. Milk is enough for me.
雅婷:
好。中午我們吃午餐，晚上吃晚餐。 (Hǎo. Zhōngwǔ wǒmen chī wǔcān, wǎnshàng chī wǎncān.) Okay. At noon we'll have lunch, and in the evening we'll have dinner.
昱廷:
好啊！ (Hǎo a!) Sounds good!

Grammar & Notes

1. 根 (gēn) — Measure Word

根 is a measure word used for long, thin objects, such as bananas, carrots, sausages, pencils, and sticks.

Pattern: Number + 根 + Noun

一根香蕉 — yì gēn xiāngjiāo — one banana
兩根香蕉 — liǎng gēn xiāngjiāo — two bananas
一根胡蘿蔔 — yì gēn húluóbo — one carrot
一根鉛筆 — yì gēn qiānbǐ — one pencil

2. 有…還有… — There is/are… and also…

有 (yǒu) means “there is / there are / have.” 還有 (hái yǒu) means “there is/are also” or “and also.”

Pattern: 有 + A + 還有 + B

桌上有牛奶，還有麵包。
Zhuō shàng yǒu niúnǎi, hái yǒu miànbāo.
→ There is milk on the table, and there is also bread.

早餐有香蕉，還有蘋果。
Zǎocān yǒu xiāngjiāo, hái yǒu píngguǒ.
→ There are bananas and also apples for breakfast.

3. 要…還要… — Want… and also want…

要 (yào) means “want” or “would like.” 還要 (hái yào) means “also want” or “want more.”

Pattern: 要 + A + 還要 + B

我要牛奶，還要麵包。
Wǒ yào niúnǎi, hái yào miànbāo.
→ I want milk, and I also want bread.

我要一根香蕉，還要一個蘋果。
Wǒ yào yì gēn xiāngjiāo, hái yào yí ge píngguǒ.
→ I want one banana, and I also want an apple.

4. 就 (jiù) — then / just / simply

就 has several meanings depending on the context. At a beginner level, learn it as “then” or “just / simply.”

Pattern & Examples:

你要喝牛奶，就喝牛奶。
Nǐ yào hē niúnǎi, jiù hē niúnǎi.
→ If you want milk, then drink milk.

我喝牛奶就好。
Wǒ hē niúnǎi jiù hǎo.
→ Milk is enough for me. / I'll just have milk. (就好 means “that's enough / that's fine”)

5. 用 / 不用 — Need / Don’t need

用 (yòng) means “to use.” 不用 (bú yòng) means “don’t need to” or “no need.”

不用 + Verb:
不用喝果汁。 (You don't need to drink juice.)
不用了，謝謝。 (No, thank you. / I'm good.)

用 + Noun + Verb:
我用杯子喝牛奶。
Wǒ yòng bēizi hē niúnǎi.
→ I drink milk using a cup.

6. 吧 (ba) — Suggestion Particle

吧 is a sentence-final modal particle used to soften a tone, make a suggestion, or invite someone to do something together.

Pattern: Action/Suggestion + 吧！

來吃早飯吧！
Lái chī zǎofàn ba!
→ Come have breakfast!

我們走吧！
Wǒmen zǒu ba!
→ Let's go!

喝茶吧！
Hē chá ba!
→ Have some tea!
