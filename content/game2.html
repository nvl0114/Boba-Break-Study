的 (de) — Possession & Relationships

Learn how to use 的 to show possession, relationships, and noun descriptions.

Lesson Progress 0 / 100 XP

Grammar

的 (de) is commonly used to connect a person or noun with something that belongs to them or is related to them.

1 / 4

的 = Possession

Use 的 to show that something belongs to someone.

Person + 的 + Thing
我的手機
my phone
媽媽的車子
Mom's car
2 / 4

Pronoun + 的

Pronouns can be followed by 的 to express possession.

我 + 的 → 我的
我們的朋友
our friend
他們的電腦
their computer
3 / 4

Noun + 的 + Noun

的 can connect two nouns to show a relationship.

Noun + 的 + Noun
老師的眼鏡
teacher's glasses
爸爸的禮物
Dad's gift
朋友的照片
friend's photo
4 / 4

的 + Sentence Patterns

You can combine 的 with sentence patterns you already know.

S + V + O
我喝我的牛奶。
I drink my milk.
他不看他的電視。
He doesn't watch his TV.
這是你的手機嗎？
Is this your phone?
你是不是我的朋友？
Are you my friend?
