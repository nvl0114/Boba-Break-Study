1. Grammar Guide & 的 (de)

的 (de) connects an owner or descriptor with a noun, functioning similarly to the English possessive “'s” or words like “my,” “your,” “his,” and “her.”

Owner + 的 + Noun
Example: 我的書 (wǒ de shū) — my book
我的書
wǒ de shū
my book

Core Sentence Patterns

Combine the possessive structures with foundational sentence patterns:

1. Affirmative (S + V + O):}
我喝水。
I drink water.

2. Negative (S + 不 + V + O):
我不喝水。
I don't drink water.

3. Question with 嗎 (S + V + O + 嗎):
你喝水嗎？
Do you drink water?

4. A-not-A Question (S + V + 不 + V):
你喝不喝水？
Do you drink water or not?
