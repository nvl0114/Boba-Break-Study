GRAMMAR LESSON • POSSESSION

的 (de) — Possession & Describing Nouns

Learn how to use 的 to say “my,” “your,” “his,” “her,” “our,” and “their.”

Lesson Progress XP 0

1. What is 的?

的 (de) is commonly used to connect a person or owner with a noun.

Person + 的 + Noun
my / your / his / her / our / their + noun
我的手機
wǒ de shǒujī
my phone

的 can also express English possessives such as “'s”.

爸爸的車
bàba de chē
Dad's car

2. Grammar Guide

Possession

Person + 的 + Noun

Use this structure to express possession or a relationship between a person and a noun.

我的手機
wǒ de shǒujī
my phone
我們的老師
wǒmen de lǎoshī
our teacher

“'s” — Owner + 的 + Thing

Owner + 的 + Thing
媽媽的手機
māma de shǒujī
Mom's phone
老師的電腦
lǎoshī de diànnǎo
the teacher's computer

的 + Previous Sentence Patterns

S + V + Possessor + 的 + O
我看我的照片。
wǒ kàn wǒ de zhàopiàn.
I look at my photo.
她買她的禮物。
tā mǎi tā de lǐwù.
She buys her gift.

的 + Questions & Negatives

S + 不 + V + 的 + O
我不看我的照片。
wǒ bù kàn wǒ de zhàopiàn.
I don't look at my photo.
你看你的照片嗎？
nǐ kàn nǐ de zhàopiàn ma?
Do you look at your photo?
你看不看你的照片？
nǐ kàn bù kàn nǐ de zhàopiàn?
Do you look at your photo or not?
