的 (de) — Possession & Describing Nouns

Learn how to use 的 to say “my,” “your,” “his,” “her,” “our,” and “their.”

Lesson Progress XP 0

1. Grammar Guide

What is 的?

Person + 的 + Noun
my / your / his / her / our / their + noun

的 (de) is commonly used to connect a person or owner with a noun, or to express possessives such as “'s”.

我的手機
wǒ de shǒujī
my phone
爸爸的車
bàba de chē
Dad's car

Possession & Relationships

Person + 的 + Noun

Use this structure to express possession or a relationship between a person and a noun.

我們的老師
wǒmen de lǎoshī
our teacher
朋友的信
péngyǒu de xìn
friend's letter

的 + Previous Sentence Patterns

S + V + Possessor + 的 + O
我看我的書。
wǒ kàn wǒ de shū.
I read my book.
她買她的禮物。
tā mǎi tā de lǐwù.
She buys her gift.

The 4 Core Patterns

1. S + V + O (e.g., 我喝水) 2. S + 不 + V + O (e.g., 我不喝水) 3. S + V + O + 嗎 (e.g., 你喝水嗎) 4. S + V + 不 + V (e.g., 你喝不喝)

Combine these structural templates with possessives using 的 to build complex sentences!
