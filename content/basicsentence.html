Basic Verbs

Learn how to say “I eat”, “you drink”, and “he comes to school” in Mandarin. 🍚

🧠 Let's Learn: S + Verb + Object

1. The Pattern (+)

A Mandarin sentence with an action word (verb) is very simple. Say who does it, then the action, then what the action is about. The verb does not change, and you do not need 是.

Swho
+
Vaction
+
Owhat
我吃飯
wǒ chī fàn
I eat. (I eat a meal.)

2. Negative Sentence (−)

Put 不 (bù) right before the verb to say you do not do it.

Swho
+
不not
+
Vaction
+
Owhat
我不吃飯
wǒ bù chī fàn
I do not eat.

3. Questions (?) — 2 Ways

There are two easy ways to ask a yes/no question. Both mean the same thing.

Way 1: say the verb two times with 不 in the middle (V不V)

Swho
+
V不Vdo or not?
+
Owhat
你吃不吃飯？
nǐ chī bù chī fàn?
Do you eat?

Way 2: add 嗎 (ma) at the end of the sentence

Swho
+
Vaction
+
Owhat
+
嗎question
你吃飯嗎？
nǐ chī fàn ma?
Do you eat?

4. How to Answer

This is different from 是. To answer a verb question, just repeat the verb.

  • Yes = the verb itself (吃)
  • No = 不 + verb (不吃)
Q
你吃不吃飯？
nǐ chī bù chī fàn?
Do you eat?
A (Yes)
吃！
chī!
Yes! (I eat.)
Q
你吃飯嗎？
nǐ chī fàn ma?
Do you eat?
A (No)
不吃！
bù chī!
No! (I do not eat.)

💡 Good to Know

  • Action words (verbs) like 吃 and 喝 do not use 是. Just say S + V + O.
  • Verbs never change. 吃 can mean “eat”, “eats”, or “ate”, depending on the situation.
  • 不 always goes before the verb: 我不吃飯.
  • To answer, repeat the verb: 吃 (yes) or 不吃 (no).
  • 讀書 and 唸書 mean the same thing: “to study”. 唸書 is more casual in daily talk.
  • 學習 means “to study / to learn”. You can also use 學 alone: 學中文.

The patterns never change: S + V + O, S + 不 + V + O, S + V不V + O, S + V + O + 嗎 🌱
