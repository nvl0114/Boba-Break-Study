Directions & Getting Around

Learn how to give directions, find places, and transfer transportation in Mandarin.

🧭 Grammar Patterns

Learn the essential Mandarin patterns you need to give and understand directions.

GRAMMAR 1 OF 6

1. Left & Right — 左邊 / 右邊

Use 左邊 and 右邊 to describe where something is located.

左邊 / 右邊
left / right side
+
Location

左邊 (zuǒbiān) means "the left side."

右邊 (yòubiān) means "the right side."

You can use them to describe the location of a place.

Example 1
左邊有銀行。
Zuǒbiān yǒu yínháng.
There is a bank on the left.
Example 2
右邊有咖啡店。
Yòubiān yǒu kāfēidiàn.
There is a café on the right.
GRAMMAR 2 OF 6

2. Going Left or Right — 往 + Direction + 走

Use 往 (wǎng) to indicate the direction of movement.

往
toward
+
Direction
+
走
walk / go

往 + direction + 走 means "walk or go toward a direction."

This pattern is especially useful when giving directions.

往左走。
Wǎng zuǒ zǒu.
Go left.
往右邊走。
Wǎng yòubiān zǒu.
Go to the right.
GRAMMAR 3 OF 6

3. Going Straight — 直走

直走 (zhí zǒu) means "go straight."

You can combine 直走 with time or distance when giving more detailed directions.

一直走。
Yìzhí zǒu.
Keep going straight.
往前走。
Wǎng qián zǒu.
Go forward.
直走三分鐘，再往左走。
Zhí zǒu sān fēnzhōng, zài wǎng zuǒ zǒu.
Go straight for three minutes, then go left.
GRAMMAR 4 OF 6

4. Turning Left & Right — 左轉 / 右轉

Use 左轉 and 右轉 when you need to turn at a particular location.

到 + Place
when you reach...
+
左轉 / 右轉
turn left / right

Use 到 (dào) to indicate that you should perform an action when you reach a place.

You can also use 在 (zài) to describe the location where you should turn.

到十字路口左轉。
Dào shízì lùkǒu zuǒ zhuǎn.
Turn left when you reach the intersection.
在十字路口右轉。
Zài shízì lùkǒu yòu zhuǎn.
Turn right at the intersection.
GRAMMAR 5 OF 6

5. Transferring Transportation — 轉車 / 轉乘

When changing buses, trains, or MRT lines, the most useful word is 轉.

轉車 (zhuǎn chē) means "to transfer or change vehicles."

換車 (huàn chē) also means "change vehicles," with more emphasis on replacing one vehicle with another.

轉乘 (zhuǎnchéng) is a more formal word for "transfer" and is commonly used in transportation information in Taiwan.

在台北車站轉車。
Zài Táiběi chēzhàn zhuǎn chē.
Transfer at Taipei Main Station.
從紅線轉藍線。
Cóng hóngxiàn zhuǎn lánxiàn.
Transfer from the Red Line to the Blue Line.
在台北車站轉藍線。
Zài Táiběi chēzhàn zhuǎn lánxiàn.
Transfer to the Blue Line at Taipei Main Station.
GRAMMAR 6 OF 6

6. Giving Directions Step by Step

Use sequence words to give clear directions in the correct order.

先
first
→
然後
then
→
最後
finally

先 (xiān) introduces the first action.

然後 (ránhòu) introduces the next action.

最後 (zuìhòu) introduces the final action.

You can also use 再 (zài) to mean "then" or "after that."

先直走，然後右轉，最後在車站轉車。
Xiān zhí zǒu, ránhòu yòu zhuǎn, zuìhòu zài chēzhàn zhuǎn chē.
First go straight, then turn right, and finally transfer at the station.
先往左走，再直走，然後右轉。
Xiān wǎng zuǒ zǒu, zài zhí zǒu, ránhòu yòu zhuǎn.
First go left, then go straight, and then turn right.
