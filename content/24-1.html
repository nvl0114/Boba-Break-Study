DIRECTIONS • TRANSPORTATION

Finding Your Way in Mandarin

Learn how to give directions, turn, transfer transportation, and understand common travel instructions.

🧭 Grammar Patterns

1. Left & Right — 左 / 右

Use 左邊 and 右邊 to describe the location of something on the left or right side.

左邊 / 右邊 left / right side
+
Location place

左邊 (zuǒbiān) means "the left side."

右邊 (yòubiān) means "the right side."

左邊有銀行。
Zuǒbiān yǒu yínháng.
There is a bank on the left.
右邊有咖啡店。
Yòubiān yǒu kāfēidiàn.
There is a café on the right.

2. Moving in a Direction — 往 + Direction + 走

往 (wǎng) indicates the direction of movement. It is especially useful when giving directions.

往 toward
+
Direction 左 / 右
+
走 walk / go

Use this structure when telling someone which direction they should go.

往左走。
Wǎng zuǒ zǒu.
Go left.
往右邊走。
Wǎng yòubiān zǒu.
Go to the right.

3. Going Straight — 直走 / 一直走

Use 直走 when telling someone to go straight. 一直走 emphasizes continuing without stopping or turning.

直走 (zhí zǒu) — go straight

一直走 (yìzhí zǒu) — keep going straight

往前走 (wǎng qián zǒu) — go forward

直走五分鐘。
Zhí zǒu wǔ fēnzhōng.
Go straight for five minutes.

4. Straight + Then Change Direction

You can combine several direction instructions in one sentence.

直走 go straight
+
Time / Distance 三分鐘
+
再 then
+
Direction 往左走
直走三分鐘，再往左走。
Zhí zǒu sān fēnzhōng, zài wǎng zuǒ zǒu.
Go straight for three minutes, then go left.

5. Turning Left & Right — 左轉 / 右轉

Use 左轉 and 右轉 when you need to turn at a specific location.

到 when reaching
+
Place 十字路口
+
左轉 / 右轉 turn left / right
到十字路口左轉。
Dào shízì lùkǒu zuǒ zhuǎn.
Turn left when you reach the intersection.
在十字路口右轉。
Zài shízì lùkǒu yòu zhuǎn.
Turn right at the intersection.

6. From A to B — 從...到...

Use 從...到... to describe movement from one place to another.

從 from
+
Place A starting point
+
到 to
+
Place B destination

從 (cóng) introduces the starting point.

到 (dào) introduces the destination.

從車站到餐廳。
Cóng chēzhàn dào cāntīng.
From the station to the restaurant.
從淡水到台北。
Cóng Dànshuǐ dào Táiběi.
From Tamsui to Taipei.

7. Transferring Transportation — 轉車 / 換車

In Taiwan, 轉車 is commonly used when transferring between buses, trains, or MRT lines.

從 A from A
+
轉 transfer
+
B to B
從紅線轉藍線。
Cóng hóngxiàn zhuǎn lánxiàn.
Transfer from the Red Line to the Blue Line.
在台北車站轉車。
Zài Táiběi chēzhàn zhuǎn chē.
Transfer at Taipei Main Station.

8. Transfer Connection — 轉乘

轉乘 means "to transfer" or "make a connection." It is slightly more formal and commonly appears in Taiwan transportation information.

轉乘捷運 — transfer to the MRT

轉乘公車 — transfer to a bus

轉乘火車 — transfer to a train

在車站轉乘捷運。
Zài chēzhàn zhuǎnchéng jiéyùn.
Transfer to the MRT at the station.

9. Giving Multiple Instructions

Use sequence words to give several directions clearly.

先 first
→
然後 then
→
最後 finally
先直走，然後右轉，最後在車站轉車。
Xiān zhí zǒu, ránhòu yòu zhuǎn, zuìhòu zài chēzhàn zhuǎn chē.
First go straight, then turn right, and finally transfer at the station.
先往左走，再直走，然後右轉。
Xiān wǎng zuǒ zǒu, zài zhí zǒu, ránhòu yòu zhuǎn.
First go left, then go straight, and then turn right.
