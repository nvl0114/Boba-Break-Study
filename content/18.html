Āháo 阿豪 喜歡誰？ Āháo 阿豪 是一個學生。 他每天都去學校，也有很多同學。 bān shàng 班上 有幾個女生常常跟他 shuōhuà 說話 。 Āháo 阿豪 juéde 覺得 Xiǎo Ān 小安 很可愛，也 juéde 覺得 Xiǎo Yǎ 小雅 很漂亮。 Xiǎo Ān 小安 喜歡看書， Xiǎo Yǎ 小雅 喜歡聽音樂。 她們都很好，也都常常跟 Āháo 阿豪 一起吃飯。 可是， zuìjìn 最近 Āháo 阿豪 fāxiàn 發現 yí jiàn shì 一件事 。 他每天都會 zhǎo 找 Xiǎo Ān 小安 。 xiàkè 下課 de shíhou 的時候 ， 他想跟 Xiǎo Ān 小安 shuōhuà 說話 。 吃 wǔcān 午餐 de shíhou 的時候 ， 他想坐在 Xiǎo Ān 小安 pángbiān 旁邊 。 yǒushíhou 有時候 Xiǎo Ān 小安 bú zài 不在 教室， 他還會問同學： 「 Xiǎo Ān 小安 去哪裡了？」 朋友 kàndào 看到 yǐhòu 以後 ， 就問他： 「你是不是喜歡 Xiǎo Ān 小安 ？」 Āháo 阿豪 聽了 yǐhòu 以後 ， 有 yìdiǎn 一點 bù hǎoyìsi 不好意思 。 「我 bù zhīdào 不知道 ……」 可是，他 xīnlǐ 心裡 知道答案。 因為 kàndào 看到 Xiǎo Ān 小安 的 shíhou 時候 ， 他 zhēnde 真的 > 很開心。 所以現在他每天都有一個 xiǎo 小 wèntí 問題 ： 我要不要 gàosù 告訴 Xiǎo Ān 小安 ， 我喜歡她？
English Meaning:

Who does A-Hao like? A-Hao is a student. He goes to school every day and has many classmates. There are several girls in his class who often talk to him. A-Hao thinks Xiao An is cute, and he also thinks Xiao Ya is beautiful. Xiao An likes reading, while Xiao Ya likes listening to music. They are both nice and often eat with A-Hao. But recently, A-Hao discovered something. Every day, he looks for Xiao An. When class ends, he wants to talk to Xiao An. At lunchtime, he wants to sit next to Xiao An. Sometimes Xiao An is not in the classroom, so he even asks his classmates, "Where did Xiao An go?" After his friend sees this, the friend asks him, "Do you like Xiao An?" A-Hao feels a little embarrassed. "I don't know..." But in his heart, he knows the answer. Because whenever he sees Xiao An, he is really happy. So now he has one little question every day: "Should I tell Xiao An that I like her?"

🧠 Grammar Patterns

Learn eight useful patterns from the Lesson 18 story. All explanations are in English.

1. 覺得 (juéde) — to think / feel

Subject + 覺得 + Clause

覺得 is used to express what you think or how something feels to you.

It can be translated as "think," "feel," or "feel that."

我覺得這本書很好看。
Wǒ juéde zhè běn shū hěn hǎokàn.
I think this book is very good.

2. 常常 / 平常 — often / usually

Subject + 常常 + Verb
平常 + Subject + Verb

常常 means "often." It describes something that happens frequently.

平常 means "usually," "normally," or "on ordinary days."

他常常跟朋友吃飯。
Tā chángcháng gēn péngyǒu chīfàn.
He often eats with his friends.

平常我七點起床。
Píngcháng wǒ qī diǎn qǐchuáng.
I usually get up at seven.

3. 最近 (zuìjìn) — recently / lately

最近 + Subject + Verb / Adjective

最近 refers to the recent period around now.

It is commonly placed at the beginning of a sentence to introduce a recent situation.

最近我很忙。
Zuìjìn wǒ hěn máng.
I have been very busy lately.

4. 想 (xiǎng) — want to / would like to

Subject + 想 + Verb

想 can express a desire or intention to do something.

It is normally followed by a verb or verb phrase.

我想跟朋友說話。
Wǒ xiǎng gēn péngyǒu shuōhuà.
I want to talk with my friend.

5. 有時候 (yǒushíhou) — sometimes

Subject + 有時候 + Verb

有時候 means "sometimes."

It describes something that happens occasionally, but not all the time.

我有時候在家吃午餐。
Wǒ yǒushíhou zài jiā chī wǔcān.
Sometimes I eat lunch at home.

6. 真的 (zhēnde) — really

Subject + 真的 + Adjective / Verb

真的 emphasizes that something is genuinely true or strongly felt.

It can be translated as "really," "truly," or "actually."

我真的很喜歡這本書。
Wǒ zhēnde hěn xǐhuān zhè běn shū.
I really like this book.

7. 的時候 (de shíhou) — when / at the time when

Verb / Situation + 的時候，...

的時候 means "when" or "at the time when."

Put the activity or situation before 的時候.

下課的時候，我想喝水。
Xiàkè de shíhou, wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.
When class ends, I want to drink water.

8. 一點 (yìdiǎn) — a little / a bit

有 + 一點 + Adjective

一點 means "a little" or "a bit."

It can soften a description or show a small degree of a feeling or condition.

我有一點不好意思。
Wǒ yǒu yìdiǎn bù hǎoyìsi.
I feel a little embarrassed.
