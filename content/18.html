LESSON 18 • FEELINGS & REALIZATIONS

Who Does Ahao Like?

Learn how to express thoughts, habits, recent situations, feelings, and questions about whether someone wants to do something.

📖 Reading: Who Does Ahao Like?

Read the story and listen to the pronunciation. Pinyin appears only above Lesson 18 vocabulary.

Reading Text

Pinyin is shown only for target vocabulary in this lesson.

Āháo 阿豪 是一個學生。 他每天都去學校，也有很多同學。 bān shàng 班上 有幾個女生常常跟他 shuōhuà 說話 。

Āháo 阿豪 juéde 覺得 Xiǎo Ān 小安 很可愛，也 juéde 覺得 Xiǎo Yǎ 小雅 很漂亮。 Xiǎo Ān 小安 喜歡看書， Xiǎo Yǎ 小雅 喜歡聽音樂。 她們都很好，也都常常跟 Āháo 阿豪 一起吃飯。

可是， zuìjìn 最近 Āháo 阿豪 fāxiàn 發現 yí jiàn shì 一件事 。

他每天都會 zhǎo 找 Xiǎo Ān 小安 。

xiàkè 下課 de shíhou 的時候 ，他想跟 Xiǎo Ān 小安 shuōhuà 說話 。 吃 wǔcān 午餐 de shíhou 的時候 ，他想坐在 Xiǎo Ān 小安 pángbiān 旁邊 。

yǒu shíhou 有時候 Xiǎo Ān 小安 bú zài 不在 教室，他還會問同學： 「 Xiǎo Ān 小安 去哪裡了？」

朋友 kàndào 看到 yǐhòu 以後 ，就問他： 「你是不是喜歡 Xiǎo Ān 小安 ？」

Āháo 阿豪 聽了 yǐhòu 以後 ， yǒu yìdiǎn 有一點 bù hǎoyìsi 不好意思 。

「我 bù zhīdào 不知道 ……」

可是，他 xīnlǐ 心裡 zhīdào 知道 答案。

因為 kàndào 看到 Xiǎo Ān 小安 de shíhou 的時候 ，他 zhēnde 真的 很開心。

所以現在他每天都有一個 wèntí 問題 ：

我要不要 gàosù 告訴 Xiǎo Ān 小安 ，我喜歡她？
English Meaning:
Ahao is a student. He goes to school every day and has many classmates. There are several girls in his class who often talk to him.

Ahao thinks Xiao An is cute, and he also thinks Xiao Ya is beautiful. Xiao An likes reading, while Xiao Ya likes listening to music. They are both nice and often eat together with Ahao.

But recently, Ahao realized something. He looks for Xiao An every day. After class, he wants to talk to her. At lunchtime, he wants to sit beside her. Sometimes Xiao An is not in the classroom, and he even asks his classmates where she went.

After his friends noticed this, they asked him, "Do you like Xiao An?" Ahao felt a little embarrassed. "I don't know..."

But in his heart, he knows the answer. Because whenever he sees Xiao An, he is really happy. So now he has one little question every day: "Should I tell Xiao An that I like her?"

🧠 Grammar Patterns

1. 覺得 — To Think / Feel

Subject + 覺得 + Statement

覺得 means "to think" or "to feel." It is commonly used when giving your personal opinion or describing how something seems to you.

The structure is: Subject + 覺得 + description / sentence.

我覺得小安很可愛。
I think Xiao An is very cute.

我覺得今天很好。
I think today is good.

2. 常常 / 平常 — Often / Usually

Subject + 常常 + Verb
Subject + 平常 + Verb

常常 means "often." It describes an action that happens frequently.

平常 means "usually / normally." It describes what normally happens in someone's routine.

他常常跟朋友說話。
He often talks with his friends.

我平常在班上吃午餐。
I usually eat lunch in class.

3. 最近 — Recently / Lately

最近 + Subject + Verb / Adjective

最近 means "recently" or "lately." It refers to a period close to the present.

It is often placed at the beginning of the sentence to establish the recent time period.

最近我很忙。
I have been very busy recently.

最近阿豪常常找小安。
Recently, Ahao often looks for Xiao An.

4. 想 — Want To / Want

Subject + 想 + Verb

想 can mean "want to" when it comes before another verb.

It expresses a desire or intention to do something.

我想跟小安說話。
I want to talk with Xiao An.

他想坐在小安旁邊。
He wants to sit beside Xiao An.

5. 有時候 — Sometimes

有時候 + Subject + Verb

有時候 means "sometimes." It describes an action that happens occasionally, but not every time.

It is often placed at the beginning of the sentence, although it can also appear before the verb.

有時候小安不在班上。
Sometimes Xiao An is not in class.

我有時候找朋友。
Sometimes I look for my friends.

6. 真的 — Really / Truly

Subject + 真的 + Adjective / Verb

真的 means "really" or "truly." It emphasizes that a feeling, opinion, or statement is genuine or strong.

我真的很開心。
I am really happy.

我真的覺得很好。
I really think it is good.

7. 的時候 — When / At the Time When

Verb / Situation + 的時候 + Sentence

的時候 means "when" or "at the time when."

It connects a time or situation to what happens during that time.

The pattern can be used with activities, situations, or time expressions.

下課的時候，我想說話。
When class ends, I want to talk.

看到小安的時候，我很開心。
When I see Xiao An, I am very happy.

8. 一點 — A Little / A Bit

有一點 + Adjective

一點 means "a little" or "a bit." 有一點 + adjective is commonly used to describe a small degree of a feeling or condition.

In the reading, 阿豪 is 有一點不好意思, meaning that he feels a little embarrassed.

我有一點不好意思。
I am a little embarrassed.

今天有一點冷。
It is a little cold today.
