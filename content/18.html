LOCATION & POSITION

Using 在 with Position Words

Learn how to describe where people and things are using 在 and 方位詞.

🧠 Grammar Patterns: Detailed Breakdown

🔄 Review: 在

We learned 在 (zài) in an earlier lesson. Let's review it!

Previously, we learned that 在 can be used to say where someone or something is.

But sometimes, simply saying the place isn't enough.

書在教室。
Shū zài jiàoshì.
The book is in the classroom.

Where exactly is the book?

Inside the bag? Outside? In front? Behind?

To give a more specific location, we can use 方位詞 (position words) with 在.

### 📍 Position Words

Position words help us describe a more specific location.

In this lesson, we will practice: 裡面、外面、上面、下面、前面、後面、中間、對面.

1. Subject + 在 + Place

Subject + 在 + Place

Explanation: We use 在 to say where someone or something is.

The place can be a room, building, workplace, or another location.

我在房間。
Wǒ zài fángjiān.
I am in the room.
他在銀行。
Tā zài yínháng.
He is at the bank.
她在郵局。
Tā zài yóujú.
She is at the post office.
哥哥在工廠。
Gēge zài gōngchǎng.
My older brother is at the factory.

2. Subject + 在 + Position Word

Subject + 在 + Position Word

We can use 在 + 方位詞 to describe a more specific location.

The position word tells us exactly where the subject is.

貓在裡面。
Māo zài lǐmiàn.
The cat is inside.
老師在外面。
Lǎoshī zài wàimiàn.
The teacher is outside.
書在桌子上面。
Shū zài zhuōzi shàngmiàn.
The book is on the desk.
包包在椅子下面。
Bāobāo zài yǐzi xiàmiàn.
The bag is under the chair.

3. A + 在 + B + 的 + Position Word

A + 在 + B + 的 + Position Word

Now we can describe where A is in relation to B.

Here, 的 connects B with the position word.

貓在椅子的下面。
Māo zài yǐzi de xiàmiàn.
The cat is under the chair.
銀行在郵局的前面。
Yínháng zài yóujú de qiánmiàn.
The bank is in front of the post office.
工廠在銀行的後面。
Gōngchǎng zài yínháng de hòumiàn.
The factory is behind the bank.
銀行在郵局的對面。
Yínháng zài yóujú de duìmiàn.
The bank is across from the post office.

4. Asking About Location with 哪裡

A + 在哪裡？

We use 哪裡 (nǎlǐ) to ask about location.

Pattern 1 — Where is A?

A + 在哪裡？

銀行在哪裡？
Yínháng zài nǎlǐ?
Where is the bank?
銀行在郵局的對面。
Yínháng zài yóujú de duìmiàn.
The bank is across from the post office.
A + 在 + B + 的 + 哪裡？

Pattern 2 — Where is A in relation to B?

A + 在 + B + 的 + 哪裡？

銀行在郵局的哪裡？
Yínháng zài yóujú de nǎlǐ?
Where is the bank in relation to the post office?
椅子在房間的哪裡？
Yǐzi zài fángjiān de nǎlǐ?
Where is the chair in the room?
椅子在房間的裡面。
Yǐzi zài fángjiān de lǐmiàn.
The chair is inside the room.

💡 Remember:

A 在 B 的 + 方位詞

Think of it as:

A → 在 → B → 的 → position

銀行 → 在 → 郵局 → 的 → 對面

The bank is across from the post office.
