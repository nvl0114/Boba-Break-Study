CONVERSATION • BEGINNER

Introductions & Identity

The questions Taiwanese people will most likely ask you when they meet you. 🧋

👋 Before We Start

When you live in Taiwan, people are friendly and curious. Sooner or later, almost everyone you meet will ask you the same few questions: what is your name, where are you from, how old are you, how long have you been here, and why did you come to Taiwan.

In this lesson you will learn how to understand each question and how to answer it naturally. Every Chinese word comes with pinyin (how to read it) and its English meaning, so you can follow along even as a beginner.

What you will learn:

  • Asking and giving your family name and full name
  • Saying where you are from
  • Saying your age
  • Saying how long you have been in Taiwan
  • Saying why you came to Taiwan

🧠 Let's Learn: Q & A

1. Asking for your family name

This is the polite, formal way to ask someone's family name (surname). Usually, the person only wants your surname, not your full name.

QUESTION
您貴姓？
nín guìxìng?
May I ask your family name? (polite)
ANSWER
我姓王。
wǒ xìng Wáng.
My family name is Wang.
ANSWER (WITH FULL NAME)
我姓王，叫王小明。
wǒ xìng Wáng, jiào Wáng Xiǎomíng.
My family name is Wang, and I am called Wang Xiaoming.
我姓wǒ xìng
+
Family namee.g. 王
  • 貴姓 (guìxìng) = honorable surname (a polite way to ask)
  • 姓 (xìng) = family name / to be surnamed
  • 叫 (jiào) = to be called
💡 If you only want to give your surname, say 我姓 + family name. If you also want to give your full name, add 叫 + full name.

2. Asking for your full name

This question asks for your full name. It is more casual than 您貴姓.

QUESTION
你叫什麼名字？
nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
What is your name?
ANSWER 1
我叫王小明。
wǒ jiào Wáng Xiǎomíng.
I am called Wang Xiaoming.
ANSWER 2
我的名字叫王小明。
wǒ de míngzi jiào Wáng Xiǎomíng.
My name is called Wang Xiaoming.
我叫wǒ jiào
+
Full namee.g. 王小明
  • 叫 (jiào) = to be called
  • 什麼 (shénme) = what
  • 名字 (míngzi) = name
💡 叫什麼名字 means they are asking for your full name. Answer with 我叫 + full name or 我的名字叫 + full name.

3. Where are you from?

People in Taiwan will often ask which country you come from. There are two common questions, and they mean almost the same thing.

QUESTION 1
你是哪裡人？
nǐ shì nǎlǐ rén?
Where are you from? (literally: which place's person)
QUESTION 2
你是哪國人？
nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?
Which country are you from?
ANSWER 1
我是美國人。
wǒ shì Měiguó rén.
I am American.
ANSWER 2
我來自美國。
wǒ láizì Měiguó.
I come from America.
我是wǒ shì
+
Countrye.g. 美國
+
人rén
  • 哪國 (nǎ guó) = which country
  • 哪裡 (nǎlǐ) = where / which place
  • 人 (rén) = person
  • 來自 (láizì) = to come from
  • 美國 (Měiguó) = United States
💡 Replace 美國 with your own country, for example 日本 (Rìběn, Japan) or 印尼 (Yìnní, Indonesia). Then answer with 我是 (country) 人 or 我來自 (country).

4. How old are you?

Both questions simply ask your age. You will hear 你幾歲 more often with younger people, and 你多大了 is a very common, natural way to ask adults.

QUESTION 1
你幾歲？
nǐ jǐ suì?
How old are you?
QUESTION 2
你多大了？
nǐ duō dà le?
How old are you? (literally: how big have you become)
ANSWER
我三十歲。
wǒ sānshí suì.
I am thirty years old.
我wǒ
+
Your agee.g. 三十
+
歲suì
  • 幾 (jǐ) = how many
  • 歲 (suì) = years old
  • 多 (duō) = how (much)
  • 大 (dà) = big
  • 了 (le) = particle that shows a change or "already"
💡 多大了 literally means "how big have you become", but people use it to ask your age. Answer with 我 + (your age) + 歲.

5. How long have you been in Taiwan?

This is one of the most common questions foreigners hear in Taiwan.

QUESTION
你來台灣多久了？
nǐ lái Táiwān duōjiǔ le?
How long have you been in Taiwan?
ANSWER (YEARS)
我來台灣三年了。
wǒ lái Táiwān sān nián le.
I have been in Taiwan for three years.
ANSWER (MONTHS)
我來台灣三個月了。
wǒ lái Táiwān sān ge yuè le.
I have been in Taiwan for three months.
我來台灣wǒ lái Táiwān
+
Number + 年 / 個月nián / ge yuè
+
了le
  • 多久 (duōjiǔ) = how long
  • 久 (jiǔ) = a long time
  • 年 (nián) = year
  • 月 (yuè) = month
  • 個 (ge) = measure word for "months"
💡 For months you must add 個. Without 個, it becomes the name of a month:
一個月 (yí ge yuè) = one month
一月 (yīyuè) = January (you will learn this in the Time lesson)

6. Why did you come to Taiwan?

These questions ask about your purpose for coming to Taiwan.

QUESTION 1
你來台灣做什麼？
nǐ lái Táiwān zuò shénme?
What do you do in Taiwan? / What did you come to Taiwan to do?
QUESTION 2
你為什麼來台灣？
nǐ wèishénme lái Táiwān?
Why did you come to Taiwan?
ANSWER
我來台灣工作。
wǒ lái Táiwān gōngzuò.
I came to Taiwan to work.
我來台灣wǒ lái Táiwān
+
Reasone.g. 工作
  • 做 (zuò) = to do
  • 什麼 (shénme) = what
  • 為什麼 (wèishénme) = why
  • 工作 (gōngzuò) = to work
  • 讀書 / 唸書 (dúshū / niànshū) = to study / go to school
  • 結婚 (jiéhūn) = to get married
💡 Answer with 我來台灣 + reason. For example: 我來台灣讀書 (I came to Taiwan to study) or 我來台灣結婚 (I came to Taiwan to get married).
