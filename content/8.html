GRAMMAR LESSON • CONTINUOUS ACTIONS

Grammar: 在 / 正在 + Verb

Learn how to express ongoing actions (similar to is / am / are + Ving) in Mandarin.

🧠 Grammar Card 1: Continuous Actions (Without Object)

1. Positive Continuous Action

S + 在 / 正在 + V

Explanation: Use 在 (zài) or 正在 (zhèngzài) before a verb to show an action currently in progress. It is the same as is / am / are + Ving in English.

他在睡覺 (Tā zài shuìjiào - He is sleeping)
哥哥正在跑步 (Gēge zhèngzài pǎobù - Older brother is running)

2. Negative Continuous Action

S + 沒在 + V

Explanation: Use 沒在 (méi zài) before a verb to state that someone is not currently doing that action.

我沒在休息 (Wǒ méi zài xiūxí - I am not resting)

3. Question with 嗎

S + 在 / 正在 + V + 嗎？

Explanation: Add 嗎 (ma) at the end to ask if someone is doing an action right now.

他们在逛街嗎？ (Tāmen zài guànjiē ma? - Are they shopping / strolling?)
狗正在散步嗎？ (Gǒu zhèngzài sànbù ma? - Is the dog taking a walk?)
Answer: 他在逛街 (Tā zài guànjiē - He is shopping)
Answer: 他沒在逛街 (Tā méi zài guànjiē - He is not shopping)
Short Answer: 對 (Duì - Correct / Yes)
Short Answer: 不 (Bù - No)

4. Note on 在不在 + Verb

在不在 + Verb is NOT natural

Explanation: Note that using the A-not-A form directly before a verb like 在不在 + Verb is not natural in Mandarin. To ask questions about ongoing actions, use the 嗎 particle instead.

Incorrect style: 他在不在睡覺？ (Unnatural)
Correct style: 他在睡覺嗎？ (Tā zài shuìjiào ma? - Is he sleeping?)

5. Difference Between 在 and 正在

在 vs 正在

Explanation: 在 (zài) is more casual and flexible. 正在 (zhèngzài) places stronger emphasis on the action happening right at this exact moment.

我在吃飯 (Casual: I am eating)
我正在吃飯 (Emphatic: I am eating right now at this exact moment)

6. Bonus: What are you doing?

你在做什麼？ / 你在幹嘛？

Explanation: Use 你在做什麼？ (Nǐ zài zuò shénme?) or 你在幹嘛？ (Nǐ zài gànmá?) to ask "What are you doing?". Reply with 我在... (Wǒ zài...).

你在做什麼？ (Nǐ zài zuò shénme? - What are you doing?)
你在幹嘛？ (Nǐ zài gànmá? - What are you up to / doing?)
Answer: 我在玩遊戲 (Wǒ zài wán yóuxì - I am playing a game)

🧠 Grammar Card 2: Continuous Actions with Objects

1. Positive Action with Object

S + 在 / 正在 + V + O

Explanation: Add an object after the verb to specify what is being acted upon during the continuous action.

學生在做作業 (Xuésheng zài zuò zuòyè - Students are doing homework)
你正在學習英文 (Nǐ zhèngzài xuéxí Yīngwén - You are studying English right now)

2. Negative Action with Object

S + 沒在 + V + O

Explanation: Use 沒在 (méi zài) before the verb and object to negate the ongoing activity.

弟弟沒在寫功課 (Dìdi méi zài xiě gōngkè - Younger brother is not writing homework)
她沒在唱歌 (Tā méi zài chànggē - She is not singing)

3. Question with Object and Answers

S + 在 / 正在 + V + O + 嗎？

Explanation: Form questions with objects using 嗎 and answer positively or negatively.

媽媽在看醫生嗎？ (Māma zài kàn yīshēng ma? - Is mom seeing a doctor?)
他們正在吃蛋糕嗎？ (Tāmen zhèngzài chī dàngāo ma? - Are they eating cake right now?)
Answer: 媽媽在看醫生 (Māma zài kàn yīshēng - Mom is seeing a doctor)
Answer: 媽媽沒在看醫生 (Māma méi zài kàn yīshēng - Mom is not seeing a doctor)
Answer: 他們正在吃蛋糕 / 他們沒在吃蛋糕 (They are eating cake / They are not eating cake)
Short Answer: 對 (Duì - Correct / Yes)
Short Answer: 不 (Bù - No)
