GRAMMAR LESSON • 有 / 沒有

有、沒有 & 有沒有

Learn how to say what you have, what you don't have, and ask whether someone has something or has done an action.

🧠 Grammar Patterns

1. Positive 有

S + 有 + O

Explanation: 有 (yǒu) means “to have” or “there is/are”. Use it to say that someone possesses or has something.

Structure: Subject + 有 + Object

我有錢。
Wǒ yǒu qián.
I have money.

2. Negative 沒有

S + 沒有 + O

Explanation: 沒有 (méiyǒu) means “do not have / does not have”. It is the standard negative form of 有.

Important: Do not normally use 不有 to mean “do not have”. Use 沒有.

我沒有錢。
Wǒ méiyǒu qián.
I don't have money.

3. 有沒有 Question

S + 有沒有 + O？

Explanation: 有沒有 creates an A-not-A question. It asks whether someone has something or not.

Answering: 有 = Yes, I have.
沒有 = No, I don't have.

你有沒有錢？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu qián?
Do you have money?

4. 有沒有 + Verb

S + 有沒有 + V + O？

Explanation: 有沒有 can also be used before a verb phrase to ask whether an action has happened or not.

This structure is very common in spoken Mandarin, including Taiwanese Mandarin.

Answering: 有 + V + O / 沒有 + V + O

你有沒有吃飯？
Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu chīfàn?
Have you eaten?

5. 有 + Food / Object

S + 有 + Food / Object

Explanation: 有 can be used when asking about or stating the availability of food, drinks, or other objects.

For example: 我有包子 = I have buns.
我有湯 = I have soup.

我有包子。
Wǒ yǒu bāozi.
I have steamed buns.

6. 沒有 + Food / Object

S + 沒有 + Food / Object

Explanation: Use 沒有 to say that something is unavailable or that you do not have it.

我沒有餃子。
Wǒ méiyǒu jiǎozi.
I don't have dumplings.

7. Short Answers

有。 / 沒有。

Question: 你有沒有湯？

Positive answer: 有。
Yes, I do / Yes, I have.

Negative answer: 沒有。
No, I don't / No, I don't have any.

有。
Yǒu.
Yes, I have.
