Are You Feeling Well?

Learn how to say what someone didn't do, ask whether something has already happened, and give gentle advice with 應該.

📖 Reading: Where Is Xiao Mei?

This lesson uses a short story about a worried friend to practice talking about things that didn't happen and giving advice. Pinyin is shown only for this lesson's vocabulary.

Reading Text

Pinyin appears only above target vocabulary from this lesson.

今天早上，阿豪 lái 來 學校，但是他 méi 沒 看到小美。
阿豪想：「小美今天有 lái 來 學校嗎？」
他問同學：「你有 méi 沒 有看到小美？」
同學 shuō 說 ：「 méi 沒 有，我不 zhīdào 知道 。」
阿豪有一點 dānxīn 擔心 ，所以打電話給小美。
「小美，你怎麼 méi 沒 lái 來 學校？你還好嗎？」
小美 shuō 說 ：「我今天不太 shūfú 舒服 ，所以 méi 沒 lái 來 學校。」
阿豪問：「你吃了 zǎocān 早餐 嗎？」
小美 shuō 說 ：「我 méi 沒 吃 zǎocān 早餐 。」
阿豪 shuō 說 ：「你 yīnggāi 應該 休息,也要吃一點東西。今天 jiù 就 好好休息，不要 lái 來 學校了！」
小美 shuō 說 ：「好，謝謝你。明天我 yīnggāi 應該 huì 會 lái 來 學校。」
English Meaning:
This morning, Ah Hao came to school, but he didn't see Xiao Mei. Ah Hao thought, "Did Xiao Mei come to school today?" He asked a classmate, "Did you see Xiao Mei?" The classmate said, "No, I don't know."

Ah Hao got a little worried, so he called Xiao Mei. "Xiao Mei, why didn't you come to school? Are you okay?" Xiao Mei said, "I'm not feeling well today, so I didn't come to school."

Ah Hao asked, "Did you eat breakfast?" Xiao Mei said, "I didn't eat breakfast." Ah Hao said, "You should rest, and you should also eat something. Just rest well today, don't come to school!"

Xiao Mei said, "Okay, thank you. I should come to school tomorrow."

🧠 Grammar Patterns

1. 沒 + V — Didn't Do Something

S + 沒 + V + O

沒 + V is used to say that someone did not do something.

When using 沒 to negate a completed action, we normally do not use 了 after the verb.

❌ 我沒吃了早餐。
✅ 我沒吃早餐。

我沒看到小美。
I didn't see Xiao Mei.

我沒吃早餐。
I didn't eat breakfast.

小美沒來學校。
Xiao Mei didn't come to school.

2. S + V + 了 + O + 嗎？

S + V + 了 + O + 嗎？

Use V + 了 + O + 嗎？ to ask whether someone has already done something.

In the reading, 阿豪 asks Xiao Mei whether she has eaten breakfast: 你吃了早餐嗎？

你吃了早餐嗎？
Did you eat breakfast?

你看了電影嗎？
Did you watch the movie?

你做了功課嗎？
Did you do your homework?

3. Review: S + 有沒有 + V + O？

S + 有沒有 + V + O？

有沒有 + V is an A-not-A question pattern. It asks whether someone did something or not.

In the reading, the classmate is asked: 你有沒有看到小美？

你有沒有吃早餐？
Did you eat breakfast?

你有沒有看到小美？
Did you see Xiao Mei?

你有沒有去學校？
Did you go to school?

4. 應該 — Should / Probably

S + 應該 + V

應該 means "should" or "probably." It is often used for giving advice.

In the reading: 你應該休息，也要吃一點東西。 "You should rest, and you should also eat something."

你應該休息。
You should rest.

你應該吃早餐。
You should eat breakfast.

你應該回家。
You should go home.

5. 來 — To Come

來 + Place

來 means "to come." Use it when someone moves toward the speaker or a place where the speaker is.

In the reading: 明天我應該會來學校。 "I should come to school tomorrow."

來學校
come to school

來我家
come to my house

來這裡
come here

6. Key Grammar Review

A quick recap of all five patterns

沒 + V → did not do something

V + 了 + O + 嗎？ → Did someone do something?

有沒有 + V + O？ → Did someone do something or not?

應該 + V → should do something

來 + Place → come to a place
