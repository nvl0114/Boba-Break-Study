是 · 很

“am / is / are” in Mandarin

是 (shì) and 很 (hěn) can both feel similar to “am / is / are” in English, but they are used in different situations.

🧋 Two Little Words, Two Different Jobs!

Don't worry — the easiest way to remember them is: 是 goes with a noun or identity, while 很 goes with an adjective or description.

是 → 👤 Noun    |    很 → ✨ Adjective
1

是 (shì) — “to be” / am, is, are

Use 是 when the word after it is a noun, person, identity, or category. Think of it as the Mandarin version of “am / is / are” when identifying someone or something.

Basic Pattern
S + 是 + Noun
Example
我是學生。
Wǒ shì xuéshēng.
I am a student.
Example
他是老師。
Tā shì lǎoshī.
He is a teacher.
Example
她是台灣人。
Tā shì Táiwān rén.
She is Taiwanese.
Negative · 不是
S + 不是 + Noun

To say that someone or something is not a noun or identity, use 不是.

Negative
我不是老師。
Wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.
I am not a teacher.
Negative
他不是學生。
Tā bú shì xuéshēng.
He is not a student.
💡 Easy way to remember:

是 → “am / is / are” + WHO or WHAT

我是學生。👩‍🎓
I am a student.
2

很 (hěn) — “very” / am, is, are

很 is often introduced as “very”. But in basic Mandarin adjective sentences, 很 can function like “am / is / are” in English. It is commonly placed before an adjective.

Basic Pattern
S + 很 + Adjective
Example
我很忙。
Wǒ hěn máng.
I am busy.
Example
他很好。
Tā hěn hǎo.
He is good.
Example
她很可愛。
Tā hěn kě'ài.
She is cute.
📝 Important Note:

Even though 很 is often translated as “very”, it does not always have a strong “very” meaning in basic adjective sentences.

我很忙。
Wǒ hěn máng.
I am busy.

The natural meaning is simply “I am busy.” It does not necessarily mean “I am VERY busy.”

✅ Natural

我很忙。

Wǒ hěn máng.

I am busy.

❌ Don't use 是

我是忙。

Wǒ shì máng.

Not the basic Mandarin pattern.

💡 Easy way to remember:

很 → “am / is / are” + HOW someone/something is

我很忙。😵‍💫
I am busy.
3

是 vs. 很

The easiest way to choose between them is to look at the word that comes after them.

👤 是 → Noun / Identity

是 + Noun

我是學生。

I am a student.

✨ 很 → Adjective

很 + Adjective

我很忙。

I am busy.

🧋 Think of it like this:

是 asks: “What / who are you?” 👤
很 tells us: “How are you?” ✨

Quick Guide

是 = am / is / are + noun

S + 是 + Noun

S + 很 + Adjective
