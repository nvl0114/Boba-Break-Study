About Boba Break Study 🧋

Sip, Study, and Sweeten Your Mandarin Journey

Boba Break Study is a Traditional Chinese learning website designed to help learners study Mandarin in a simple, structured, and enjoyable way.


This platform provides learning materials such as vocabulary, grammar, Traditional Chinese characters (Hanzi), daily conversations, and practice exercises to help users improve their Mandarin skills step by step.


The learning content in Boba Break Study is adapted from the Taiwan Center for Mandarin Learning (TCML) e-books and another learning resources. These materials are designed to support Mandarin learners with practical language knowledge, including reading, writing, listening, and everyday communication skills.


Boba Break Study was created to make learning Traditional Chinese more accessible, especially for learners who want to study Taiwan-style Mandarin independently. With a clean interface and interactive learning approach, users can learn at their own pace and track their progress.


Just like taking a relaxing break with a cup of boba, learning Mandarin can be enjoyable, consistent, and rewarding. 🧋

  • Expanding Access: Providing reliable Traditional Chinese learning resources that are often difficult for independent learners to find.

  • Trusted Standards: Adapting authentic, high-quality materials from official curricula (such as Taiwan Center for Mandarin Learning (TCML)) to ensure structured and accurate learning.

  • Stress-Free Learning: Creating a flexible, enjoyable learning experience as refreshing as taking a relaxing break with a cup of boba.

Boba Break Study started from my personal journey when I was preparing to move to Taiwan. As I began learning Mandarin, I quickly realized a major challenge: Traditional Chinese learning materials were much scarcer and harder to find compared to Simplified Chinese.


After moving to Taiwan and experiencing the learning process firsthand, I wanted to share what I had learned. My goal is to help other learners find reliable Traditional Chinese resources more easily, so they don't have to face the same difficulties I experienced.


Why "Boba Break Study"?


  • My Study Companion: Whenever I worked on Mandarin assignments, I often treated myself to a cup of Zhēnzhū Nǎichá (珍珠奶茶 / bubble tea) during my study breaks.

  • Flavor & Hanzi Memories: Before I knew it, many Chinese characters became connected with the sweet taste and memories of enjoying boba while studying.

  • A Symbol of Taiwan: Since boba is one of Taiwan's most iconic creations, it felt like the perfect symbol for this project. While many Simplified Chinese learning apps use a panda to represent China, I wanted Boba Break Study to celebrate Taiwan's culture through one of its most beloved drinks.
