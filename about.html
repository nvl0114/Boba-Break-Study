About Boba Break Study 🧋

Boba Break Study is a Traditional Chinese learning website designed to help learners study Mandarin in a simple, structured, and enjoyable way.


📖 What is Boba Break Study?

This platform provides learning materials such as vocabulary, grammar, Traditional Chinese characters (Hanzi), daily conversations, and practice exercises.


🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make Traditional Chinese learning more accessible, enjoyable, and organized for independent learners.


📜 Our Story

Boba Break Study was inspired by a personal journey of learning Traditional Chinese after moving to Taiwan. Finding Traditional Chinese resources was more challenging compared to Simplified Chinese materials.

The name "Boba Break" comes from the habit of enjoying bubble tea during study breaks while learning Mandarin. Like a cup of boba, learning can be sweet, relaxing, and enjoyable.
